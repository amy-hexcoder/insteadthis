Weekend calls for a small get together with family and friends and what’s better than watching an amazing movie sequel with your dear ones. There are so many sequels doing so good on the big screen, in fact better than the originals. Let’s jump into some of them real quick and pick one for this weekend.

**THE GOOD, THE BAD AND THE UGLY**

This movie sequel actually got an 8.8/10 so one can figure out how the movie would be but we won’t leave you without stating forward a review by a parent herself [@erica1](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/movie-reviews/the-good-the-bad-and-the-ugly/user-reviews/adult) has posted a review on commonsensemedia.org that says :

Our boys watched the trilogy when they were 8 and 10. They loved all three. I couldn’t believe they sat through them as so long!! It is tame but gripping. To put this in context, our ten yr old found Ghost Busters terrifying after first five minutes so couldn’t watch it. But he loved all 9 hours of this trilogy. They are 16 and 14 now and still say these are some of their favourite films. Really not violent at all by today's standards.

**BRIDE OF FRANKENSTEIN**

Bride of Frankenstein perfects the campiness that the original film, Frankenstein, desperately needed. This Frankenstein is not scary—just a mild-mannered monster in need of a friend. He gets one in his mate, who sports one of the most iconic hairdos in Hollywood’s history. Still considered a high watermark in the monster movie franchise, the movie slyly snuck a queer subplot, feminist plot points and meditations on necrophilia past the ratings board.

**GLASS**

Glass is said to be an amazing movie with a great story line as one expects it to be but hey hey we aren’t going to spoil it for you but we and this reddit user can definitely give you guys quick thoughts about the same.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/glass-717x1024.jpg)
*Courtesy: imdb.com*

“I really loved the movie. Story-wise, it really felt like a comic-book/superhero tale in the most grounded & smallest scale possible. This movie is also extremely character-driven just like Unbreakable and Split are; don't expect a summer-blockbuster going in. I was ecstatic to hear some of the Unbreakable score in some moments of the movie; Thordson's score is good, but it's no where near the level of Howard's in my opinion.

For those who have read reviews of Glass, I'm sure that many of the reviewers have mentioned how they felt about the ending. Again, no spoilers, but I do think it will be pretty divisive. That being said, I personally loved where it went”…. writes a user on [Reddit.](https://www.reddit.com/r/movies/comments/afen50/saw_glass_today_wanted_to_post_quick_thoughts_no/)
