Finding your dream man or woman is not an easy task. And at times, it feels like the dating pool gets filled with too many frogs and not enough princes. Yes, I agree that in the age of online dating finding- your [love](https://www.goodtherapy.org/blog/psychpedia/love) is accessible but at the same time difficult. It can be as simple as swiping right if that is more your speed. But online profiles do not always tell the full story, and meeting up with someone you get connected with online can lead to discoveries. Getting to know the real person behind the profile is a crucial step before proceeding to any conclusion. Any good relationship is structured; on many different qualities. While other factors such as good looks and sexual chemistry are a few early indicators of compatibility, but there are a few more must-have characteristics women should look in the man before getting 'hitched' for life.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/07/1-8.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

- **Confidence is the key:**

So, when a man believes in himself, he knows who he is and knows what he wants. It is very appealing to a woman and is usually something that makes her fall in love with. She considers this as an important factor for being [compatible](/stories/compatibility-testing-a-much-needed-criterion-these-days). Being confident means being comfortable with who you are. Women respond to the upbeat energy of a [confident man](https://www.lindseyhood.net/blog/2019/2/2/what-is-confidence-and-why-is-it-important). She likes a confident man but not an egoistic; person. When a man does not feel the need to compete with others to lift himself, it almost wins a woman's attention.

- **Trustworthiness plays an important role:**

When a man is trustworthy, he becomes more appealing and desirable to a woman. If he is truthful, honest, and genuine, he is a guy who is worth pursuing. It is through this that she comes to know if the relationship will last long-term or not.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/07/2-4-1024x683.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

- **Integrity attracts all:**

When it comes to having integrity, you are acting virtuously and have got a strong moral character. Women always desire a man who is ethical and fair. In terms of relationships, having integrity can deepen your bond with your partner. Also, brownie points when this integrity extends beyond the relationship, and a man can treat others with kindness.

- **Being emotionally available:**

Women desire a man who is emotionally supportive and honest. She would never like a man who is cut off and [emotionally](https://www.etmoney.com/blog/let-your-emotions-stay-at-bay-while-investing/) unavailable. While fully and openly expressing feelings may be a little awkward at first, it is also important to show a vulnerable, human side of yourself to create a strong foundation for an ever-lasting relationship. Women also prefer a man who is not afraid to show their true feelings and discuss them openly, instead of a man who keeps everything decked up inside with him. At times, this means talking about previous relationships and confessing your past.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/07/3-6-1024x576.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

- **Respecting each other:**

So, to have a successful relationship, partners have to treat each other with respect. If a woman feels that she is taken for granted or treated poorly by her man, she will drop him to avoid heartbreak. When a person is made to feel disrespected, the relationship ends sooner. Hence, a woman likes a man who would show her care and give her the respect she deserves.

- **[Maturity](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/blogs/O-zone/what-does-it-mean-to-be-mature/) is a must-have:**

It is important to show a woman that you are not only emotionally involved but open to communicate. Showing a woman that you are mentally mature and capable of behaving like a grown-up when it is time to do so will attract her. You can do this by showing that you are thinking rationally and not overreacting when dealing with a situation.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/07/4.jpeg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

- **Good sense of humor:**

You do not need to be a stand-up comedian to capture a woman's attention. You should have the ability to joke around and have a sense of humor that is appealing to a woman. So, when two people are laughing at the same thing, they agree to the same perspective, and it is amusing. A relationship that has a good sense of humor and romance is likely to sustain for a lifetime. A woman likes a man who can make her laugh, after all, she wants to be happy. She wants a light-hearted person with whom she can be witty at times.

- **[Equal relationship](https://heavymetalgroup.com.au/resources/blog/the-importance-of-an-equal-relationship/) is necessary:**

If you are quiet in a relationship, it might be because your partner has never treated you equally. The cultural discrepancy between inequality that exists around for years is changing slowly. Now women love men who will treat them equally in everything.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/07/5-4.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

- **Curiosity about her:**

It is always important that you feel like your partner is interested in you. Instead of judging a person, try to be curious about her likes and dislikes. A woman loves a curious man; to know about her and what type of person she is.

- **Stability in everything:**

This one is big. A woman looks for emotional stability, economic stability, and relational stability. She likes men who will give her 'stability in everything. These days, women are looking for partners who are stable in everything.
