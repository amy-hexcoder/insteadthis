## 4 types of hugs men give, with each revealing something about them:

**1. Friendly Hug**

A friendly hug may last longer than a "hello" hug between friends, but it goes like this: Your arms go below his shoulders and his go around yours. If you stay long enough, his hands may wander, but they shouldn't go beyond your mid-back or upper arms.

Many cultures use quick pecks on the cheek as a way to express affection between friends or family members. However, in a romantic sense, you can examine his cheek kiss in light of previous hugs or his kisses to other girls.

When one of you is comforting the other, a longer hug rarely means anything more than friendship. Sometimes it has a deeper meaning.

**2. Heart-to-Heart Hug**

The friendly hug may be hiding something deeper if it gets even more intimate. Heart-to-heart hugs occur when chests touch while shoulders are hugged.

A heart-to-heart hug allows both people's hearts to touch as they embrace with their left sides. Many romantic couples use this hug as a form of intimacy. If he does this kind of hug with you, there may be more to his embrace.

A heart-to-heart hug that lasts at least 20 seconds releases the love hormone oxytocin, responsible for bonding. Keep your eyes on his hands as well during the hug. Hands wandering longer and farther than usual are probably not your imagination.

**3. Long Hug**

Whether you're in a relationship or not with this guy you like, if you're making lower body contact, you've already been intimate or you'll be in the near future. Long hugs last longer than a few seconds; they can last up to a minute and leave you feeling like there is lingering chemistry.

A long hug is common among friends, but in a romantic context, it occurs when one or both participants cling tightly to one another and even linger their hands on the other's neck.

Even though men don't tend to show emotional vulnerability, especially verbally, a long hug from someone you like may be his way of letting you know he wants to be physically close to you. In public, longer and tighter hugs demonstrate commitment, and they indicate intense feelings.

**4. Hug From Behind**

In a hug from behind, a person stands behind another, touching the person's frontside with their backside. After that, the person behind the other individual hugs them on the chest or stomach.

It's obvious that a guy is romantically interested in you when he hugs you from behind. Many things can be conveyed by a hug from behind, including comfort, devotion, and trust. As such, this hug is a very intimate way of communicating all these feelings.

You can feel his affection when he hugs you from behind by the way he wraps his arms around you. To get even closer to you, they may rock you from side to side or press their faces against your neck or back.

So, there you have it—the secret language of hugs decoded! Your relationship GPS lies in understanding these cuddly cues, whether they're a lackluster embrace or an epic hug. Get your detective hat on and cuddle up! You're just a hug away from discovering your relationship status.
