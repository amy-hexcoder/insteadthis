If your kids are off for the summer and you're free too, don't waste any time and get packing to escape the oppressive May heat in India and discover the top destinations for an unforgettable trip. India offers a plethora of locations with verdant foliage, breathtaking vistas, sun-kissed peaks, cascading rivers, and refreshing breezes. Thus, if you're looking for the greatest locations to travel to in India in May, have a look at our selection of amazing spots that provide the ideal starting point for a summer holiday.

1. **Mcleodganj, a well-known tourist attraction:** It is among the greatest locations in India to travel to in May so that you can spend time with friends and family. Situated in the foothills of the Himalayas, Mcleodganj presents mesmerizing vistas of surreal landscapes and a pristine ambiance that evokes a sense of paradise. March through June is the ideal time to come because it is convenient for trekking and sightseeing.

   [Bhagsu Falls](https://www.tripadvisor.in/Attraction_Review-g1092107-d1598717-Reviews-Bhagsu_Waterfall-McLeod_Ganj_Dharamsala_Kangra_District_Himachal_Pradesh.html), Namgyal Monastery, Tsuglagkhang Complex, Bhagsunag Temple, Tibetan Museum, Triund, Dal Lake, and many more attractions can be found in Mcleodoganj.

   Activities include hiking, boating, camping, trekking, sightseeing, and shopping.

   **How to get there:**

   **By Air:** Gaggal Airport is the closest airport.

   **By Train:** Pathankot Junction is the closest train station.

   **By Road:** Mcleodganj Terminal is the closest bus stop.

2. **The Queen of the Hills, Manali!** One of the greatest destinations in India to escape the intense heat is Manali. You can take advantage of the lovely weather, vibrant flowers, and stunning landscape in May. There are various retail sections at this hill station where you may purchase a lot of souvenirs.

Manali attractions include the Manu Temple, Hidimba Devi Temple, Manali Sanctuary, and more.

Activities include shopping, paragliding, river rafting, camping, and sightseeing.

**How to get there:**
**By Air:** Bhuntar is the closest airport.

**By Train:** Jogindernagar is the closest railway station.

**By Road:** Manali is easily accessible by road from cities such as Delhi, Dehradun, Chandigarh, and Ambala. To go to this charming hill station from other parts of the state, take a private bus.

3. **Popular for its Landscape: Shillong** Because of its breathtaking natural features, which include verdant gardens, thick forests, hiking paths, hills, and waterfalls, Shillong is sometimes referred to as the Scotland of the East. The greatest time to visit Shillong and take in the area's natural beauty is in May.

Shillong's attractions include Lady Hydari Park, the Air Force Museum, [Ward's Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward%27s_Lake_(Shillong)), Elephant Falls, Umiam Lake, Sweet Falls, and David Scott Trail.

Activities include rock climbing, rappelling, zip-lining, trekking, and exploring Shillong's other well-known locations, like Elephant Falls.

How to get there:

By Air: Shillong Airport is the closest airport.

By Train: Guwahati is the closest railway station.

**By Road:** Travel to Shillong from Guwahati by bus. Numerous buses, both private and public

**4. Lansdowne: the homeland of liberation warriors:** Searching for a tranquil vacation spot? In May, the most popular destination in India is Landsdowne. Experience complete tranquility in stunning surroundings—this is the ideal weekend escape from Delhi. Arrange a journey that will never be forgotten to Lansdowne and discover tranquil surroundings.
[The Tarkeshwar Mahadev Temple](https://www.tripadvisor.in/Attraction_Review-g1208973-d2693642-Reviews-Tarkeshwar_Mahadev_Temple-Lansdowne_Pauri_Garhwal_District_Uttarakhand.html), Bhulla Lake, the Darwan Singh Museum, and other locations are among Lansdowne's attractions.

Activities include hiking, kayaking, camping, bird watching, and boating.

**How to get there:**

Dehradun is the closest airport via air.

**By Train:** Kotdwar is the closest railway station.

**By Road:** Lansdowne is reachable by several public and private buses. You will undoubtedly appreciate the delightful bus trip to Lansdowne

![](/images/uploads/2024/03/v2.webp)

5. **Kerela: Renowned for its stunning backwaters**: Since Kerela is one of the greatest tourist attractions for summer holidays and one of the top places to visit in India in May, its beauty cannot be compared to any other tourist spot. The ideal location for a summer holiday is Kerela, thanks to its stunning combination of beaches, backwaters, mountains, and wildlife, enhanced by the presence of palm trees that add to the area's allure.
Thrissur, Munroe Island, Alleppey, Kannur, Bekarl, Kizhunna Beach, Bekal Fort, and many more are some of the attractions in Kerela.
Activities include touring tea estates, taking a houseboat trip, experiencing the village life, dining at a floating restaurant, and witnessing a Kathakali dance performance and Neelakurinji Bloom.

**How to get there:**

**By Air:** Coaching Airport is the closest airport.

**Via Train:** closest train station

6. **Kashmir: A utopia on earth**: Visit Kashmir in May to get the most out of your exploration of the valley if you want to feel like you are in heaven on earth. When the weather is nice in May, this is the best spot to visit for breathtaking scenery, incredible people, and culture. Prepare your belongings for the fantastic itinerary to Kashmir and enjoy yourself!

Kashmir's attractions include Jama Masjid, Chashmashahi, Yousmarg, Pulwama, Leh, Gulmarg, Sonamarg, and Shalimar Garden.

**Activities:** Shopping, a pony ride, a houseboat stay, a cable car ride, a Shikara ride, and more.

**How to get there:**

**Via Air:** The closest airport is Srinagar

**By Train:** Jammu Tawi is the closest train station.

**By Road:** Both state and private buses connect Kashmir well. NH-1A without difficulty

7. **Rajasthan's magnificent hill station, Mount Abu:** It is Rajasthan's only and most picturesque hill station, offering a pleasant ambiance and verdant environs that make it a popular tourist destination. The main draw on Mount Abu is the Dilwara temple, which should not be missed at all. You will be enthralled by the shrine's breathtaking architecture.

Mount Abu's attractions include the Wildlife Sanctuary, Dilwara Temple, Guru Shikhar, Jain Temples, Nakki Lake, and Arbuda Devi Temple.

Activities include hiking, camping, rock climbing, boating, and sightseeing.

**How to get there:**

**Via Air:** The closest airport is in Udaipur

**By Train:** Abu Road is the closest train station.

**By Road:** There are frequent private and public buses that go to this city.
