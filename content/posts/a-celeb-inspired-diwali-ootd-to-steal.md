We are just a few days away from Diwali, so you might be worried about your [OOTD](https://in.pinterest.com/pin/695172892480061757/) (Outfit Of The Day). Though Diwali celebrations this year are quite different, why not make the most of the occasion by dressing to the nines? It's no secret that the festival of lights involves more than just cleaning and decorating; it's also about dressing up! Whatever you're doing this Diwali, whether you're hosting a dinner, going to a card party, or attending a puja.
Don't miss your chance to dress up in festive wear. Get inspired by these sartorial [suggestions](https://shaadiwish.com/blog/2021/10/19/diwali-outfit-ideas/). You can take notes from Kajal Aggarwal and Kriti Sanon.

## Dress and Dupatta Look!

- ![Sonam Kapoor Slays In A Red Dress Attending The Zoya Factor's Trailer -  StarBiz.com](https://img.starbiz.com/resize/750x-/2019/08/30/sonam-kapoor-slays-in-a-strapless-red-dress-9912.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

There are indeed (more than) a couple of dresses in your wardrobe. Isn't it? Take a look at your closet and find the best party gowns and dresses among flares and voluminous pieces. Next? You can experiment with embellished versions in varying color schemes or go monochrome bright like Sonam Kapoor. Don't forget your tall heels!

## Preppy Co-ords

- ![Meet the BEST DRESSED celebs this week - Rediff.com Get Ahead](https://im.rediff.com/getahead/2020/oct/29fashion5.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

Diya Mirza is looking sensational in this angular hemline on her maxi skirt and bell-sleeves crop top. This outfit is perfect for any Indian festival. This bohemian look goes great with black heels, a cinch choker, and silver earrings.

## Layers of lace!

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/Capturez.png)
  *Credits@Google*

Lace is always a striking feature of Indian wear. A festive touch is added to any look when pieces blend with lace and embroidered dupattas. Using the scalloped edge of the dupatta as a neckline detail, Athiya Shetty opted for a pair of earrings.

## Phool-Jhadi!

- ![PIX: Kriti Sanon parties with Kajol, Rekha - Rediff.com movies](http://im.rediff.com/movies/2019/sep/03ambani40.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

Keep your Diwali ensemble fun and youthful by wearing flower power. Anarkali kurtas in this print make a style statement for Diwali parties, and Aditi Rao Hydari appears to have taken this print to another level with her floor-length kurta.

## Blend Of Neo-Bohemian and Phulkari!

- ![](https://cdn0.weddingwire.in/articles/images/1/4/4/7/img_87441/diwali-styles-inspired-by-bollywood-celebrities-sukriti-akriti-blend-of-neo-bohemian-and-phulkari1.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

Is there anything better than kicking off the holiday season with style and zest? Choosing an ideal outfit for traditional gatherings is like searching for the one true love. Immerse yourself in the atmosphere of neo-bohemian and phulkari dresses. Kriti Sanon shines to the nines in a luxurious Neo Phulkari lehenga set that exudes festive spirit!

## Chanderi Saree Wins It All!

- ![](https://cdn0.weddingwire.in/articles/images/5/4/4/7/img_87445/diwali-styles-inspired-by-bollywood-celebrities-mishru-official-chanderi-saree-for-the-win.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

The stunning Chanderi scalloped saree worn by Tamannaah Bhatia is absolutely to die for. In addition to the elegant saree, the blouse has breathtaking chintz embroidery. We love everything about this look, from its soothing color to its delicate embroidery.

## Let Sharara Do The Talking!

- ![Arpita Mehta](https://cdn0.weddingwire.in/articles/images/1/5/4/7/img_87451/diwali-styles-inspired-by-bollywood-celebrities-arpita-mehta-official-let-gharara-do-the-talking.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

If you're seeking a subtle yet elegant outfit that keeps the festive vibes in a sober tone, look no further! This hand embroidery sharara and blurred polka print kurta have Kajal Aggarwal looking ravishing! Our hearts are racing over this beautiful outfit, sure to add some glitz and glamour to the festival!

## Fusion of Tradition and Modernity!

- ![](https://cdn0.weddingwire.in/articles/images/5/3/4/7/img_87435/diwali-styles-inspired-by-bollywood-celebrities-ridhima-mehra-marry-the-traditional-with-contemporary.jpg)

Florals in vibrant prints to flowing silhouettes and ruffles that fall to the ground always capture the heart of finesse. There's no denying we are smitten with Shilpa Shetty, especially when she wears traditional attire. For a colorful Diwali bash, this stunning sleeveless Gharara set comes with a matching belt. Ideal for a revelry day.

## A red extravaganza!

- ![](https://www.pinkvilla.com/files/an_over-the-top_look_-_karina_kaif_.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

A regal red lehenga by Katrina Kaif will do the trick if simple isn't enough. This outfit is not jazzy or too loud in any sense, but the bold red color combined with the thick golden border will make a strong statement if you are going to a Diwali party. As Katrina did, choose lips that complement your lehenga and blush pink for your cheeks to achieve the perfect look.
