## SAY HEY BAE MIDI BANDAGE [DRESS](/stories/an-aurora-inspired-outfit-reviews) WITH CUT OUT

[Say Hey Bae Midi Bandage Dress With Cut Out](https://www.shopakira.com/dresses/say-hey-bae-midi-bandage-dress-with-cut-out) by AKIRA Label is crafted from slimming bandage material. It features a halter neckline with a cross-over front and a torso cutout that gives it an edgy look. With a back zipper closure, it is midi in length.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 70% Rayon, 30% Nylon
- Care: Hand wash with cold water
- Shoulder-to-Hem: 46"
- Size: Small
