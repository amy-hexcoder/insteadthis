For an unforgettable clutch experience, unbuckle the strap and hold it by the bag's collar.

**Features:**

Material: 100% man-made

Dimensions: 8.5" x 3" x 6" approx.

Imported

**My thoughts are:**

There is ample space to store a phone, cosmetics like lipstick, liner, etc. With the detachable chain, it makes a stylish sling bag as well. The chains are durable and of good quality. Wear this statement piece with all black and let it stand alone.
