# Ground Breaking Fall Trench

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/11/ground-breaking-fall-trench_beige_1_1-678x1024.jpg)
  *Credit@shopakira.com*

It goes without saying that if there is a classic staple for a winter wardrobe, it has to be a Trench Coast! And this classy [Trench Coat](https://www.shopakira.com/jackets-coats/ground-breaking-fall-trench#) from Akira is sure to make you fall in love with it. It comes in this beautiful beige color which makes it even more irresistible. Beige is one of those neutral colors that every girl should have in their wardrobes! You can pair it up with so many colors while still maintaining that classy look. And the material of this coat is super smooth with very fine knits. The best part about the coat is a double-breasted rain jacket!

**Features:**

- Material: 100% Polyester
- Care: Dry Clean only
- Sleeves Length: 24''
- Shoulder to Hem: 42''

To elevate your look, style this coat with a pair of classy black boots! Have a look at these thigh-high boots from Akira.
