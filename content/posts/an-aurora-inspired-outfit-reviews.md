## THE RIGHT ONE RHINESTONE BUTTON CARDI- An Aurora-inspired Outfit- Review

Your search is over! [AKIRA Label's The Right One Rhinestone Button Cardi](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/the-right-one-rhinestone-button-cardi) is a big hit. The knit fabric of this long-sleeved sweater is soft and cozy. It features a plunging V-neckline with a cropped style. The front panel features ornate rhinestone buttons, and the trim is ribbed.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 50% Acrylic 30% Polyamide 20% Polyester
- Care: Hand wash in cold water
- Length: 17" Shoulder to Hem
- Sleeves: 23.5" approx. (Size small)

## ALL ABOUT SMILES HIGH RISE STRAIGHT JEANS- Review

Nothing but good vibes! The [All About Smiles High Rise Straight Jean by AKIRA Label](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/all-about-smiles-high-rise-straight-jeans) comes from structured denim. Straight-leg pants with a high-rise waist and classic zip button closure. These pants sport a 5-pocket design, painted smiley faces along the legs, and working belt loops.

**Features:**

- Material: 99% Cotton, 1% Spandex
- Fabric: Slightly stretchy
- Care: Machine wash cold
- Size: 43.5" Waist to Hem
- Inseam: 33"
- Rise: 11" (based on size 3)
