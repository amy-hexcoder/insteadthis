ANSWER

Hi Josie!

Thanks for stopping by. Halloween is a time when everyone can be someone else, and having the right makeup for Halloween can make all the difference. You will look meticulously planned with these makeup tricks, yet they are easy and fast to apply. Using these simple ideas, you can make your face the center of attention in any creative costume. Take a [look at these last-minute makeup ideas](https://www.pinterest.com/akureshi1519/last-minute-easy-halloween-makeup/) for Halloween.

## Cotton Candy Clown

This creepy, cotton candy clown works if you're looking for something a little spooky.

You can achieve this [look](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCzbrXSSWChwaDBOC-06s-lQ) by layering tissue strips and liquid latex at the corners of your mouth to mimic a creepy smile. Drizzle fake blood over the fake mouth to complete the look. Draw triangles above and below the lash line with matte pink eyeshadow and matte blue eyeshadow. Add rhinestones for a dramatic effect.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/sddefault.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

**DIY Fake Blood:**

You can use beet or pomegranate juice as well as food dyes. Jello, tomato paste, strawberry syrup, or fruit punch will make the blood thick. In case you don't feel like DIYing, buy red Kool-Aid instead.

## Stitched-Up Mouth

Get a spooky [look](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q2g5GeVg-MU) with this sewed-mouth look featuring some brutal details.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/Capturemakup-1024x524.png)
  *Credits@Google*

Put liquid latex around your mouth and then apply foundation over it, then finish with powder. Apply red shadow to the latex to give it the appearance of skin. After blending, apply fake blood to the lips and the latex, coating them with the gruesome product. Lastly, threads yarn through the holes in the latex attached to the skin to make it appear sewn up. Add blood for extra gore.

## Bug Queen

To spread terror everywhere you go. A bunch of scary 3D bugs will do the trick. That's it! You can achieve this look by using eyeliner or stickers to draw the bugs. To create a bug bite, you can use eye shadow.

## Half Clown

There is something creepy about clowns. Don't you think so? Check out this half-clown makeup; it's a bit scary. One half is real, while the other half seems clownish. Don't forget to add creases on the skin. It will enhance the realistic look. In such a makeup look, you're sure to be the talk of the party!
