## INKED SLEEVELESS SEQUIN APPLIQUE CROP TOP

The [inked Sleeveless Sequin Applique Crop Top](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/inked-sleeveless-sequin-applique-crop-top) from AKIRA Label is made from sheer mesh fabric and features tattoo-inspired sequin applique front detailing, lace-up sides, a sleeveless silhouette, and cropped length.

**Features:**

- Shell 1: 100% Polyester
- Shell 2: 85% Polyester
- Spandex: 15%
- Care: Hand wash cold
- Shoulder to Hem: 14.5” (approx.)
- Size: S/M

What a beautiful color. It's easy to slip into this lively sequin top on days when you need a little sparkle to brighten up your day. This top features a relaxed fit for comfort.
