Hi Stephie!

Yes, I am having a super happy week. How about yours? Well, I read your question, and I always thought of bringing this onto the table but couldn't. Well, health drinks are a great way to get minerals and vitamins into your diet. Many health drinks are available in the market right from juices to smoothies. They can be a great way to get some nutrients when you are on the go. But always be careful when you have to choose the right ones. Because the sugar present in them can be an unhealthy option if consumed in a large quantity. So, always make sure to look for health drinks low in [sugar](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/14-ways-to-eat-less-sugar).

![](/images/uploads/2022/05/d3-1024x512.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

No matter which health drink you choose, just look for the ingredients. Ideally, they should be fruits or forms of proteins like milk. Try to avoid drinks having sugar as the first or second ingredient in the drink. [Calories](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/35-ways-to-cut-calories) are equally important too. If these health drinks are to be replaced with something, then look for a label that has 400 calories per serving. Try to avoid drinks as snacks, but if you must, then never opt for something that provides more than 200 calories.
