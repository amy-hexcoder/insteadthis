## BABY GIRL ROMPER WITH FRONT TIE

With its ultra-soft stretchy fabric, the AKIRA Label [Baby Girl Romper With Front Tie](https://www.shopakira.com/clothing/rompers-jumpsuits/baby-girl-romper-with-front-tie) is very comfortable to wear. With an open torso and a tie-front detail, adjustable straps, and mid-length shorts, it is sure to become your new go-to piece.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 90% Cotton 10% Spandex
- Care: Hand wash cold
- Measurement: 31" shoulder to hem
- Size: Small

**My thoughts:**

It is so cool! The fit is perfect, and it feels very cozy and stylish. If you wish, you can even adjust those straps if you want them a bit lose or tight. The tie on the front gives the outfit a [striking look](/stories/a-valentines-date-dress-review).
