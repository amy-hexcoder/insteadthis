## BELT

Putting on a belt is like getting your confidence back. [Check up on it Belt](https://www.shopakira.com/accessories/belts-body-jewelry/belt-125470) from AKIRA LABEL is designed to show off your curves in any fit. A gold chain detail and two faux leather bands adorn the front of the waistband, and the rear waistband is elastic.

**Features:**

- Material: 100% man-made
- Care: Hand wash cold
- Dimensions: 34.5"
- (roughly based on size O/S)

**A few thoughts:**
Worth it! The best belt you can own is a black belt with a gold touch. The black belt is always my top choice. This piece has just the proper width, too. Wear it with sexy mini and stilettos for an edgy look. Almost [anything looks gorgeous](/stories/how-to-dress-for-an-unforgettable-birthday-party) with it.

## 9 RINGS CHAIN BELT

With a faux gold base, nine rhinestone ring details, and an adjustable clasp, this [chain belt](https://www.shopakira.com/accessories/9-rings-chain-belt) from the AKIRA Label has a luxurious feel.

**Features:**

- Materials: Manmade
- Length: 42"
- Size: O/S (Note: Dimensions are approximate)

**My thoughts:**
This belt is stylish, versatile, and aesthetically pleasing. It does not fall off and combines well. Not one bit uncomfortable or irritating. Dress this belt elegantly with a [satin shift dress](/stories/take-your-style-to-the-next-level-with-the-perfect-white-holi-outfit) and hoop earrings for a stunning look.

## WISHFUL SILVER 5 LAYER CHAIN BELT

This [Wishful Silver 5 Layer Chain Belt](https://www.shopakira.com/accessories/wishful-silver-5-layer-chain-belt) from AKIRA Label comprises a faux silver curb chain base, five layers of chains on the front, and a lobster claw clasp.

**Features:**

- Materials: 100% man-made
- Length: 44.5"
- Size: O/S

**Thoughts:**
What a cutie! I adore those layers! Size is not a concern as it can be easily adjusted to fit any waist size by clamping it with lobster claws. The little black dress or blazer is one of the ways to pull it off. But it will go great with skirts, too!
