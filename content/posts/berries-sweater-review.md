## BERRIES BUTTON CARDIGAN

The [AKIRA Label Berries Button Down Cardigan](https://www.shopakira.com/clothing/berries-button-cardigan) features a cropped silhouette and knit construction. There is a soft knit base throughout, as well as three-dimensional berry knit detailing. A ribbed knit trim surrounds the ribbed collar, as well as the long sleeves and button-up front closure.

**Features:**

- Material: 100% polyester
- Care: Hand wash cold
- Shoulder to hem: 21"
- Sleeve length: 23"
- (approx. measured from size S)
- Size: Small

**Comments:**

There is no doubt that the AKIRA Label Berries Button Down Cardigan is one of their best-selling knit sweaters. You can wear this piece with a cropped tank, straight-leg pants, and combat boots to make it look more casual while giving it an elegant touch.

## DAISY DREAMING HIGH LOW SWEATER

With its cozy ribbed knit fabrication, the [AKIRA Label Daisy Dreaming High Low Sweater](https://www.shopakira.com/clothing/daisy-dreaming-high-low-sweater) will keep you warm and cozy in the winter. There is a fold-over turtleneck collar, long sleeves, a high-low silhouette, and a curved, vented hemline on this sweater. At the back, the garment is embellished with an oversized daisy design.

**Features:**

- Material: 60% Cotton 40% Acrylic
- Care: Hand wash cold
- Shoulder to longest hem: 33"
- Shoulder to shortest hem: 23"
- Side Slit: 12"
- Sleeves: 28"
- (Approximately measured from size small)
- Size: Small

We've got cooler temperatures, but we're still in good spirits! Despite the fact that it is very comfortable, the color is very striking. A classic yet elevated look can be achieved by wearing this piece with straight-leg denim and booties.
