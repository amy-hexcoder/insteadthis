QUESTION

Hi Amanda,

Friendship Day is just around the corner, and I'm going to Berlin to meet my childhood friend. Would you mind sharing your BFF bucket list ideas with me? As I'm hoping to make it the most this year, let me know.

Love,

Alice!

ANSWER

Hi Alice,

Friendship Day is a special day to cherish the bond between you and your best friend. It's a great time for making memories and strengthening friendships. For your BFF Bucket List on Friendship Day, here is a list of exciting and fun activities you can do with your BFF:

1. Have a Movie Marathon: Watch your favorite movies or binge-watch your favorite series. Prepare some popcorn, snacks, and drinks to make it a cozy and enjoyable experience.

2. Take a Road Trip: Get your best friend together and hit the road. Make lasting memories as you explore new places, enjoy scenic views, and create new memories together. Don't forget to capture the moments with lots of pictures.

3. Have a Picnic in the Park: Find a beautiful spot in the park and pack a picnic basket with your favorite snacks. Play games, have heartfelt conversations, and enjoy the outdoors.

![](https://c.ndtvimg.com/2022-03/bbjhjaf_happiness-friends-generic-unsplash_625x300_20_March_22.jpg)

4. Try a New Hobby Together: Find something you both enjoy or try something new that you've always wanted to do. You could paint, cook, dance, or even learn a musical instrument. Embrace the learning process and have fun along the way.

5. Volunteer for a Cause: Together, give back to the community. Spend the day volunteering at a local charity or organization that resonates with you both. Not only will the act of kindness make a difference in someone else's life, but it will also strengthen your relationship.

6. Have a DIY Spa Day: Get cozy at home with candles, soothing music, and homemade face masks. You can pamper yourself with spa treatments, manicures, or pedicures. Enjoy some self-care with your best friend while unwinding.

7. Go Camping: Get away from the hustle and bustle of everyday life and enjoy nature. Make a campsite, roast marshmallows, share stories around the campfire, and sleep under the stars. It's a perfect opportunity to bond and reconnect with your BFF.

![](https://f.hubspotusercontent40.net/hubfs/9253440/Selfie-Of-Friends-Traveling-In-Off-Road-Car.jpg)

8. Take a Cooking Class: Learn how to make a new dish or cuisine together. Try new flavors, laugh at kitchen mishaps, and savor the delicious results. Perhaps you will discover a hidden talent for culinary arts.

9. Create a Scrapbook: Make a scrapbook of the memorable moments you've shared. Put photographs, notes, and mementos that symbolize your friendship in it. For years to come, it will be a treasured keepsake.

10. Write Letters to Each Other: Handwritten letters still hold a special place in this digital age. Express your love and gratitude to each other by writing heartfelt letters. It's a wonderful way to strengthen your bond and create lasting memories.

So, these are just a few ideas to get you started on Friendship Day bucket lists. You should always spend quality time with your best friend, cherishing each other's presence and creating beautiful memories together. True friendships are precious and should be celebrated.

Love,

Amanda!
