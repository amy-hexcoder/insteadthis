Hi Missy and Nanno!

Yes, I had an amazing New year. I hope your new year was good? Many people, especially men find it difficult: to take care of their skin especially, in winters. But, we should look after our skin no matter what.

So, here is a skincare routine, men should follow:

- **CLEANSE:**

Always make sure that you cleanse your skin with almond oil. Take a few drops of oil and massage on your neck and face. Tissue off and then wash your skin with lukewarm water and a mild cleanser.

- **TONE:**

Mix an equal proportion of water and apple cider vinegar for toning your skin. Then, use a cotton pad to apply it to your face. Post this, just wash this after two minutes. It will make your skin shiny.

- **MOISTURIZE:**

You can use any mild oil-free [moisturizer](https://www.burkewilliams.com/blog/2014/10/23/5-reasons-must-moisturize-skin-2) to moisturize your skin.

- **NIGHT CARE:**

Use extra virgin olive oil for nighttime healing. Then see how it works!

**Note:** Always use sunscreen lotion wherever you are going.
