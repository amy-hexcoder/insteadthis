**WRAP ME UP OVERSIZED BOW TOP**

The AKIRA Label [Wrap Me Up Oversized Bow Top](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/wrap-me-up-oversized-bow-top) boasts a structured denim fabrication in a lightweight design. It features a strapless design, a cape-like construction, and an oversized bow. A cropped silhouette and a pull-on fit give this top a perfectly fitted look, while internal elastic arm bands help keep it in place.

**Features:**

- Material: 100% polyester
- Spot cleaning only
- Chest to hem: 8"
- Shoulder to hem: 20"
- (approximately based on size S/M)
- Model: S/M

This top is a perfect example of the unique style you possess. The intricate details of the top, from the fabric to the fit, make it a standout piece in any wardrobe. If you want to round out this look, pair this dramatic piece with a fitted pair of trousers and pumps.

**KYLE BOW DETAIL BRALETTE**

A sexy, chic bralette with bow detail, the [Kyle Bow Detail Bralette](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/kyle-bow-detail-bralette) is made from a smooth base fabrication. This cute top has ruched elastic tank straps, a big ribbon bow tie at the front, and an inner lining at the cups. An elastic-shirred back and a metallic silver zipper closure complete the look.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 91% Polyester 9% Spandex
- Shoulder to hem: 13"
- (Measurement is based on a size Small)
- Model: Small
