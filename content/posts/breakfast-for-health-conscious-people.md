Health and its importance are known to all but still, a lot of people tend to make mistakes with what they choose to eat, one such mistake is the first meal of the day i.e. the morning breakfast. Some of us constantly binge eat things and get used to them with time. It is said that if your day starts with the right nutrients necessary for your body then it'll help you with enough energy throughout the day.

People who are health conscious or check the calorie intake and everything of a specific food item are more worried about what goes inside their body and if it helps their body to grow healthily.

**Why and when are people called health conscious?**

Health-conscious people are the ones who are always concerned about themselves and the people they love as to what one eats and in what proportion is it required and they firmly believe in relying on the things that benefit one's body in the long run not just for a short period.

Moreover, they experiment with tons of healthy fruits and veggies recipes to stay fit and keep themselves healthy for ages. Health-conscious look for the best of options they can get first thing in the morning as it can help them with to fuel up for the whole day.

Such people go for things that are less oily and rawer as it helps their body to absorb the nutrients in a better way and it thus provides all the minerals and vitamins required by the body.

**Does breakfast play a role in staying fit and living a healthy life?**

The simple answer to this is YES it does play a vital role in shaping and molding one's life in a healthy way as it is said that breakfast provides folate, calcium, iron, B vitamins, and fiber which is essential for the body. Breakfast also improves your energy levels and ability to concentrate in the short term and can help with better weight management, reduced risk of type 2 diabetes and heart disease in the long term. What more reasons one would need to NEVER skip their breakfast have a healthy one.

**HEALTHY BREAKFAST OPTIONS:**

1. COFFEE- For people who love beverages and wish to sip some in the morning, nothing can beat a strong brewed coffee without sugar. As it is also known that coffee helps you maintain the right body weight and keep it healthy. Too much of anything wrong but a single cup in the morning can help you with a better start to the day. Those who don’t like coffee can also rely on green tea for a fresh start.

2. FRESH SEASONAL FRUITS AND NUTS- Fruits and nuts are easily available in one's household and can also be the best option while in a hurry. Fruits and nuts contain lots of good amounts of nutrients required by the body and you won't even have to wait till it gets cooked. Research has also found that having fruits first thing in the morning can be very beneficial.

3. CHIA SEEDS- These seeds contain lots of fiber and it is also high in antioxidants which might help in better digestion and metabolism, one can make a pudding or anything of their own which includes chia seeds as the main ingredient to obtain the best results/benefits out of it.

**What can the health-conscious breakfast options help you with?**

All of us eat breakfast in the morning so how does it just help the people or benefit the ones who are conscious with what they eat in their breakfast. The reason being, health-conscious people are more concerned about the time they eat than what they eat. Eating at the right time can also be beneficial to the human body, the right to eat your breakfast is before 10 am.

The right breakfast helps you boost your immune system by fighting away all the viruses invisible to your naked eyes. A healthy breakfast can stimulate the happy hormones in the body so to keep mood swings at bay. As breakfast consists of all the natural food items so it provides the required vitamins by the body and helps it fight deadly diseases.
