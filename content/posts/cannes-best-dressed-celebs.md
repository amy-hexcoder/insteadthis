With the Cannes Film Festival currently in its second week, the splendor and glamour of the red carpet do not appear to be waning anytime soon. This year's Cannes Film Festival will fill that sartorial hole in your feed if you are into glitz and glamour.

The purpose of the festival is to highlight the best in international cinema, but there are also some spectacular red carpet [moments](/stories/sunshine-moment-must-see-looks-of-2021) to behold.

Since the festival began on May 17, we've already been blown away by the red carpet. Let's glance at some of the best-dressed celebrities on the Cannes red carpet 22, including Eva Longoria in a Chopard choker and Alessandra Ambrosio in Elie Saab Couture.

## Katherine Langford

It's no surprise that Katherine's Cannes look is a hit with us. The actress donned a sparkling, extravagant dress at the festival. This look, featuring silver sequins and a long train adorned with a bow, was undeniably elegant. Moreover, the twisted updo is also beautifully sophisticated.

![](https://graziamagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/GettyImages-1240734217.jpg?resize=1024%2C1542)
*Credits@Google*

## Eva Longoria

Eva Longoria's elegant style stood out at the Cannes Film Festival. She wore a sleek black dress with a hint of sheer fabric. Alberta Ferretti's dress features a deep neckline that adds a dash of charm.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/jV6Vyyb8EC6Us7quMWcpnP-768-80.jpg.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## Rebecca Hall

The moment Rebecca Hall made her entrance, Cannes got better. This gal was glammed up in Gucci, her outfit adorned with crystal-studded rhinestones and shimmering material adding to the 70s spirit of the evening. Moreover, the cinched sleeves and drop earrings added an exciting twist to her overall appearance.

![](https://tomandlorenzo.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Rebecca-Hall-Cannes-Film-Festival-2022-Red-Carpet-Fashion-Custom-Gucci-Tom-LOrenzo-Site-3.jpg)
*Credits@Google*

## Alessandra Ambrosio

What a stunning dress! Alessandra looked magnificent in an Elie Saab couture ensemble that she was wearing. We were amazed at the elegance of the couture gown, decked with floral appliqués. A little sun-kissed makeup made this look spectacular.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/rfvyhYAHBktT5dVe3hff5S-768-80.jpg.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## Urvashi Rautela

This white gown would have been unmissable at the Cannes Film Festival! Urvashi Rautela, making her festival debut, looked stunning in a ruffled gown. A bold pair of earrings, a slender bun, and a stunning pair of bright lips rounded off this OTT look perfectly.

![](https://www.india.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/AP22137589761331-1024x683.jpg)
*Credits@Google*

## Elle Fanning

With her stunning look at Cannes, Elle Fanning commanded all the attention. She wore an embellished, strapless gown that looked like a fairytale. She looked pristine in the Armani Privé outfit, complete with a bow hair clip and a sparkling choker.

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Lxnm875qAgUhBV5pVZdZHj-768-80.jpg.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## Aishwarya Rai Bachchan

We were deeply enchanted by our OG queen's gorgeous ball gown bursting with flowers. An ensemble with a 3D effect from Dolce & Gabbana has all the attention and complements minimal accessories. Her look gained more finesse with half-open hair, winged eyeliner, and pink lip color.

![](https://preranatvchannel.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/aishwarya-rai-bachchan-at-2022-cannes-2.jpg)

## Tamannaah Bhatia

Tamannaah Bhatia debuted at the 75th Cannes Film Festival too, and what a debut! With a fitted gown and a veil, she exuded oomph. Added to the look were diamond earrings and high heels.
