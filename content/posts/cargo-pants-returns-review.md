## IT IS BACK CARGO PANTS

In the world of fashion, they are forever. [AKIRA Label](/stories/check-my-hands-review) [It Is Back Cargos Pants](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/it-is-back-cargo-pants) are made of a cotton blend with a high-rise waist, side pockets, cargo pockets at the back and on the thighs, and adjustable drawstring hems. There is a classic button and zipper closure on the front as well as a tie-up hem detail.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 97% Cotton 3% Spandex
- Care: Hand Wash Cold
- Stretch: Slight
- Rise: 11"
- Waist to Hem: 41" (approx.)
- Size: Small

**Thoughts:**

This pair of cargo pants is perfect if you love the baggy silhouette of Y2K fashion. In a cotton blend with a high waist, this piece adds comfort and flare to any wardrobe. To complete your pumped-up casual look, pair this piece with a cropped tee and loafers.

## ALEXA CARGO PANT

Wear The AKIRA Label [Alexa Cargo Pant](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/alexa-cargo-pant) to add some edge to your wardrobe. High-waisted wide-leg pants with slant pockets, a slick nylon fabrication, and two cargo pockets: one with a zip lock closure, and one with D-ring closure. Featuring faux back pockets, strappy detailing, adjustable toggle-cinched hems, and a zipper front closure.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 100% polyester
- Stretch: No
- Care: Hand wash cold
- Dimension: 12" rise, 42" waist to hem (approx.)
- Imported
- Size: Small
