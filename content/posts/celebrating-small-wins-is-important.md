All of us want to be successful in our goals, but the journey of reaching our destination can sometimes be very demotivating. This is why celebrating small wins that come your way is important. It gives you motivation and helps you push through difficulties and make a way to move ahead. So, what is the secret of successful people who achieve big goals? Read this blog to know more:

**What is meant by small wins?**

Small wins are something you accomplish that amalgamates with your intentions. They can be related to professional or personal life, finances, or habit changes. Small wins are easy to achieve, especially if you know what self-criticism and perfection mean.

**Appreciation helps a lot:**

Appreciation can be played down in life, and sometimes we forget how to appreciate what we have done. Appreciating our small wins and the small steps can create a big difference between succeeding and failing. Also, a lack of appreciation and [gratitude](https://ggia.berkeley.edu/practice/gratitude_journal#:~:text=A%20gratitude%20journal%20forces%20ourselves,can%20shift%20in%20profound%20ways.) can sometimes make us feel demotivated and we are not able to see the importance of our small wins. Celebrating the small stuff is all about acknowledging that we are on our way to achieving something big.

**Creating successful habits is important:**

Successful habits mean successful success. We know that creating and changing habits can be difficult as our mind finds it difficult to adapt to new strategies. So, acknowledging and celebrating the small wins are about helping yourself establish those habits you need and keep them going.

Our brains need positive feedback, and allowing yourself to be appreciated can result in an ‘addiction to progress'.

The secret to a successful habit is by understanding the importance of the current moment and taking the time to celebrate your small wins. We tend to take the present moment for granted. But the little things we do at the moment add a lot to our growth.

Also, you should invest in the small things over a long period and understand that you only live in the moment. It is also about a combination of moments over time and about achieving big things in life.

**Why you should celebrate tiny victories?**

The key to success is realizing that your big goals are not going to be achieved overnight. So, tend to focus on the end goals rather than the small steps which we take to get us there.

Hence, it is crucial to celebrate small wins. The problem with not doing this is that we end up diminishing our motivation, but only motivation keeps us on the right path and gives us the courage to do better even.

A lack of motivation happens when we are not sure of how far our goals are. We sometimes believe that the goal is still far away when it can be just around the corner.

So, it is, therefore, crucial to make sure that you celebrate the tiny goals that keep coming your way. Acknowledging small wins releases chemicals that give us a feeling of happiness and pride, thus making us take a step toward our next achievement.

**Reason for celebrating our small wins:**

**Boosting your energy:**

Sometimes when your energy is low, it can be difficult to achieve anything. Also, when you celebrate your small victories, you keep pushing your energy in the right direction. So, once you are high on energy, you feel motivated to achieve your small and big goals.

**It's all about positive emotions:**

When we are engaged in our lives, our confidence runs high, our actions match our intentions and our love for life grows. Also, we experience a life filled with meaning. So, if you want to experience happiness, you should always find ways to incorporate meaning into your life. Celebrating little wins helps in finding meaning in your life.

Also, if you are not willing to give up pursuing happiness, try to practice [happiness](https://www.pursuit-of-happiness.org/what-is-happiness/). Also, try to celebrate small victories.

**Celebrating small wins:**

When we tend to focus on the end goal, it can seem too far away to reach there. So, try to relax, take one thing at a time, and enjoy the moment. This also includes incremental progress which keeps happening in your life. So, you can celebrate your small wins by:

**1. Breaking large goals into smaller ones:**

You do not have to focus on greater things. Make sure that you create small goals that can allow you to see your progress. Also, these small successes will make you feel good with every little step.

**2. Rewarding yourself is important:**

Think about what you enjoy doing the most and make sure that you do this every time. This could be anything from treating yourself to your favorite coffee or traveling.

**3. Tracking your progress:**

Writing or tracking down your progress reminds you of how far you've come in achieving your desired goals. Sometimes, we give you because we are not aware of how far we are close to our success. So, write down all the small wins, which can be a reward in itself.

Remember that motivation plays a crucial role in your success. So, celebrating small wines is the key to leading a happy life.

**Takeaway:**

Learning how to celebrate small wins is the only key to keeping yourself motivated. Each time you achieve a milestone, find a way to celebrate yourself.
