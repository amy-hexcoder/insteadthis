## CHECK MY HANDS RING SET

This AKIRA Label [Check My Hands Ring Set](https://www.shopakira.com/accessories/jewelry/rings/check-my-hands-ring-set) will add a touch of class to your fresh mani. Five faux gold rings are included in this set, including a multi-layered stretchy band, a single flexible slim band, and three double rhinestone embellished rings. Stack them up or wear them individually to add some sparkle to your next outfit perfect for hitting the town!

**Features:**

- Material: All man-made
- Sizing: O/S on the model

**Here are some thoughts:**

Wearing this delicate, flattering, and authentic ring set will lend your ensemble an air of authenticity. Each of these elegant statement rings is adorned with a rhinestone, creating an ideal plush feel. The minimalistic chic look of each ring makes them a stylish addition to any outfit.
