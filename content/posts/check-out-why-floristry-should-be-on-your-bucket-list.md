QUESTION

Hi Amanda,

I have always wished I could do what I see in a florist's window. I tried making a bouquet myself and wondered why it didn't look quite right. What do you think about trying this florist hobby?

Love,

Josie!

ANSWER

Hi Josie,

Even though it seems pretty straightforward to stick a few flowers in a vase, it takes a bit of design knowledge and thought to create a really beautiful bouquet. This is why florist-made bouquets look so gorgeous! The good news is that basic floristry skills are not difficult to learn, and there are many reasons why floristry makes it such a great hobby.

## 1. No prior experience is necessary to get started.

You don't need any prior knowledge of flowers or floristry to get started with flower arranging, unlike other hobbies like playing an instrument or gardening. While snips and gloves are recommended, you only need your hands to start arranging–no special tools are needed. It is possible to learn the basics by reading a book or using [online resources](/stories/shopping-addiction-how-to-save-your-marriage).

![](https://floranext.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/floranext-hiring.png)

## 2. You'll learn a new skill.

Beautiful bouquets are not something everyone can arrange. It is quite a brag to have floristry skills under your belt, even if they are basic! As you learn how to arrange flowers, you will continue to discover new techniques and methods to do so. As a skill, it's constantly evolving, so you'll always be learning new things. Isn't that a cool skill?

## 3. It helps you relax and meditate.

Making flowers is a relaxing hobby that allows you to unwind while creating something beautiful. It's a bit like painting, in that you're really listening to your intuition, putting things together in a way that feels good. Also, a flower arrangement is an effective meditation method.

## 4. You can give your arrangements as gifts.

It's time to say goodbye to worrying about what to get loved ones for every birthday and celebration! Create stunning, one-of-a-kind gifts by creating stunning bouquets or gift bags. It is more thoughtful to give them arranged flowers rather than anything from a store since you put time and effort into it.

![](https://kevsbest.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/5-Best-Florists-in-Tulsa.png)

Finally, floristry is not just a useful tool, but a very [relaxing hobby](https://www.bhg.com/decorating/home-accessories/flower-arranging/how-to-make-a-flower-arrangement/) for anyone who is interested. It looks like the best hobby of all, doesn't it? Beautiful bouquets, easy gift options, a niche skill that will certainly impress, and a new way to explore meditation and mindfulness.

Love,

Amanda!
