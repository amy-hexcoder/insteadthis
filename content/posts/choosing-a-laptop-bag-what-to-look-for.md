QUESTION

Hi Petrica,

I just started a new job and am looking for a good office bag that fits my laptop, water bottle, books, and other necessities for the office. Would you mind helping me find one?

Love,

Shanaya!

ANSWER

Hi Shanaya,

Congratulations on your new position! One way to make your work life even better is to choose the right laptop bag during this exciting time.

Whether you're a tech enthusiast or a trendy trendsetter, your laptop bag is more than just an accessory; it's your [trusted sidekick](/stories/how-can-couples-with-dual-careers-make-it-work).

So, let's explore [what to look](https://arcticfox.com/blogs/news/factors-to-consider-while-choosing-a-laptop-bag) for in laptop bags to be both stylish and productive during your commute:

### 1. Size and Fit:

Consider the size of your laptop first. Does it require a cozy 13-inch nest or a spacious 15-inch hideaway? Like Cinderella's glass slipper, your lappy should fit comfortably into your bag. You'll be able to protect your tech treasure from the daily rigors of life with a snug fit.

### 2. Compartments Galore:

A laptop bag should have compartments for everything, like Mary Poppins' magic bag. Look for zippered pockets, pen holders, and padded slots for your laptop. Nothing is worse than digging through a black hole of tangled cords and lost pens during a meeting!

![](https://www.dailyobjects.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/banner.png)

### 3. Durability Matters:

You will be using your bag every day. Materials that can hold up to daily commuting trials should be used. With water-resistant fabric and reinforced zippers, your gadgets will stay dry during unexpected downpours.

### 4. Comfort is Key:

Look for a bag with padded shoulders and a back panel to prevent those day-long aches and pains. Make sure your bags have adjustable straps.

### 5. Safety First:

Protect your laptop from prying eyes by using a laptop bag with extra security features. During crowded commutes, anti-theft zippers and RFID-blocking compartments can make all the difference.

Your laptop bag is more than just a carrier for your tech gear; it's also a practical companion for your daily commute to work. Make your bag a reflection of your personality. Until next time, happy bag hunting and success in your workdays!

Love,

Petrica!
