## GIGI KNIT PLAID OVERSIZED SWEATER- [REVIEW](/stories/fits-product-reviews)

This super-comfy [Gigi Knit Plaid Oversized Sweater](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/holiday-gift-guide/gigi-knit-plaid-oversized-sweater) from AKIRA Label features ribbed knit and an oversized silhouette. With flared sleeves and a turtleneck collar, it is a chic piece you won't want to miss. The cropped hem and ribbed wrists add some cute details. You can team this sweater with the matching AKIRA Label Gigi Knit Plaid Mini Skirt for a cozy holiday look!

**Features:**

- Fabric: 82% Viscose, 14% Polyester
- Care: Hand wash only
- Dimensions: 14.5" shoulder to hem
- Sleeves: 27" approximately
- Product: Imported
- Size: S/M

**Thoughts:**

An ideal knit sweater for everyday wear, it's soft to the touch, skin-friendly, and not itchy. It's a loose and stretchy knit that suits almost every figure. I think this would make an awesome [Christmas](/stories/solo-christmas-celebrations-how-to-have-a-magical-time) sweater.

## AZALEA WANG DIAMOND DRIP STILETTO BOOTIE IN BLACK- [REVIEW](/stories/mens-grooming-product-reviews)

AZALEA WANG's [Diamond Drip Stiletto Bootie in Black](https://www.shopakira.com/shoes/azalea-wang-diamond-drip-stiletto-bootie-in-black) sports a black vinyl leather upper and ends mid-calf. A clear stiletto heel and pointed toe make these shoes quite striking. Wrap around lace detail adorned with rhinestones, as well as a side zipper closure, combine to make it an instant classic.

**Feature:**

- Sizing and details are approx.
- Peaked toe and stiletto heel
- Heel Height 4.5"
- Imported

**Thoughts:**

These mid-calf boots instantly infuse sharpness into any outfit. The pointed toe and the stiletto heels give them an elevated look. You don't have to worry about your weight redistributing in heels, making them comfortable to wear all day long.

What beautiful earrings! This pair is very snug on the earlobes. It has a unique [setting](/stories/getting-the-most-out-of-your-thrift-store-trip-every-time), making it very versatile. I am completely blown away by this pair of elegant earrings.
