No matter how you take it, first dates are usually preceded by nerves, a lot of jitters, and a lot of panics. You go on double-checking everything.

So, just listen, when you are dealing with first-date nerves, make sure that you wear something that you are comfortable in, something that defines 'YOU' and makes you the best version of 'yourself'...So, whether you are heading out on a daytime coffee date or having reservations at the cool restaurants, take some styling tips for your first date from us.

**KNOW YOUR STYLE PERSONALITY:**

Start by understating your styling personality and what image you would like to portray. Your clothes must make you feel your best version. So, to express yourself with your clothes, think about what you want to convey, it can be modern, romantic, classic or traditional.

If you want to portray a modern look, try something minimalistic. For an effortless look; you can mix- and- match structured tailoring with a soft jumper. You can also dress from head to toe in one color and choose wide-leg [high-waisted trousers](https://www.outfittrends.com/high-waisted-pants-outfits-ways-to-wear-high-waisted-pants/) with block heels to make your legs look extra long. We would always say that fashion is a tool, and it is both on the observer and the wearer. It is about creating a first impression that reflects your style and self.

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/m-1024x996.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**DRESS ACCORDING TO YOUR BODY TYPE AND COMPLEXION:**

When you are getting ready for your first date, it is important to choose dresses that suit your body shape and skin complexion.

Also, avoid [oversized clothes](https://www.merricksart.com/how-to-wear-oversized-clothes/) or too-tight pieces, instead opt for that one piece that will fit you perfectly. When deciding on colors, select shades that suit your skin. Picking, the right shade for you is important, and it can even brighten up your face. Wrong colors will make you look dull and tired too.

**DRESSING FOR THE DATE VENUE:**

An important aspect of the first date is the venue. It is always important to check the venue location online and pick your outfit accordingly so that you don't look underdressed or overdressed.

"For dressy dinner, pick intricate fabrics. If you want to be something creative, then opt for intricate prints. Nothing beats a killer dress in feminine print and a [pair of heels](https://www.independent.co.uk/life-style/fashion/how-to-find-pair-high-heels-that-dont-hurt-tips-advice-foot-size-a7347731.html). Everyone looks more elegant in heels and you should also feel relaxed on your first date.

![](/images/uploads/2022/01/d1.jpg)

**TRANSFORMING YOUR WORK LOOK TO YOUR DATE LOOK IS NOW EASY:**

So, for people who have to go from work to date; you can transform your work look into a date look by adding a few elements. If your workwear is formal, you can switch to your trousers and add statement earrings. Adding some accessories is a great way of transitioning from your workwear to date wear.

For more casual dates like lunch, you can opt for well-fitted staples and add a scarf or shoes.

Also, when you choose your outfit, finding the right balance in your look, whether it is a smart-casual mix and match or a minimalistic look with decent color, feel confidence is important.

**THE DOS AND DON'TS:**

**Do:**

1.) Always wear something that you have already worn.

2.) Always check the venue on Instagram before deciding on what to wear.

3.) Think about what makes you feel good and confident.

**DON'TS:**

1.) Never wear a brand-new piece that is pricking you up like a needle.

2.) Never overthink and over-experiment with your looks.
