Nothing says [romance](/stories/first-christmas-as-a-couple-tips-for-giving-it-your-all) like Paris, with its beautiful canals, incredible sights, pretty streets, and foodie heaven. There's love everywhere in Paris! A romantic trip to Paris is a wonderful way to express your love for your loved ones.

For those who want to experience the romance of Paris with their loved ones but aren't sure what to do, we show you the romantic things to do in Paris and the hot spots that will make you fall in love once more. With these 9 romantic must-dos in Paris, you're sure to feel all the feels:

## Must-Dos in Paris

## 1. Picnic under the Eiffel Tower

Even though Paris has so many parks and gardens, there is nothing more romantic than picnicking under the Eiffel Tower! Make your own picnic basket or order one from some popular cafes and treat your loved one to a romantic date in Paris.

## 2. Enjoy an evening cruise on the Seine

You can't beat an evening cruise on the Seine in Paris! A Seine cruise will make you and your loved one even closer as you enjoy the breeze, glittering lights, and a romantic dinner on the river.

During the cruise, you'll also get to watch the Eiffel Tower sparkle for 5 minutes every hour!

## 3. Get a couple's photoshoot in Paris

While in Paris, you should do a couple photoshoot with a professional photographer! I mean there's no better way to keep your memories of Paris than through pictures! You can goof around with your partner while making lovely poses in Paris.

## 4. At night, stroll around the city.

Even though Paris is beautiful, it's even more magical at night! A leisurely stroll through the city at night is a great activity for couples in Paris! Think of walking hand-in-hand down a pretty street with your significant other under the city lights! It doesn't get any better! It's also free, so you won't have to spend a dime on a romantic moment in Paris.

## 5. Take your date to a posh restaurant.

Even in Paris, going out to dinner is the most romantic thing to do! It's just that you'll be able to taste some of Paris' prettiest restaurants' world-renowned French cuisine.

You can make it even grander by booking a table at a restaurant that has an Eiffel Tower view! Say sweet nothings to each other while tasting Paris' favorite foods!

## 6. Enjoy a picturesque sunset.

Sunset is a symbol of romance! While some people prefer sunsets on the beach, sunsets with the Eiffel Tower as a backdrop are hard to beat! Watching a sunset is one of the best romantic must-dos in Paris on a budget.

## 7. Say I love you on the I love you wall

In Paris, I love you is spelled out 311 times in 250 languages on a 40-square-meter wall dedicated to love.

If you are enjoying Montmartre, don't forget to stop by Jehan Rictus garden square to say "I love you" in those many languages. Make it even more exciting by guessing the languages as you Google the correct answers! While it might seem like a simple thing to do, it's actually very romantic.

## 8. Get a sidecar ride on a vintage bike.

Paris romantic dates don't have to be boring or just a meal! Sidecar motorbikes are a new trend in Paris that's sure to please those with an adventurous spirit.

## 9. Get a romantic massage in the darkness.

The kind of massage that I am talking about here may seem ordinary but it isn't. This unique massage will allow you to get rid of all of your insecurities in a dimly lit room. Nothing makes a romantic getaway in Paris more interesting than being pampered in the dark while listening to soothing music.

Paris has many romantic must-dos to offer, but I hope that this article gave you [some unique ideas](https://francevoyager.com/most-romantic-things-to-do-in-paris-for-couples/) for planning a romantic Paris weekend getaway with your loved one.
