## DESTINATION PARADISE DAISY CROCHET CROP TOP

Daisy floral detailing embellishes the tank straps of this [Destination Paradise Daisy Crochet Crop Top](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/destination-paradise-daisy-crochet-crop-top) from AKIRA Label. The unlined crocheted base provides an airy feel. The cropped style of this piece is emphasized by cable knit detailing.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 100% Acrylic
- Care: Hand wash cold
- Dimensions: 15" from shoulder to hem
- Size: Small (approx.)

**Several thoughts:**

There's a great summery feel to this top, and I love the daisy detail on the straps. Gorgeous! Wear it with the coordinating AKIRA Label Destination Paradise Daisy Crochet Maxi Skirt, and you're all set for your summer getaway!

## DESTINATION PARADISE DAISY CROCHET MAXI SKIRT

Vacay never looked so good. This [Destination Paradise Daisy Crochet Maxi Skirt](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/destination-paradise-daisy-crochet-maxi-skirt) by AKIRA Label features an acrylic crochet fabrication, an elastic waistband, a maxi length silhouette, and slits on the sides. Crocheted daisies, cable patterns, and wooden buttons make up this flirty skirt.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 100% Acrylic
- Care: Hand wash only
- Dimensions: 34" waist to hem
- Slit: 24"
- Fringe: 6"

**A few thoughts:**

OMGOSH! What a gorgeous skirt! It's got some great details, a snug fit, and is super comfy to boot. The daisies scattered all over it make it look so pretty.

## AZALEA WANG ALL YOU NEED IS LOVE CHUNKY SANDALS IN YELLOW

Make your heart sing with this sandal! The neon snake-embossed faux patent leather upper is the highlight of the AZALEA WANG [All You Need Is Love Chunky Sandal](https://www.shopakira.com/shoes/azalea-wang-all-you-need-is-love-chunky-sandal-in-yellow) in Yellow. With a clear sculpted heel and an open square toe, it has an insole with a cushion and a pair of thin faux leather toe bands.

**Features:**

- Toe: Open square
- Heel: chunky
- Upper: PU
- Height: 4" (based on size 8).

**Thoughts:**

It's no secret that clear chunky heels are my passion, and I was blown away by this sandal! I love this heel because it is so comfortable and doesn't scratch even after all-day wearing.
