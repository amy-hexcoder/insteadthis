**Tips for your successful dating photoshoot:**

1.) Make sure that your shoot is a 'lifestyle' shoot and not a corporate headshot. Refrain from using backdrops in solid colors.

2.) Make sure there is only one person in your photo, you! Do not include anyone else in your picture.

3.) Dress well. This thing matters.

4.) This platform is not the place to show off your abs or trying to look overtly sexy- it will always backfire on you.

5.) Never use a selfie.

6.) A gentle smile in your dating photography will always give you maximum swipes. So, smile often!
