## FEATHERS ALL OVER CROP HIGH WAIST JEANS

With a washed denim fabrication and a high-rise waistline, the [Feathers All Over Crop High Waist Jeans](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/feathers-all-over-crop-high-waist-jeans) from AKIRA Label are an ideal match. This classic five-pocket design has working belt loops. You'll be smitten by whiskered detailing, skinny, cropped legs, and feather-detailed ankle hems.

**Features:**

- Material: 72% Cotton 24% Polyester 3% Viscose 1% Spandex
- Care: Hand Wash Cold
- Stretch: Slight
- Dimension: 35" Waist to Hem
- Rise: 11.5"
- Size: 3
