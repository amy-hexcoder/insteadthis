Hi Jenna!

Happy Friendship's day gorgeous! Thank you so much for your kind words. It matters to me. Well, I would say that I do not believe in Friendship Day or Valentine's Day. That is because every day should be a day for lovers and friends. Yes, that is true! You know, Jeena, many pretentious people keep flooding my timeline with friendship day wishes. Some of them keep messaging me constantly, but that is very funny. It is because I did not have any conversation with them.

You know Jeena, [true friends](https://tinybuddha.com/blog/25-ways-to-be-a-true-friend/) are the ones who have seen you through all your highs and lows. They do not need one particular day to express their love. They should be celebrated every day, every minute, and every second.

I have friends who have helped me get away from stress, anxiety, and depression. They are the ones who know the real me. And celebrating them on one particular day does not do them justice. So, I celebrate them every day! We sit, we sing, we chill, and we Netflix.

I really cannot explain what they mean to me, but yes, they are my world that makes me complete.

Also, there are many beautiful memories I had with them. Like, singing in the salon or just sitting and jamming together. Sometimes, good conversations over a glass of wine make us happy. Yes, there are plenty of amazing memories which make them special.

So, yes, I have made [friends](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k5ukKf4vi6g&ab_channel=JordanPetersonFanChannel) from all walks of my life and for us, every day is a friendship day.

Hugs!

Petrica!
