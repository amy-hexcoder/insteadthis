Over the years, many of the latest hairstyles have changed and evolved with the times. Many hairstyles have proven to stand the test of time, and they will always be in style. While your favorite celebrities are never afraid of dramatic changes, many stars have haircuts that keep on coming back again and again. So, whether you are loyal to a long length or want to try a curly hairstyle, you are assured to find your new signature cut and all thanks to the following timeless styles. So, say goodbye to the cringe-worthy 'dos of old pictures past and read this blog.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/start-2-1024x650.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**1.)   SIDE PART HAIRSTYLE:**

This hairstyle is a similar alternative to the middle part, and you can switch between the two hairstyles for sure. You will recognize that the side part hairstyles have been sported- by celebrities like Shay Mitchell, Emma Stone, and Sienna Miller. The side part hairstyle is a signature look for a couple of celebrities and many other women. This hairstyle is so popular because it looks flattering on anyone, no matter the shape of your hair or face. The versatility of this haircut never goes out of style. It goes perfectly well with side-swept bangs that add movement to your hair and can soften your features too.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/11-7.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**2.)   ANGLED BOB HAIRSTYLE:**

It is a fashionable hairstyle that has been in style for a long time. It gives you a sleek and modern appearance which makes your look effortlessly cool. This cut is usually shorter in the back, while the longer layers will cover your face in a cool way. So, whether you have a short-angled bob or shorter hair, this [hairstyle](https://in.pinterest.com/pin/39054721743185134/) will bring out the best from your facial features and make you look stunning.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/12-4.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**3.)   [BEACHY WAVES](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JdJooU57Qno&ab_channel=AllanaDavison):**

It is a go-to celebrity hairstyle for one good reason. It looks good on virtually everyone since the effortless look can go with all face shapes, ages, and hair types. Though this hairstyle is deliberately messy, it still manages to come across as charming. A good feature about beachy waves is that you can style them with different hair products to create a distinctive appearance.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/13-3.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**4.)   NATURAL CURLY HAIRSTYLE:**

So, if your hair is naturally curly, you can embrace it for a timeless look. This hairstyle is always fresh and appealing. Whether you have short hair or long hair curls, add volume to your face. But always make sure to take care of your beautiful curls by using plenty of conditioners and shampoos so that your hair doesn't get dry. Also, never use hot styling tools which, can be hard on curly hair, so always make sure that they are sundried naturally.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/CURLY-2.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**5.)   PIXIE CUT HAIRSTYLES:**

So, with erratic schedules, there is a constant demand for chic low-maintenance hairstyles. It is where pixie cut hairstyles come into action. This hairstyle gives you a more playful and youthful look. A pixie cut is choppy, asymmetrical, and tousled. It is a cute haircut that never goes out of fashion. This hairstyle allows you to have some experiment and fun with short hair. These haircuts add texture and manageable volumes. So, if your hair is fine, you can choose the right [pixie haircut](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBZI3R9yfKU&ab_channel=SanjaCaricaKarasman) that adds dimension to your hair.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/15-2.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**6.)   MIDDLE PART HAIRSTYLES:**

When we talk about the middle part of hairstyles, they are always popular because they make you look clean, sleek, and stylish. Many celebrities like Jessica Alba and Gwyneth Paltrow have flaunted their middle part hairstyles in confidence. Yes, we know that these actresses change their appearances occasionally, but this hairstyle is always on their to-do list. So, whether you are looking for shorter hair or long locks, you can still ask your hairstylist to renew your look with this hairstyle. It looks good with straight hair. It gives you a sophisticated look at any time or on any occasion.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/16-1.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**7.)   LONG HAIRSTYLES WITH BANG:**

It is an effortlessly chic hairstyle that never goes out of fashion. No matter how you dress up, this hairstyle will give you an elegant look that cannot be replicated- with any other hairstyle. The bangs need not be particularly long, and a decent length will make you look good. Your [long bangs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uZWxiecP-GY&ab_channel=IPSY) will look super stylish when paired with sleek locks.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/17-1-799x1024.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**8.)   TWO-TONED RINGLETS:**

Celebrities like Beyonce are flattered with equally warm hair color, like her tone-on-tone honey blond shade. The two distinct colors give her tousled curls a good amount of contrast. The two-tone hair is a cool trend and is very creative and super fun in that way. So, opt for this super amazing look and let your hair do the talking.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/18-1.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**9.)   LONG TAPERED LAYERS:**

Choppy layers tapered a few inches from the bottom of the hair adds movement to lengthy strands. Also, the longer fringe makes room for versatility. So, whenever you feel like sporting this hairstyle, go for it!

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/19-1-811x1024.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**10.) [THE LONG BOB](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TZAuI4niduI&ab_channel=LynSire):**

Indeed, this is a great one-size-fits-all option hairstyle that looks very graceful. It gives you an edgy look and enhances your personality. Many celebrities have sported this hairstyle. The long bob hairstyle has stolen many hearts. So, one in a lifetime, try sporting this hairstyle. It will make you look different, classy and stylish.

So, whenever you think of chopping your hair, you can try any of these hairstyles that will make you look classy and graceful too.
