Hey, happy year-end of 2021 to you all! We are grateful to be sharing with you on this very auspicious day of a calendar year. We believe that time constructs allow us the opportunity to adjust, reflect and enter a new year with pride and wisdom. So, as we say goodbye to 2021, we felt. that you should end your year with more positive aspirations.

We also believe that each calendar year brings challenges, opportunities, lessons, and new growths. Sometimes one year can make things harder and some years more blessed. We are not here to tell you how 2021 went for you but to offer you an opportunity to pause, reflect, and take your time to express your [gratitude](https://positivepsychology.com/gratitude-appreciation/) for what all has passed.

If we have no time to reflect and understand, then we are not destined to repeat our life's circumstances over and over again. At times, we also love reflecting on past years, going through our old pictures and writings, noticing what has changed and how it has changed. We always see a shift in tone, and it is a meaningful sign of growth. Whether or not you feel that 2021 has been good to you, take some time to go through the things, the hardships, and the lessons learned.

**WAYS TO END YOUR YEAR ON A POSITIVE NOTE:**

**1.) Just stay in the moment:**

Yes, your year-end is filled with dos and dos, and it feels like there is not enough time to get to everything on the list. Each year has its personality. So, take the time to appreciate that you had come out of it.

**2.) Create memory books:**

Make sure that you create a memory book. It will help you capture things you have learned this year and how you learned them. It is also the time for all of us to be thankful for everything that has shaped us. Also, to preserve your memories, try making a memory book that will capture things about you and your life.

**3.) Recognise the year's [accomplishments](https://goalcrazyplanners.com/blogs/news/5-reasons-why-is-it-important-to-write-you-accomplishments):**

Have you taken out time to think about all your accomplishments this year? If not, then take a pause and think about the moment to recognize and celebrate what you have achieved so far.

**4.) Just focus on kindness:**

Make sure that you spend some time thinking about what went wrong. Instead of paying attention to something that turned your focus away, pay attention to remembering the moments of kindness. Overall, talk about positive things only.

**CLOSING NOTE:**

We might have felt a multitude of emotions this year. But sometimes, some moments are insurmountable- while others are full of shine. During the lows and highs, we are reminded that life is changing, people are changing, and things are changing too. So, remind yourself of your inner strength and overcome anything that will break you. Yes, we had unique experiences and opportunities that have changed us. So, take time to honor that and get ready for brand new adventures that life has stored for you.

Wishing you the happiest, loving, and most peaceful supportive end to this decade.

With all the love, hope, and possibility for the New year ahead, let us end this 2021 and enter 2022 with full enthusiasm.
