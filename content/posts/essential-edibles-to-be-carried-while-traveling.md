A perfect trip requires perfect planning!

If you wish to explore this enthralling world, you must plan your trips efficiently. From packing your bags to food items you decide to carry and from your outfits to the weight of the luggage, everything affects the quality of your [trip](https://www.claimcompass.eu/blog/travel-food-blogs/). Planning can ease the entire experience, save your time, and it will avoid unwanted chaos.

Above all the other required planning, taking care of your health is a must because your body needs to be fit enough for the trip. Fasting, starving, overeating, and an unhealthy diet can harm your mental and physical health. So, keeping your body healthy should be the top priority while [traveling](/stories/travel-around-the-world-with-your-pet).

**List of** **essential edibles**

- Plenty of drinking water: Especially if you prefer to drink boiled water. Change in the quality of water can also result in food poisoning.
- Dry dates and dry fruits (Seeds): The healthiest food items to carry while [traveling](https://www.budgettravel.com/).
- Wheat Flakes Nut Mix: Make sure to eat the right amount of wheat flakes nut mix.
- Fruits: Fruits will not just provide you with nutrition but also will keep you hydrated. Just make sure not to eat fruit on an empty stomach.
- Vegetable chips: Low-fat vegetable chips can be healthy as well, a tasty snack to eat while reading a novel on your trip.
- Sandwich: Make sure to make a healthy vegetable sandwich that can last for as long as possible.
- Juice: Not just juice but, you can also carry detox water with you.
- Nuts: Salted, fried, or boiled nuts can be a healthy and handy snack to carry.
- Boiled eggs: It is required to pack boiled eggs in the right way as they can get spoiled easily but, boiled eggs are one of the healthiest things to have for the right amount of protein.
- Bean salad: Make sure to make it low fat if you want to fry it, or else you can pack a simple uncooked salad with sprouts and veggies.

**Why it is necessary to carry [essential food](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/healthy-road-trip-snacks)**

Traveling can be unpredictable. You never really know what could happen next, which is why planning is required to be ready to face any challenges. The most important thing to keep in mind while traveling is to keep all the essential things with you, and the topmost priority is food. Traveling can be hectic, expensive, and unexpected as you never know how much the food items may cost or how far you’ll have to travel to have a single meal. In such situations, people tend to eat whatever and whichever thing is available. Such reckless behavior can make you sick.

So, it is better to keep all these things in mind beforehand and plan accordingly.

**Food items to avoid while traveling**

- Raw meat
- Seafood
- Bushmeat.
- Berries
- Apples
- Frozen foods
- Sauces and condiments
- Shellfish
- Raw vegetables

**Cons of** **not maintaining a healthy diet while traveling**

People often skip their meals while traveling, which causes weakness, low metabolism, anxiety, low blood pressure, etc. Similarly, eating unhealthy food can cause poor digestion, tiredness, stress, food poisoning, etc.

These things can cause more problems if you are planning a long-run trip. They can damage your body internally and result in serious health issues.

**Vital luggage**

We desire to pack as many things as we can but keeping your [luggage](https://www.st-christophers.co.uk/travel-blog/what-to-pack-in-your-travel-backpack) handy can help you in many ways. Only carry the required amount of clothes and save most of the space in your bag for essentials.

**Necessary items to be carried while traveling**

- Travel Wallet
- Well-planned set of clothes
- Toiletries
- Medications
- Sunscreen
- Face Masks & Sanitizer
- Neck Pillow
- Travel Pouch
- Sleeping Bag Liner
- Earphones

**If you are planning a backpacking trip**

If you are planning a backpacker trip like hiking, camping, or trekking, you’ll have to add an extra amount of food items because on such trip’s you don’t have the privilege to shop for food items or dine in a restaurant or café. In the list of essential foods, you can add frozen vegetables as well.

**Checklist for a backpacking trip**

- Hiking boots or shoes
- Backpack
- Tent
- Sleeping bag and sleeping pad
- Stove and fuel
- Kitchen supplies
- Plenty of food
- Water bottles and water-treatment supplies
- Weather-appropriate clothing
- Emergency and hygiene supplies
- Small repair kit

**Tip for a Backpacking trip**

Though backpacking water supply will be easy. But to be on the [safer](https://www.nationwide.com/lc/resources/home/articles/travel-saf) side, always boil the water before drinking.

**Take away**

Always keep the food items in disposable and airtight containers and packages, and remember to clean the containers before reusing them.

If you wish to explore the food of different places you are traveling to, make sure to check the reviews of that place beforehand.

Always carry a sanitizer and tissue paper with you to use before and after having a meal.
