In today's busy world, it does not take long for couples in a relationship to fall out of love. If you seriously think about it, although you sleep next to them, eat meals with them, or even talk to them, how much connection is nurturing and mindful to let your relationship sustain?

Because of our hectic schedules, we are unable to give time to our beloveds. And as we all know, relationships take time, and at times they do need a lot of work. But if taken seriously, being together with each other can be fun.

**GESTURES TO INFUSE LOVE INTO YOUR RELATIONSHIP:**

Many couples do not know that every day loving gestures can go a long way. They help in nurturing a healthy relationship. So, by boosting your partner's day in small ways, it can add a lot to your [relationship](/stories/is-there-any-impact-of-zodiac-signs-in-a-relationship).

![](/images/uploads/2022/05/r2.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

Well, here are the small everyday gestures you can do daily to make your relationship work.

**1.) Leaving a love note is the first step:**

It can be very simple. You can simply grab an 'I love you' note on the bathroom mirror and express your love for your partner. Or you can simply add on something unexpected like "Treat yourself with an ice-cream' kinda notes. It makes them feel special.

**2.) Greeting each other genuinely**

It is an important thing to do in any relationship. Just make it a point to give each other a hearty welcome along with a kiss and hug when you get back home. Many times, we underestimate the power of a hug and a kiss, especially after we have been together for a while. However, all of us like it, and we value it too.

**3.) Look into each other eyes and [communicate.](https://www.ourrelationship.com/7-steps-to-understanding-the-importance-of-communication-in-a-relationship/)**

Make loving eye contact as much as possible while talking. You communicate a lot through your eyes.

**4.) Celebrating small achievements:**

Be there for your partner in their small achievements. You can celebrate their tiny milestones in their career with special dinners and flowers. Every time we highlight our partner's actions, we make our lives even better. It is an emotional deposit.

**5.) Reaching out to them during the day**

Do not wait until your partner gets home. Send them a long loving text message to let them know that you are thinking about them. Shoot them an email or something that says something funny or loving. It is a great booster for your relationship.

![](/images/uploads/2022/05/r4.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**6.) Giving them time and listening to them attentively**

If your partner vents about their day, try to empathize instead of reacting. And give them your time when they are talking to you. Also, when your partner is excited about their day, give them focused attention. Always make an effort to be present for one another during all the highs and lows of life.

**7.) Surprising each other**

Always bring something special at home once in a while. It can be anything, a flower, a cake, or your partner's favorite dessert. Or you can also drop off their favorite lunch at work when they are too busy to get out. It helps your partner know that you are thinking about them.

**8.) Getting goofy**

Try acting out of character by adding lightness and laughter to your relationship. It works as a stress-buster in your relationship. Simply do something out of the box to shake things up! Nothing is more healing than laughter therapy. Try this, it works!

**9.) Giving loving touches daily**

It is another important thing to make your relationship work. You can walk by each other or give your partner that extra-long hug. It helps you stay connected and make things work in your relationship.

**Small things matter!**

Just like a small amount of money when put into a savings account can grow into a huge nest egg, small actions can add a lot to your relationship. So, try doing this daily and make your partner feel special.
