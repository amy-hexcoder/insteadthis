## LOOKS CAN FOOL YOU FLORAL MAXI DRESS

With a vintage-inspired floral print, the [Looks Can Fool You Floral Maxi Dress](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/looks-can-fool-you-floral-maxi-dress) features a sheer, lightweight fabrication. A plunging neckline, long blouson sleeves, and gorgeous ruffles on the shoulders and wrists make this dress a beauty. Shirred elastic runs across the waistline and back, lined with polyester; this maxi style is sure to turn heads.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 100% Polyester, Lining: 100% Polyester
- Care: Hand Wash Cold
- Dimensions: 59.5" Shoulder to hem
- Sleeves: 27.5"
- Lining: 31"
- Size: Small

**My thoughts:**
I'm a fan of long floral dresses, and this one stands out. Exactly what you'd want to wear on a warm summer night. Thanks to its elastic waistband, they accentuate your body's curves. Pair it with strappy sandals, and you're ready to go!
