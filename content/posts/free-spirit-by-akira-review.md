**FREE SPIRIT SATIN MAXI DRESS**

Can’t be tamed. AKIRA Label [Free Spirit Satin Maxi Dress](https://www.shopakira.com/brands/clothing-brands/akira-label/free-spirit-satin-maxi-dress) is crafted from satin base fabrication and features a vibrant abstract spilled paint print, self-tie halter tank straps, a deep plunging neckline, an O-ring accent on the center chest, an open back, a hip-hugging silhouette, and a maxi length with a slit hem for easy movement. It features sexy side cut outs, additional self-tie straps in the mid-back, and a tonal back zipper. Finish off your bold look with strappy sandals and a statement bag.
