It is a festival about the colors that give meaning to our lives, the colors that fill in the voids and add sparkle to them, the colors that remind us of what happiness and joy are, and the colors that unite us all to celebrate.

As the Coronavirus ruined the last festival, we will celebrate with double the fervor this year to make it up for that. Keeping in mind that everything begins at home, let's all take this opportunity to decorate and [spruce up](/stories/get-into-the-holi-spirit-through-these-vibrant-and-exciting-games) our homes ahead of Holi. With a pinch of creativity and the right accents, you can make your home [Holi-ready](https://www.squareyards.com/blog/top-5-holi-decoration-ideas-for-2021) without putting in the extra effort. Here are a few tips for making your home decor reflect the essence of Holi.

## Understand This Month's Theme.

The only thing that comes to mind when we think about Holi is the countless colors. This year, go overboard with the colors. Make the house look as beautiful as possible by decorating it with numerous shades and tints. Try not to get caught up in the details; go with the flow. If it feels overwhelming, dial it back a little after everything is complete. Also, make full use of the color palette since that's the defining theme for this month.

![](/images/uploads/2022/03/colorful-rugs-adorn-home-interior-with-blue-wingback-chair-and-small-round-table-on-wood-floor-3.jpg)
*Credits@Google*

## Start By Replacing Your Curtains.

In a lot of our homes, curtains take up more space than walls, and painting the walls can be tedious. However, installing or changing curtains isn't as complicated as it may sound. To begin, let's discuss curtains. You can use both printed or sheer curtains of diverse colors. Even better, if you're short on time, you can make a curtain out of old dupattas in your closet. It instantly adds charm to your home with its striking colors. It will help you decide how to decorate the rest of the room/home once the curtains are installed.

![](/images/uploads/2022/03/s-l1600.jpg)
*Credits@Google*

## Invest In New Cushions.

Of course, it makes no sense to buy a new couch every time a festival comes around, but changing the cushion covers can dramatically transform the overall look. Turn the tiniest things in your home into a colorful paradise if you want to give your house a Holi vibe. A multicolored set of cushion covers will do the trick.

![](/images/uploads/2022/03/1003280-3.jpg)
*Credits@Google*

## Throws And Covers For Sofas.

Choose a multicolored or a matching couch throw and cover that fits the surrounding area as well. A new exterior will give your home an entirely new look. You can even get one personalized for your home if you prefer.

## Table Runner.

In the overhaul of the look, don't forget the tables. Add color to your table by adding a layer of colorful collectibles on top of it or by draping a matching table runner across it.

![](/images/uploads/2022/03/Atrangi_Studio_Flower_Table_Runner_1_1024x1024@2x-3-1024x1024.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## Choose Vibrant Home Decor!

To add more color to your home, use more streamers and DIY projects. Artificial flowers, colored fairy lights, and balloons, among other items, make your home look pretty. Further, arrange everything in a way that it will look seamless. Adding a colorful rug, colorful chairs, and colorful flower vases to the corners will brighten the room. If you find things are too overwhelming, remove some items or tone them down a bit.

![](/images/uploads/2022/03/cdi-eagle-plain-glass-bottle-assorted-1000-ml-cdi-eagle-plain-glass-bottle-assorted-1000-ml-l6a1sr.webp)

## Upgrade your bedroom!

A bedroom makeover is not complete without Holi decor. Create a fresh atmosphere with bright, aromatic candles. Light up your bedroom with sconces and new lighting to match the festivities. Let the darkness and melancholy of your bedroom out in true Holi spirit. Fresh flowers and candles will add a sense of cheerfulness to the ambiance. Set the mood of color with colorful bedsheets and vibrant pillows.

![](/images/uploads/2022/03/08-1-1024x536.jpg)
*Credits@Google*

## Jazz up your bathtime!

Switch out your plain bathroom towels for colorful ones. Get colorful scented soap balls. Make the bathroom appear extra-special by decorating them on the countertop. Put vases of blooming flowers in the bathroom window to complement the Holi decor.

![](/images/uploads/2022/03/11-4-1024x536.jpg)
*Credits@Google*

## Holi-Proof Your Kitchen.

Don't forget your kitchen when choosing your Holi decor ideas. An attractive rangoli at its entrance will change its entire ambiance in a flash. Add colorful paper motifs to the lighting fixtures in your kitchen to create ambient lighting. Bright pendant lights can also dress up the kitchen ceiling.

![](/images/uploads/2022/03/24.CUTE_COLORFUL_RANGOLI_uwvirz.jpg)
*Credits@Google*

## Terraces And Lawns.

Since Holi is an outdoor festival, it is usually held on the lawn, patio, balcony, or terrace. Therefore, use these places at their best to play Holi. While flowers are one of the best ways to decorate spaces, colorful buntings and festoons can also help. A floral rangoli will welcome the delights of the festival to your patio.

![](/images/uploads/2022/03/2-1560496910_crop.jpg)
*Credits@Google*

Your home is now all set for Holi 2022. Holi, in particular, is a festival of vibrancy and liveliness, so why not go all out on it?
