Winter and fashion have always been a deadly combination. People tend to believe the winter is the enemy of fashion as baggy and heavy winter wear makes one look unattractive or uninteresting. But the fact is you have to know the right place to insert the creative key to open the magical box of fashion. It all starts with the choice of material to, the choice of colors.

**The essence of winter fashion**

Mostly we prefer to keep all of our attention to the clothing, to display ourselves fashionably. We ignore the pieces of fashion which enhance our beauty. That includes [accessories](https://sites.google.com/site/importanceofaccessories/ "https://sites.google.com/site/importanceofaccessories/"), bags, and footwear.

Accessories can make your look more attractive and catchy, the bag can add a twist to your attire, and footwear can always steal the show.

**The right choice of footwear for winters**

Classic footwear for winters are sports shoes and caulk [boots](https://editorialist.com/style/best-boots "https://editorialist.com/style/best-boots"). But pairing winter clothes with chic boots is the best decision one can make to bless their Christmas and New Year outfits. The winter clothes are oversized and complex so, why not just pair them with mind-blowing boots that complement the attire. Winter is the right time to buckle up your fashion sense as you require to mix & match the shades of clothing with your footwear and accessories.

**The concept of styling footwear with outfits**

Pairing poncho with over-the-knee boots and Bomber jackets with Spike-Toe Boots and metallic jewelry can make you stand out. Standing out requires a pinch of twist and perception to look beyond the basic idea of fashion. To style an [appropriate](/stories/which-are-the-most-suitable-heels-to-wear-to-the-office "/stories/which-are-the-most-suitable-heels-to-wear-to-the-office") dress with suitable footwear, you don’t have to follow the trend but, you have to follow the notion of showcasing your personality through your outfit.

- If you have a dramatic [personality](https://www.godrejgenteel.com/how-clothes-can-impact-your-personality#:~:text=Wearing%20the%20right%20kind%20of,very%20significant%20part%20in%20it. "https://www.godrejgenteel.com/how-clothes-can-impact-your-personality#:~:text=Wearing%20the%20right%20kind%20of,very%20significant%20part%20in%20it."), go with high heels boots with a Pea Coat.

- You can also play with color combinations. You can pick yellow high heels boots to pair with bottle green and royal blue Pea Coat.
- If you wish to keep it formal, you can wear a Coatigan dress with Wedge Boots.
- Dolce & Gabbana Dress with Booties are the perfect combination if you want to keep it a little sassy.

**Must-haves for your boots collection**

*1) Ankle Boots/Booties:* Booties are one of a kind. There is no replacement for them. You can pair them with plain royal colors, animal print outfits, and you can pair them with oversized clothes or with literally anything. And they will compliment every attire differently.

*2) [High-Heel](/stories/high-heels-obsession-and-how-to-find-your-perfect-pair "/stories/high-heels-obsession-and-how-to-find-your-perfect-pair") Boots:* Sassy High-Heel Boots are the best to wear in the cozy winter. These give you the much-needed bossy look.

*3) Over-The-Knee Boots:* Never ending obsession with Over-The-Knee Boots is unquestionable as the trend and love for them can never cease. One can pair them with anything to make the outfit look more authentic.

*4) Snow Boots:* Snow Boots not just look classy but also protect you from cold, windy weather as well as they can be the most comfortable thing you own.

*5) Spike-Toe Boots:* The return of the trend of Spike-Toe Boots was much needed. It’s not easy to carry them but provides your attire a mesmerizing touch.

*6) Chunky Boots:*Chunky Boots give a different sort of retro yet funky appearance. You can pair it with summer, rainy, and also with winter outfits.

The most popular boots of 2021 are Spike-Toe Boots, Chunky Boots, and Cowboy Boots.

**How to style an outfit in winter**

Never ignore the material of your attire. Material can complement the look in both ways, negative and positive. It is necessary to check the quality of the supply of your outfits, especially when you are going to a party.

Choose colors that compliment the occasion and your mood. You can always play with color combinations to find a perfect outfit.

- You can use oversized Jackets or a cardigan and pair them with Combat Boots for a dramatic touch.
- Scarfs are the essentials of winter outfits to compliment your outfit with boots.
- You can color coordinate the Trench Coat or Winter Blazer or any upper wear with Moto Boots and wear a solid color base.
- Always pair ankle-length boots with a dress and long boots with jeans and uppers.
- [Accessories](https://sites.google.com/site/importanceofaccessories/ "https://sites.google.com/site/importanceofaccessories/") are significant to magnify the outfit. A choker necklace and bold earrings are the best choices to wear with winter wear.
