## GOOD TO ME FLORAL BRALETTE

A triangular bralette silhouette, a cropped cut, and a tie-back design are all hallmarks of the AKIRA Label [Good To Me Floral Bralette](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/good-to-me-floral-bralette). It features a floral print satin fabrication, adjustable cross-back spaghetti straps, and plunging necklines.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 100% Polyester
- Care: Hand Wash Cold
- Hem to shoulder: 15"
- Size: Small

![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/g/o/good-to-me-floral-bralette_pink_2_2.jpg)
*Credits@Akira*

![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/g/o/good-to-me-floral-bralette_pink_6_6.jpg)

**Observations:**

It's a very [breathable fabric](/stories/pretty-girl-look-review), and it fits nicely. The adjustable straps do not dig into the skin or cause discomfort. Make your outfit complete by pairing this piece with the matching AKIRA Label Good To Me Wide Leg Palazzo Pant!

## GOOD TO ME WIDE LEG PALAZZO PANT

In the AKIRA Label [Good To Me Wide Leg Palazzo Pant](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/good-to-me-wide-leg-palazzo-pant), you'll find a floral print satin fabrication, pleated front design, on-seam pockets, and a flowy broad leg shape. Shirred elastic back waistbands and concealed side zippers complete these pants.

**Features:**

- Material: 100% Polyester
- Care: Hand Wash Cold
- Stretchy
- Rise: 14"
- Length: 43"
- Size: Small
