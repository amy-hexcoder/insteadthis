QUESTION

Hi Kiara,

A close friend of mine has a wedding coming up that she's going to be tying the knot with, so I was wondering if you could share some pros and cons about gay marriage.

Love,

Mishal!

ANSWER

Hi Mishal,

Gay marriage has been a hot-button issue for years. Those who support gay marriage contend that it is a basic human right that everyone should be able to love and marry whomever they wish. In contrast, opponents of gay marriage say it goes against traditional values and harms society. Here's a look at both the goods and bads of gay marriage.

## Goods of Gay Marriage:

**1. Equality:** Gay marriage is a step towards equal rights for everyone, regardless of their sexual orientation. This would demonstrate a trend towards a more inclusive and accepting society.

**2. Legal Benefits:** Many legal benefits are associated with marriage, such as tax breaks, inheritance rights, and hospital visitation rights. Gay couples would benefit from these benefits and protections.

**3. Bringing happiness:** Gay marriage will bring happiness to many couples who have waited for this right for so long. They would be able to publicly express their love and commitment to one another.

![](https://images.csmonitor.com/csm/2013/06/0627-DOMA-REACTION-sized.jpg?alias=standard_900x600)

## Bads of Gay Marriage:

**1. Religious Beliefs:** Many religions believe that marriage should only be between men and women. These religious beliefs may be at odds with allowing gay marriage.

**2. Family Values:** Those opposed to gay marriage argue that it violates traditional family values and could harm children raised by a same-sex couple.

**3. Slippery Slope:** Many people are concerned that allowing gay marriage will lead to further changes in traditional marriage laws, possibly including polygamy legalization.

To conclude, the gay marriage debate is complex and multifaceted. Despite both good and bad sides to gay marriage, society is ultimately responsible for deciding what is best for all.

Love,

Kiara!
