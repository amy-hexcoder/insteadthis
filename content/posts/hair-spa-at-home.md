Self-care has emerged in a new shape as a result of the pandemic forcing us to reconsider and reassess our lives. The majority of us associate the word "spa" with feelings of intense relaxation, pampering, and de-stressing. While spa rituals have been practiced for centuries by Native Americans, Greeks, Romans, and others, they are a wonderful self-care ritual that allows us to take a break from the hustle and bustle of daily life while also deeply nourishing and treating ourselves in the privacy of our own homes. While the majority of us focus on our skin, nails, and bodies, we usually forget to take good care of our hair. A home hair salon is the answer.

**HAIR SPA AT HOME:**

A hair spa treatment also referred to as "hair rebirth therapy," is a great way to hydrate, condition, and undo the harm that pollution, heat-related treatments, hair color, and other factors have caused. Unfortunately, following the same shampoo and conditioner process every time won't always be sufficient to replenish the moisture that has been lost from your hair and meet all of its needs. The ideal indulgence to improve the health of your hair and elevate your weekly oil "[champi"](https://iamsahararose.com/blog/what-is-a-champi/) regimen is a home hair spa. A few easy measures will revitalize, soften, and revive the brilliance of your hair!

![](/images/uploads/2023/08/h1.jpg)

**WHAT ADVANTAGES COME WITH A HOME HAIR SPA?**

This soothing yet efficient hair therapy has many advantages, including the following:

**1) OFFERS DEEPER-ROOTED NUTRITION AND CONDITIONS**

Making your follicles stronger and nourishing down into the roots of your hair with a home spa treatment is a terrific approach to conditioning your hair if it has been feeling dry or scratchy. This guarantees healthy hair growth and thicker hair in addition to hydrating the scalp and hair shaft.

**2) REMOVES DANDRUFF AND IMPURITIES**

Impurities can accumulate in the follicles and cuticles of your hair as a result of daily exposure to the effects of pollution, dust, heat, and a variety of other factors. The pores are cleaned, these pollutants are removed, and dandruff buildup is eliminated with the help of a hair spa.

**3)Improves scalp health and delays the aging of the scalp.**

Your hair's scalp resembles the roots of a plant. Maintaining health can assist fight against a variety of issues, including inevitable aging issues like greying and hair loss. A hair spa revitalizes your hair by providing it with vital nutrients that maintain it vibrant, young, and healthy.

**4) Reduction of dullness and frizz**

A hair spa is an effective way to eliminate frizz, dryness, and brittleness if your hair appears lifeless and dull. It enhances the hair's quality and texture and makes it simpler to handle.

**5) A VERY CALMING AND RESTORATIVE RITUAL**

Any routine that relieves tension, like a home hair spa, may be quite peaceful and a terrific way to unwind. Even though we frequently become overwhelmed by nonstop activity, taking the time to focus on and take care of ourselves is crucial to maintaining both our physical and mental health.

**6) STRONGENS OIL SECRETION**

Pollution, perspiration, and greasy hair products like styling sprays might interfere with your scalp's normal oil production. A hair spa ensures that the hair does not readily get sticky or greasy by regulating the degree of oil production and soothing the sebaceous glands.

**WHERE DO I START WITH A HOME HAIR SPA?**

A home hair spa involves these six steps:

**1) AN OIL RUB**

To nourish your hair, promote blood flow, and enhance circulation in your scalp, start with a basic oil massage. You may always slightly warm the oil to give yourself a "hot oil massage" if you want. Warm oil should be taken in little amounts, about 1-2 tablespoons, and carefully massaged from root to tip.

Your choice of oil is inevitably influenced by your hair type and any issues you may be having with your hair. Choosing [cold-pressed](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/blogs/voices/mustard-oil-a-champion-of-health-with-plenty-of-nutritional-benefits/) oils is highly advised because they preserve vital antioxidants, vitamins, and minerals that are otherwise lost during extraction.

**2) STEAMING HAIR/HOT TOWEL**

The pores on your scalp will open up during this process, allowing the oil to be absorbed as fully as possible while ensuring that the nutrients from the oil get deep into the cuticles. You can either use a steamer and steam your hair for 10 to 15 minutes, or you can wrap your head in a heated towel. Simply soak a soft, dry cloth in hot water, squeeze out the excess, and repeat as necessary. If you have extremely dry hair, extend the time to 15-20 minutes. Leave this on for 5-10 minutes.

**3) SHAMPOO WITHOUT SULPHATE**

After that, wash your hair and rinse the oil off. To do this, it is strongly advised to use a Sulphate-free shampoo because they effectively cleanse your hair from the inside out without robbing it of its natural moisture. Avoid shampoos that contain SLS, SLES, Sodium Lauryl Sulphate, or Sodium Laureth Sulphate while shopping for one. When it comes to clean shampoos, less is more, so just rinse and repeat after dilution with water. A clean shampoo may not make as much foam, but it still thoroughly cleans your hair and maintains hydration.

**4) HAIR CREAM OR A HAIR MASK**

This is the most crucial stage of your hair spa treatment and entails using a hair mask or cream to hydrate both the scalp and hair, as well as to lessen dryness and flakiness. Because it contains a concentrated amount of active chemicals that are particularly useful after prolonged contact and allow the nutrients to seep in and work their magic, a hair mask is frequently referred to as an "intensive conditioner" or "deep conditioning treatment." Always look for masks that have substances that are highly conditioning, such as honey, bananas, coconut oil, aloe vera, olive oil, etc. For optimal effects, leave the mask on for anywhere between 20 and 30 minutes before rinsing it off with lukewarm or cold water.

**5) CONDITIONING:**

After that, continue with your usual conditioning routine. Use a coin-sized amount of conditioner and work it through your hair from the centre to the tips. Never apply it close to the scalp since it can easily become greasy and because the hair there is generally healthier and newer, necessitating less maintenance than the tips. Allow it to permeate completely for a minute, then rinse it off.

**6) SERUM**

The application of serum is the final but crucial step. This assists in detangling, controlling frizz, and smoothing out the hair. After taking one or two pumps, apply the serum to the center and ends of the face. Be cautious and watch out not to overdo it since this can rapidly make your hair sticky and oily!

**HOW OFTEN SHOULD A HOME HAIR SPA BE DONE?**

To give your hair the proper nutrients and care and to see noticeable effects in terms of hair texture, quality, and growth, it is ideal to treat yourself at least twice a month or once every 15 days, at a home hair spa.

So if you want to see noticeable, tangible effects over time, put your time and effort into the proper hair care. Make it a regular ritual that you participate in, and you'll not only start to like it but also look forward to engaging in it as a truly calming ritual.
