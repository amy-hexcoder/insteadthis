**How to find your perfect pair of Heels**

People often consider high heels to be a pain for the legs and entire body but, the fact is it’s all about habit and the right pair. It’s not easy to [walk](https://www.modelfactory.in/blog/64/modelling-tips-ramp-walk-tips-for-indian-models "https://www.modelfactory.in/blog/64/modelling-tips-ramp-walk-tips-for-indian-models") in high heels for hours but you get used to it. And it eventually stops bothering you. All you have to do is find the ideal pair of high heels for yourself.  How easy it will be for you to walk in heels depends upon the size and thickness of the heels. Smaller or bigger size from your fit can affect your walk and also can make you fall.

It doesn’t matter if you are buying budget-friendly heels or an expensive one, you should buy the pair which makes you feel comfortable when you wear them. Walking around a few times in heels before buying it will give you a rough idea if you’ll be able to walk around confidently in those heels or not.
