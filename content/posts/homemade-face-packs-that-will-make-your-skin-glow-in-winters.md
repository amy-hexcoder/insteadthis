Winter is here! The sheer joy of walking around in the warm winter sun gives great memories in this season. Also, one of the reasons why winters are special is because there are plenty of traditional treats to be enjoyed, and you can plan many outdoor picnics too. That said, perhaps the most common issue in this season is dry and patchy skin. And you definitely cannot follow the same summer skincare routine, which will only make your skin feel worse. So, ditch all your summer skin routines and try the exotic homemade skincare packs. These natural homemade packs will help you get [glowing skin](https://www.artofliving.org/in-en/meditation/meditation-for-you/tips-for-skin-glow?gclid=Cj0KCQiA8vSOBhCkARIsAGdp6RQ7mhnX6nxDR6LXC0vzpyFDaQtYX--LypjA0H-2tUtg46hVEfnlpjgaAvU4EALw_wcB) this season. So, here are some natural face-packs that you should try this season to look radiant.

- **Milk cream and honey face-pack:**

Malai or cream is one of the best natural [moisturizing creams](https://www.dermstore.com/blog/your-burning-questions-about-moisturizers-answered-by-a-dermatologist/) that you can apply on your skin to make it soft. Use honey to clear your skin off the bacteria responsible for the growth of acne and pimples on your face. Simply, take a tablespoon of milk cream and honey in a bowl and mix them gently. Then apply the mixture on your skin and face and leave it for 15 minutes before rinsing it off with lukewarm water. Post this, just pat your skin and see the result your skin will start breathing. Follow this regime regularly.

- **Olive oil and cocoa butter face-pack:**

You can nourish your skin with the goodness of olive oil and cocoa butter. It is indeed one of the best face packs in winter. You can also use a dash of ginger paste to remove the excess dirt off the skin. Just mix all this and apply on your skin, and leave it for 15-20 minutes. Then wash this face-pack.

- **Milk and banana face-pack:**

It is the best combination to eliminate your dry and patchy skin. In this case, you can hydrate it with a combination of milk and banana. Banana helps in peeling off dead skin, and milk helps in giving you a glow. So, just take one whole mashed banana in a bowl and mix it with one tablespoon of milk.  Apply this paste on your face, and leave it for 20 minutes and then wash it off in [lukewarm water](https://www.healthline.com/health/benefits-of-drinking-hot-water).

- **Sesame oil and aloe vera gel:**

For people who complain about patchy skin, this remedy works the best. This face pack ensures hydration. All you can do is mix 8-10 drops of almond oil and mix one tablespoon of aloe vera gel in it. Apply this on your palm, and rub the mixture on your face in circular motions for at least 15 minutes. Wash the face the next day, and you will find your face lively.

- **Papaya and milk combination:**

So, papaya is a power-packed ingredient for your skin. It has essential nutrients and minerals which will make your skin soft and shiny. Also, milk contains vitamin E, which adds moisture to your skin. So, just take half-ripe papaya and cut it into pieces and mix raw milk in it. And then, apply this paste on your face and leave it till it becomes dry. Then wash your face with lukewarm water. This face pack will bring shine to your face.

- **Honey and Carrot face-pack:**

It is one of the most used face-packs. It removes dead skin. All you have to do is take one peeled carrot and mix honey in it. Apply this face pack for 15 minutes and rinse it off with water.
