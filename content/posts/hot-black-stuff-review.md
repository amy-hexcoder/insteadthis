## HOT STUFF STILETTO SOCK BOOTIE IN BLACK

Put your best foot forward with [The Hot Stuff Stiletto Sock Bootie in Black](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/hugo-stiletto-sock-bootie-in-black-hugo34blackpu)! With its smooth vegan leather and stiletto heel, this ankle bootie is sure to be a definite [head-turner](/stories/bomber-for-you-your-pooch-review). Featuring a slip-on sock with a side ankle zipper closure, this bootie is cut with a square toe, strappy uppers, a toe band, cushioned insoles, and a square toe silhouette.
