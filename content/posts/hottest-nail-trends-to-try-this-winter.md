What is the best nail design for winter? Keeping up with fashion trends is hard, especially when there are so many photos on Pinterest to examine, fashion blogs to follow, and influencers to follow.

Due to the coronavirus pandemic and the requirement that everyone wears masks all of the time, it has been hard to show off our style this year.

However, adding a fierce eye look and a captivating nail design can rock your outfit anytime.

If you're looking to add a little something to your outfit, then a manicure is a perfect choice. Even though ballet slippers pink and classic red is always in style, why not try something unique for the next time you get your nails done? This season's most stylish nail trends offer something for everyone - a life without monotonous nails is too short.

A lot of questions, a lot of answers. Here are the best nail designs for winter.

## "Cuticle Moon" Design Is Simple But Unique:

The winter season calls for an upgrade for your nails. The only thing you need to do is create an arch by complying with your cuticle bed or moving away from it and then fill in the gaps accordingly. Once you have done so, paint your nails with the color of your choice. You can do something different with this design all year long. **Plus, the color combinations are endless, which means you can change it up every season.**

To achieve the desired look, use a fine, long-haired brush. Top it all off with a topcoat. The possibilities are endless.

## French Manicure In Winter As Well:

**French manicures remain popular because they're timeless, professional, and easy to maintain.** If you're looking to update your nail design for the winter, **try the new twist on the French tip: velvet nails.** Yes, you are right.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/09/H1d44216245f840f2bd0bc6f22d3624bdF.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

The theme of velvet has inspired several people to paint their nail tips with glittering polishes to create texture and movement. While others have added design elements to the edges of their nails (retaining the velvet/glitter look) to create a dramatic look.

With this year's nail designs, even if you are looking for something professional but still fun, this is the look for you. Don't settle for the square French tip anymore - we've gone beyond that.

## Gel Nail Extensions Are Perfect Dupe:

Gel nail extensions involve applying hard gel to a natural nail and curing it with UV light. There are many shapes and sizes to choose from, including stilettos, ovals, coffins, and squares. For a gel extension, your nails need to be a little long, not a lot, just enough to cover the surface.

In this procedure, the expert uses a form underneath the natural nail to create lengths one by one by adding a gel builder, a gel primer, and a gel top coat, letting them dry under an LED or UV light. **Since they do not contain harsh chemicals such as methyl methacrylate or toluene, they are considered healthier than acrylics.** In addition to being safer than acrylic nails, they have light-weighted nail tips, are faster and easier to remove.

## Press-On Nails And Nail Stickers Are Taking Over:

Incorporate bold nail designs using press-on. It is not the kind of press-on nail you got in middle school and hid from your mom. Almost identical to designer press-on nails, they are an excellent alternative for fastening your accessories. Consequently, press-on fingernails come in numerous colors and designs, such as red and orange swirls on yellow backgrounds.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/09/20087d78e0357a447bb6c270722b19da-768x1024.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

It sounds exciting to have a collection of reusable nails that match different moods and color schemes.

**Every one of us has tried dreaming up bold and intricate nail designs on our own, but as soon as we switch from our dominant hand to our non-dominant hand, all hell breaks loose.** Nail polish goes everywhere, so Q-tips and remover must be on hand. Thankfully, nail stickers will be a trend for the winter. They are a much safer alternative to acrylic nails, as they can be brittle.

## Jewel Accents Are In Hype:

Those who enjoy elegant nail designs must have had a hard time last year. **As bold as they may be, the bravest of the brave are opting for jewel-encrusted nail designs, and though they may be extravagant, they are stunning.**

This winter, trends will likely continue, perhaps adding even more elaborate details. There has been an enormous rise in jewel-encrusted manicures, and animal prints are going to make a return. You can choose from 3D textured prints to high gloss and matte combinations.

When you get back in the chair, be ready to show your photos to the nail artists, and prepare yourself to wait for the result. This look won't go wrong, though.

## Bold Designs And Vibrant Colors On Every Nail:

This year's nail design calls for taking risks. **The idea behind Indie nails is to give each fingernail a unique design - so whether you want to use different shades of retro green, or you prefer to go for a more exciting look and give each nail a completely different image, this is the look for you.** Indie nails give you the ability to make your design from whatever you want. No matter what your aesthetic preference is -- swirls and checkers, avocados next to a smiley face, or something in between -- you'll find it here.
