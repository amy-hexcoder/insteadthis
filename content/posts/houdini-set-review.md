## HOUDINI EMBELLISHED DENIM VEST

The [AKIRA Label Houdini Embellished Denim Vest](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/houdini-embellished-denim-vest) is a glam rocking layering piece. It has a washed denim fabrication, a pointed collar neckline, a sleeveless design, rhinestone chain link embellishments on the front, button front chest pockets, and a cropped design. Safety pin motifs, draped chain link accents, a studded shoulder, and a button-down front finish are all featured on this shirt.

![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/h/o/houdini-embellished-denim-vest_light-blue-denim_8_8_c1_c1.jpg)
![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/h/o/houdini-embellished-denim-vest_light-blue-denim_1_1_c1_c1_c1.jpg)

**Features:**

- Fabric: 100% Cotton
- Care: Machine wash cold.
- Shoulder to hem: 12" (approximately)
- Size: Small

**Thoughts:**

Its embellished design adds a touch of femininity to the piece. The fabric is lightweight and comfortable, perfect for the spring. Also, it's super versatile, so you can wear it with just about anything. Dress it up with a skirt and heels, or pair it with a tee and jeans.
