Black Girls QUESTION

Hi Petrica,

I have a dark skin tone. Please let me know how I can make my skin look beautiful when I wear makeup without making it look too heavy.

Love,

Mahire!

ANSWER

Hi Mahire,

To highlight black girl's unique features and celebrate their beauty, here are some helpful [tips and techniques](/stories/beauty-hacks-to-up-your-makeup-game-in-a-flash).

**1. Embrace Your Skin Tone:**

Black girls have a wide range of beautiful skin tones, from deep ebony to rich caramel. Make sure your foundation and concealer match your skin tone perfectly, so your base looks seamless and natural. A standout makeup look starts with emphasizing and enhancing your natural beauty.

**2. Highlight Your Eyes:**

Choose eyeshadow shades that complement your eyes' color, such as warm bronze or golden hues for brown eyes, or vibrant purples and blues for dark eyes. Play with different textures and finishes to make your eye makeup stand out.

**3. Define Your Brows:**

Well-defined eyebrows frame the face and instantly enhance your makeup look. Fill in any sparse areas and create a defined shape using a pencil or powder that matches your hair color. For a polished and ethereal look, consider seeking professional help to find the best eyebrow shape to fit your face structure.

![](https://i.pinimg.com/736x/c8/b7/33/c8b733a8d2e078e474136487c73519be.jpg)

**4. Experiment with Lip Colors:**

Bold, vibrant shades such as deep reds, plum, fuchsia, or even unconventional colors like blue or purple can make a statement and draw attention to your lips. Experiment with different finishes, such as matte or glossy, to find the perfect combination that suits your style and enhances your makeup look.

**5. Glow with Highlighter:**

For a radiant effect, use a highlighter on your cheekbones, brow bone, and cupid's bow. Choose highlighters that have warm undertones that complement your skin tone. This subtle shimmer will highlight your makeup and give you a youthful appearance.

The [beauty of black girls](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vNOZCe1Qon8) can be enhanced and celebrated through makeup. You can create stunning makeup looks by embracing your skin tone, highlighting your eyes, defining your brows, experimenting with lip colors, and adding a hint of highlighter. It is important to express yourself through your makeup and feel confident in your own skin.

Love,

Petrica!
