Hi Andrew!

Thank you for asking this question. I understand your concern. So, I would only say that my recommendation is to give her some alone time till she calms down. But don't forget to say "Hey!" to her. I am aware of your frustration. I can understand if you don't want to discuss anything right now, but once you've calmed down and are ready, don't hold back even slightly. I cherish you.

Should be effective for the majority of girls; just keep the concern alive and avoid provoking her further with queries about it. Allow her some room.

Thank you!
