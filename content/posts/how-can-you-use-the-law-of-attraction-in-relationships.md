Hi Alina!

Thank you so much for writing to me. Yes, the law of attraction does work in relationships. Wait let me give you a checklist of things:

Imagine future romantic moments with your companion or consider bringing something new into your relationship.

Be as [optimistic](https://kidshealth.org/en/teens/optimism.html#:~:text=It%20turns%20out%20that%20an,even%20help%20people%20live%20longer.) as possible in your relationship with your partner.

I'd also like to share a story that I came across on the law of attraction boards. Try taking everyday action to show your mates how much you adore them.

My best friend had told me about The Secret a long time ago, but I didn't truly use it until my relationship with my partner ended. The first two years with my partner were fantastic, and I really liked him. We had a great deal of affection for one another, but later on, due to my pessimism and the fact that his parents didn't accept me, we started fighting. I got so depressed.

I used to watch The Secret every day, but I still had bad ideas, so I went to utilize it. I visited The Secret website in search of inspiration, and I discovered that The Secret Stories really helped me. I purchased The Power and The Magic, which also really aided me. I saw him coming back to me and apologizing, and I visualized myself being grateful every day for everything in my life and for our relationship.

After a breakup of two months, he came back to me! Thus, all those in need of motivation should simply visualize, spread love, and let go.

Thank you!

Kiara!
