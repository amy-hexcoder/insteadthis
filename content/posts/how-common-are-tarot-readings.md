While numerology and palmistry have long been favored by the general public in India. Particularly in urban areas, tarot card reading has become the new-age method of "future reading." When people called a card reader to make predictions regarding the matches during the 2003 Cricket World Cup, tarot cards captured people's attention.

**How do the tarot cards function?**

Completely of life is concealed within the tarot cards when they are completely laid out. The Major Arcana, which consists of 22 cards, and the Minor Arcana, which consists of 56 cards, make up the two "Arcanas" that make up the tarot (the word meaning "secrets").

In the Minors, the four suits are identical to those in a standard deck of cards, with the exception that each suit is linked to a distinct element and feature of the self. As for the suits:

The Cups represent the emotional self and water.

The Pentacles are connected to the physical self and the ground.

The cards of air known as the Swords are all focused on the mind.

And the Wands, the fire outfit, which speak to the use of energy.

The Major Card Trip, on the other hand, depicts tectonic changes in life and tells a dramatic tale of beginnings and endings, gains and losses, stasis and development. Tarot beginners frequently recognized Major Arcana cards such as The Hanged Man or The Lovers. And when you combine all of these various cards, you have everything you need to create a thousand different stories.

**Why Do Individuals Use Tarot Readings?**

Tarot card readings are frequently used by people who need guidance in order to navigate life's challenges. A tarot card reader will be able to provide you with advice and answers thanks to the 78 cards. Tarot card readings are popular because they provide individualised and precise information, as opposed to other kinds of fortunetelling. **How precise are tarot card interpretations?**

Tarot card readers' skills and sincerity are the only factors that affect how accurate their readings are. Unfortunately, there are a lot of market participants who pretend to be tarot card readers but aren't. The ideal course of action would be to consult a person who has a reputation for giving accurate readings and offering appropriate advice on what to do and how to proceed. Any tarot card reader is welcome to offer their services, but they must be an expert.

Understanding that [Tarot cards](https://astrotalk.com/astrology-blog/how-accurate-is-tarot-card-reading/) are a compass is essential to having the right attitude towards them. It aims to provide you with feedback on your life as it actually happens. Since there are countless factors at play, the future is dynamic and being shaped by a myriad of wheels that are all turning at once.

**Using tarot cards to tell your own stories**

Finally, but not least, understanding tarot requires you to learn how to apply your own unique experiences and perspectives to each of the cards. You'll discover that the Death card represents transition and closure - but what does that mean for you? If you draw The Hermit, consider how you've used and benefited from solitude. Over time, you'll notice the cards everywhere: in everyday life, in poems or songs, in characters from Netflix shows. Some cards may get imbued with a distinct meaning that feels unique to you. That is not incorrect. It is, in fact, the lifeblood of this old practice.

The tarot continues to exist because it is relevant to each new generation. The images on the cards are symbols, not signs: they can never be fixed, pinned down, or owned. They are large enough to hold every life, every experience, and every tale. Above all, when learning and reading the cards, don't be afraid to make them your own - and to share what you discover.
