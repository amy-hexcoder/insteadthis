Hi Dameon!

Yes, I am doing good. What about you? I love connecting with my people and that is the only reason I take the initiative to respond. Well, thank you for initiating this conversation. I wanted to take up this topic.

You know Dameon, I do not know about others, but at my wedding, I took out some money from my wedding budget and planned a free meal for beggars and orphaned children. I distributed packets of food and [donated](http://parivartanfoundationindia.com/donation-is-very-important-for-poor-people-why/) a small amount from my end. Also, my husband took the lead in reciting those vows and making others understand their meaning of it.

Also, I wanted to do something special for specially-abled children, so I contributed some blankets and school books for them. And Dameon, my husband, and my in-laws were very supportive of this. They also contributed a hefty amount to this noble cause. So, instead of spending much on the wedding, we did something special for specially-abled children.

And you know what we got as our return gifts? Their blessings. It was indeed very special.

I hope my experience helped you.

Love,

Petrica
