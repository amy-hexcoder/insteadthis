Hi Joe!

I am doing great. How are you? Well, the topic that you've extracted has always surprised me. Striking work-life balance is always an ongoing negotiation. Yes, it might make your things tricky at times. And as working mothers, we pretend to find a balance, but we don't.

My kids and I do our homework together. At times, we live in a mess of a household. And other times, we hang out.

Since I keep on indulging myself in loads of interesting opportunities, I sometimes work around the clock, but I will tell you, my kids are cool, and we do a lot of fun activities together.

So, I would say that the key is to put things in [priority](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/prioritize-tasks-in-the-workplace) perspective and do things together such as washing dishes, and clothes, making food, cleaning home, and always putting out one day so that we can hang out together.

I hope this justifies your question, Joe.

Love,

Kiara
