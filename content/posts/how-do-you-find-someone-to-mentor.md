Yes, babe, I have moved into my new home. The last time when, we called, your interior designing tips helped me a lot. So, calling you again because I am unable to finalize the color for my bedroom. Please help me in finalizing one.

Coming back to your question Jessica, you know these days, finding a mentor is a difficult task. Often I feel people have wrong expectations from the mentor. They start treating him like a business advisor. But to me, a [mentor](https://cfe.unc.edu/mentoring/benefits-of-having-a-mentor/) is more of a personal relationship and I think he should be wisely used to navigate the regular ups and downs of your life.

So keeping the above in mind, I would suggest that you should look for a mentor in the immediate circle like your family members, bosses, teachers, ex-colleagues, and peers. There are also professional contacts one can use to find a mentor. But for me, those are more of a business advisor and less of mentors. A mentor for me is someone who can maintain a sustained [relationship](/stories/is-there-any-impact-of-zodiac-signs-in-a-relationship) than making it transaction based.

I hope, you got your answer.
