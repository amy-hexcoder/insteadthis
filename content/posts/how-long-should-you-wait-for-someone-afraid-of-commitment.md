Dear Kiara,

I hope you are doing well. I love someone and we are in a relationship for two years.

Now, I want to take this [relationship](/stories/signs-of-commitment-phobic-partners) ahead, but he is afraid of committing. So, how long should you wait for someone afraid to commit?

Regards,

Jessica

![](/images/uploads/2023/03/r3.jpg)

Hi Jessica!

I am doing perfectly well babe. You know it is very difficult to answer this question without many factors like your age, his age, etc. I hope this helps you!

I know it is hard to suggest how long you should wait for a guy, but I have something important to share. There could be many reasons why one is afraid of giving that commitment. Never try to force him for it. Rather, make him comfortable by being around you. You can make him trust you slowly and steadily. He has to be ok by being vulnerable with you.

Just make him realize that you are a real catch!

But this has to happen very naturally. If you give him enough time and attention, then chances are that he might come to you.

And if you wait too long, you will know what to do and what to not!

So, all the best Jessica.

Love,

Kiara.
