QUESTION

Hi Amanda,

As the evenings approach, I feel utterly drained and detached from everyone around me. I am struck by loneliness. While I am eager to pick up a hobby during the evening hours, I am unsure which one would be the right fit for the time I have. Any suggestions?

Love,

Zack!

ANSWER

Hi Zack,

Finding new and exciting ways to connect with others has become increasingly important in a constantly evolving world. There's nothing to fear if you're lonely! Our 2024 solution will not only fill your life with lively beats but also connect you to people in the coolest way possible - DJing! The list below will help you [get started as a DJ](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/becoming-a-better-dj/):

## 1. The Beat Connection:

In DJing, it's not just about mixing tracks; it's all about connecting. Start by listening to different genres and finding beats you like. If you like thumping bass, soulful rhythms, or heart-pounding energy, find the genre that speaks to you.

## 2. Equip Yourself:

You can now start your DJ career with just a laptop and a controller thanks to advancements in technology. Buy a DJ controller, headphones, and a laptop with DJ software, and you're ready to go. It's easy to get started online, and there are dozens of tutorials to help you.

## 3. Virtual Jam Sessions:

Embrace the virtual world by participating in DJ communities and forums. Platforms like Twitch and Discord host virtual jam sessions where DJs from around the world show off their skills. Make new friends, share your mixes, and chat about beats.

## 4. Collaborative Playlists:

Share playlists with friends and fellow DJs on streaming platforms. It's not only a great way to hear a bunch of music, but it's also a great way to spark conversations about your favorite tracks. It might even become a soundtrack for countless online gatherings!

## 5. Attend DJing Workshops and Events:

Take part in workshops and events in your local community. You can enhance your skills and meet fellow DJs and music lovers. Collaborations, friendships, and unforgettable musical experiences often occur.

## 6. Social Media Engagement:

Connect with other DJs, engage with their content, and join the conversation on platforms like Instagram, Twitter, and TikTok. The online music community is vast, and there's always room for one more DJ to add their unique tunes.

Become a DJ in 2024 and say goodbye to loneliness! It's not just a hobby; it's a lifestyle that [binds people together](/stories/best-friend-bucket-list-things-to-do-on-friendship-day-with-your-bff) through music! Unleash the beats and let the fun roll! Feel free to comment and share if you liked it.

Love,

Amanda!
