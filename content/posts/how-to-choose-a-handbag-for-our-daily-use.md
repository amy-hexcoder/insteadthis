**TIP NUMBER 1:**

**Choosing between your body and handbag size:**

Always choose a handbag opposite to your physique. For example, if you are too lean and tall, choose something bulky, short, and noticeable. Don't opt for shoulder bags that would merge with your figure. And if you are stout, select a bag of aesthetic size, which can match your curves.

**TIP NUMBER 2:**

**Choosing a handbag based on your outfit:**

When you want to be fashionable, always choose a handbag of subtle color. When I am saying this, I know you are shrinking your eyebrows, but hey, never stack up handbags of different shades like your tunics. Choose subtle shades in handbags that are neutral in colors. It easily goes with your outfits, plus it gives you a classy look, making your shopping simpler and easier.

**TIP NUMBER 3:**

**THINKING OF THE OCCASION:**

So, isn't it logical to choose your handbag depending on the occasion that you have? A handbag that suits your office may not be perfect for a wedding party. You need to understand the basic difference between handbags depending on the occasion. There are so many handbags to choose from like parties, work, [outings](https://intermountainhealthcare.org/blogs/topics/live-well/2017/05/5-must-haves-for-a-successful-family-outing/), etc.

**TIP 4:**

**THE BUDGET:**

Above all, you also have to think about your budget before fetching a handbag for yourself. Go for high-priced ones only if you need one. Brands are over-priced. So, just make sure that you choose a handbag depending on the quality and value for money.

**TIP NUMBER 5:**

**OPTING FOR LEATHER BAGS:**

Make sure that when you choose a handbag, choose [leather bags](https://highonleather.com/blogs/all-about-leather-bags) too. They are good-looking and last longer. That is the only reason this fifth point is apt when you fetch a handbag for yourself. Durability, high quality, and classiness should always be looked upon when selecting a leather handbag for yourself. I hope this helps you, Rose!
