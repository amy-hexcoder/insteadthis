QUESTION

Hi Kiara,

I'm going to a Barbie-themed party with my boyfriend, and all of his friends will also be there. I wanna look my best. Do you have any ideas on how to dress like Barbie?

Love,

Phoebe!

ANSWER

Hi Phoebe,

For decades, Barbie has been a symbol of style and glamour. Its vibrant colors, trendy outfits, and impeccable accessories have inspired countless people to embrace their own style. Come, let's release your inner fashionista!

**1. Embrace Color:**

Her love of vibrant and bold colors is one of the hallmarks of her style. Achieving that Barbie-inspired look requires incorporating a wide range of colors into your wardrobe, ranging from bright pinks and blues to yellows and purples. Choose vibrant accessories, experiment with color-blocking, and mix and match different hues.

**2. Opt for Feminine Silhouettes:**

Dresses, skirts, and tops from Barbie are known for their feminine and flattering silhouettes. Add a polished and elegant look to your wardrobe with dresses, skirts, and tops that emphasize your curves. You can incorporate a-line dresses, fitted blouses, and flared skirts into your Barbie-inspired wardrobe.

**3. Pay Attention to Fabrics:**

If you want to truly emulate Barbie's style, choose fabrics that add luxury and femininity to your outfits. Choose fabrics like satin, silk, tulle, and lace to enhance your look. They feel luxurious and drape beautifully, enhancing your overall appearance.

**4. Accessories Galore:**

Add bold and eye-catching accessories to your outfits, from jewelry pieces to oversized sunglasses and handbags. It is important to embrace the philosophy of "more is more" when accessorizing.

**5. Experiment with Hairstyles:**

Try voluminous curls, sleek updos, or playful ponytails to add a touch of glam to your Barbie-inspired outfit. You don't have to go for blonde hair to capture Barbie's look.

**6. Confidence is Key:**

Barbie has always been a symbol of self-expression and breaking boundaries, so experiment with your fashion choices and have fun! Confidence is the most important accessory you can wear!

If you want to dress like Barbie, don't just copy her outfits; embrace your own style. Incorporate vibrant colors, feminine silhouettes, luxe fabrics, and bold accessories, and experiment with hairstyles to create an iconic Barbie wardrobe. You can rock any Barbie-inspired outfit with the right attitude and confidence, so let your inner fashionista shine!

Love,

Kiara!
