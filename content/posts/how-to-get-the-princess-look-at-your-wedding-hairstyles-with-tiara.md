QUESTION

Hi Kiara,

It is only three weeks until my wedding, everything is ready, but my hairstyle is still missing. Can you help me pick a few tiara hairstyles and how to do them easily?

Love,

Jasmine!

ANSWER

Hi Jasmine,

The tiara completes the overall look of any bride's wedding hairstyle. Every element of a bride's bridal look is as important as her wedding outfit and wedding jewelry, including her bridal hairstyle, which must match her taste and personality.

Every wedding season brings new trends in bridal hairstyle accessories, but tiaras are timeless pieces that never fade. To make your wedding hairstyle as gorgeous as a royal princess, we bring you some jaw-dropping wedding hairstyles with tiaras.

To achieve a princess-like look at your wedding, here are some gorgeous wedding hairstyles with a tiara:

1. Piled Up Up-Do

An ostentatious hairstyle like this is perfect for an engagement or wedding reception with a crown-like tiara. This hairstyle gives off a princessy vibe that we absolutely adore.

![](https://i.etsystatic.com/7247025/r/il/1b9889/4544647042/il_794xN.4544647042_73uw.jpg)

2. Braided Up-Do With Tiara

Like us, you will be captivated by this dainty hairstyle. Quite the ideal choice for those who don't want to deal with the hassle of maintaining an open hairstyle but still want to remain stylish, then this beautifully braided up-do is perfect for you.

3. Half Up-Do With Chunky Curls

Half-up-do hairstyles are our favorite. I love how extravagant and quirky it is with the graceful tiara making it stand out even more. You've got to love the elegance of this hairstyle, don't you?

![](https://i.pinimg.com/564x/4c/4d/47/4c4d47bdc9fd57bffbbf5defa7656042.jpg)

4. Breezy Mermaid Waves

This ethereal breezy mermaid wave look would be even more elegant with a tiara to match. We can't get enough of how simple yet gorgeous this hairstyle is.

5. Loose Low Bun

Especially when paired with a delicate tiara, a low-hanging bun is never out of style. With an off-shoulder evening gown on your reception, this hairstyle oozes sophistication and class like no other.

![](https://i.pinimg.com/564x/ae/36/07/ae3607cc9bb9ce0513400f2f9f6c9a4b.jpg)

6. Messy Side Braid

There is no way to ignore the striking similarity between this tiara hairstyle and that of Princess Jasmine. This stunning hairstyle is all about the gorgeous messiness of the braid, and we heart it. With a jeweled tiara like this one or a quirky floral tiara, OTT brides would rock it on their haldi or mehndi ceremony. Either way, we're sure it will look amazing.

7. Open Hair With Tight Curls

Adding a tiara to this open hairstyle with chunky tight curls will enhance the charm tenfold. We can't get enough of this look, how about you?

So there you have some hairstyles you can try on your wedding day to get the perfect princess look. Any outfit can be made regal with a tiara with the right hairstyle.

Love,

Kiara!
