QUESTION

Hi Kiara,

Could you tell me how to look fabulous when I am at home, or how to make it better?

Love,

Zee!

ANSWER

Hi Zee,

Even though we don't go out as much as we used to, there are still plenty of ways to look fabulous at home. No matter if you work from home, attend virtual events, or simply want to feel fabulous, these tips can help.

**1. Take care of your skin:** Every fabulous look starts with a healthy, glowing complexion. Identify your skin type and concerns and establish a skincare routine. To keep your skin hydrated, cleanse your face twice a day, exfoliate regularly, and moisturize regularly. Make sure you apply sunscreen even if you are indoors to protect your skin.

**2. Dress up:** You don't have to wear sweatpants all day just because you're at home. You can instantly feel more confident and fabulous by dressing up. Wear flowy dresses, tailored tops, or a chic jumpsuit for comfortable yet stylish clothing choices. Wear fabrics that make you feel good and complete your look with accessories.

**3. Experiment with makeup:** Try new makeup looks and techniques at home. A bold, glamorous look or a natural, no-makeup look, experimenting with different styles and products can be fun. Enhance your best features, whether it's your eyes, lips, or cheekbones. For brighter eyes, groom your eyebrows and apply mascara.

**4. Practice good posture:** Having good posture not only improves your appearance but also boosts your self-confidence. Keep your chin parallel to the ground and your shoulders aligned with your ears. Additionally, this will help prevent any potential neck or back pain while making you appear taller.

![](https://www.collegefashion.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/fashion-girl-1.webp)

**5. Maintain a healthy lifestyle:** Being fabulous goes beyond appearances; it's about feeling good from the inside out. To maintain a healthy lifestyle, eat a balanced diet, exercise regularly, and get enough sleep. For better posture and flexibility, drink plenty of water and do some gentle stretching or yoga exercises every day.

**6. Take care of your hair:** Hair can make or break your overall look. No matter what, keep it well-groomed and styled. Regularly wash and condition your hair, and use styling products that suit your hair type. Make your look stand out by experimenting with hairstyles and accessories.

**7. Practice self-care:** Looking fabulous at home also means staying mentally and emotionally healthy. Indulge in relaxing baths, meditation, or reading as self-care activities. Try DIY spa days, face masks, and body scrubs. The way you look depends on how well you take care of yourself.

It's all about feeling comfortable and confident in your own skin when looking fabulous at home. You can enhance your natural beauty by embracing your unique style, taking care of yourself, and enjoying the process. Regardless of where you are, you'll look and feel fabulous by following these tips.

Love,

Kiara!
