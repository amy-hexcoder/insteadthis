ANSWER

Hi,

That's so cool, Josie! I'm glad you're trying it! This [tattooing technique](https://www.stellakimartistry.com/post/2018/04/23/what-is-lash-enhancement) creates the illusion of darker and fuller lashes while leaving you looking natural and wide-eyed. Although it may seem uncomfortable, the results are stunning, especially for those suffering from alopecia or thin lashes. This technique involves using a high-tech tool to create lush, soft lashes. The lash line is tattooed with pigment to enhance the eyelashes. With invisible eyeliner, you can get a look of darker, fuller lashes.

## Its benefits include:

**Makes the eyes stand out:** Dark eyelashes create the illusion of thicker lashes, which is excellent for those with [light hair](/stories/laser-hair-removal-all-the-answers-you-need) since it makes their eyes pop.

**Maintainable:** Ideal for those who don't want to look overly made up or cannot apply eyeliner.

**Fits all skin colors:** Lashes enhance any skin tone or eye color.

## How to prepare for a lash enhancement?

- For a better understanding of what you can expect, look at the artists' social media profiles and ask for pictures. Also, make sure the salon is clean when choosing it.
- Your appointment day should be free of makeup, including eyeshadow and mascara. To achieve the best results, discontinue using mascara two days before your appointment to avoid sticky lashes.
- Do not use any eyelash growth serums before getting the tattoo.
- If you wear contacts, you should remove them before your appointment.

## How does a lash enhancement work?

- In permanent makeup, the device used is similar to a tattoo gun but is smaller and less powerful. With the client's consent, eyelids and lashes are wiped clean with eye wipes.
- Once it is clean, an anesthetic is applied. It is necessary to wipe off the anesthesia after 20 minutes, followed by a second wipe with wet gauze.
- Next, the artist will tattoo the lash line alternately on each eye. Vibration can irritate thin eyelids and itch them.
- Once the tattooing is complete, the eyes are wiped with eye drops, and the skin is cleaned. The procedure typically takes two hours.

## Aftercare:

Cleanse your eyelids once a day with a mild cleanser and follow your artist's instructions. Some artists recommend a healing balm during the healing process. Also, don't forget to avoid wearing any eye makeup until it has healed.
