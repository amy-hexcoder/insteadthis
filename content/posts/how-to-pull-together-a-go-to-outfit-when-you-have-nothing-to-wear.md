I am sure we’ve all experienced it – waking up on a Monday, frantically searching the closet, and suddenly realizing. "There is nothing to wear!”

In the midst of endlessly sifting through dull coats, we have lost all creativity, and being without clothing is no fun at all.

Honestly, this used to be a daily issue for me. I even skipped classes because I had [nothing to wear](https://thewelldressedlife.com/6-reasons-why-you-have-nothing-to-wear/)! Thank goodness, those days are over, and I managed to put together some go-to outfits despite that feeling.

Once we understand how to get the most out of our clothes, getting closet inspiration isn't hard.

Listen up ladies, here are a few tips on how to get your wardrobe in gear and find something to wear, or at least to give your clothes a fresh perspective:

## Biker Shorts and a Graphic Tee

You are missing out on the fun of graphic tees if they aren't a staple in your wardrobe. You can wear them with anything, including [biker shorts](https://thegoldengirlblog.com/my-favorite-bike-shorts/), not only because they are super comfortable but also because they are super versatile. You can finish the look with hoop earrings and a pretty necklace to make it seem like you put some effort into your outfit without going overboard.

## Versatile Dress along with Sandals

A wardrobe would not be complete without dresses. Despite being simple and versatile, these can be styled in countless ways! A dress and sandals are elegant enough to wear from the daytime to evening events. Minidresses are ideal for scorching hot days, while maxi dresses are suitable for formal gatherings. A perfect outfit does not require countless pieces of clothing-just a few stylish dresses.

This look is best achieved with a short bodycon dress since there is less fabric to tuck in, so lumps and wrinkles are less noticeable. Put on your sneakers, and you've got yourself a brand new outfit with an interesting twist. For a bit of cute conservative styling, layer a collared shirt underneath a plunging neckline dress.

## Adding Cardigan to Jeans

These days, [cardigans](https://www.whowhatwear.com/fall-cardigan-outfits-2018) have replaced jackets. It is no secret. And their best feature? There is not much you need to do to make them look stunning. Just grab a pair of jeans and a pair of sneakers, and you are all set. It is my reliable go-to option whenever I am unsure of what to wear.

To give the jeans a more casual and sporty look, roll out the hem of them. Simple outfits are advantageous because you can accessorize them with a lot of extra pieces. You can play around with handbags, hats, and sunglasses. Additionally, you can experiment with different makeup looks, such as wearing dark lips or no makeup at all.

## Romper & Ankle Boots

You can wear a romper in several ways to create the illusion that your outfit took a long time to put together. Even though rompers are incredibly versatile, they are suitable for casual days and other occasions that call for a more elegant look. If you're looking for a quick, one-and-done look, you can stock up on a variety of styles with varying degrees of elegance.

## Cropped Top with Jeggings

Crop tops let you mix and match with dresses, skirts, and even [jeggings](https://www.luxury-legs.com/blog/2019/08/jegging-style-guide/) if you're feeling adventurous. You can make your crop tank look Gen-Z-inspired by pairing it with an unbuttoned white shirt. For a work-friendly look, you can pair it with a backpack and loafers. Consider pulling together an easy, youthful look with high-waisted leggings and a hoodie. Jeggings are one of our favorite all-purpose outfit pieces due to their endless possibilities.

## Bold Blazers

The perfect blazer is one of the most coveted items in women's closets. It ought to be the perfect outfit to wear when you don't have anything to wear. There was a time when [blazers](https://www.glamour.com/gallery/11-ways-to-wear-a-blazer-from-our-favorite-fashion-bloggers) were worn only by office workers, but those days are gone. Nowadays, celebrities, influencers, and fashion enthusiasts wear blazers everywhere they go. You can wear blazers everywhere, from coffee runs to date nights.

To add a little high fashion to your look, dress up your blazer and pants with your favorite black heels, a simple nude shirt, and cool sunglasses. Add a few statement rings to finish it off! It's also popular right now to stack a bunch of rings. Your look is ready to sweep everyone off their feet.

## Favorite Slip Dress

You can easily dress it up or down by switching up your shoes, hairstyles, and accessories in the summertime. However, slip dresses remain popular even after summer ends. Layering is a quick and easy way to transform your favorite summer slip into a winter one.

The lightweight nature of slip dresses makes layering easy. Turtleneck sweaters look great with them. Plus, it's one of the classic pieces you can wear with chunky sweaters, with a floor-length coat, or even with your favorite long sleeves shirt.
