This is Mishelle. I was in a relationship with someone, but he was too toxic. Hence, I left him and broke that relationship as well. Now, he keeps disturbing me. So, how do I respond to my toxic ex? Can you please help me with this?

Hi Mishelle!

I know how it feels to be trapped in a relationship that's full of toxicity. So, here are the things I believe you can do to get rid of this toxicity:

1. If you don't feel like responding, just don't do it.

2. Reuniting with a toxic person is a trap if you know they are poisonous.

3. When your toxic ex keeps contacting you while you're going about your daily activities and living your best life, it's annoying.

4. Your future could be ruined if you [communicate](https://iditsharoni.com/the-importance-of-communication-in-relationships-how-to-build-stronger-connections-with-your-partner/#:~:text=Communication%20plays%20a%20central%20role,a%20greater%20amount%20of%20empathy.) with your past and let it influence your present.

5. Think about all the things that tore you apart, yet nothing is probably more important than your self-respect. Take your time responding to the circumstance. Realise your worth.

6. Nobody else is here to bring you happiness besides yourself.

7. If those close to you seem upbeat about your breakup, believe them.

This is what I believe you can do.

Good luck babe!

Lots of love!

Kiara
