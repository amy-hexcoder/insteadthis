QUESTION

Hi Kiara,

As my husband returns from his business trip after so many days, I want to make this special for him. I'm thinking about decorating my bedroom in a romantic style. Would you be able to help me set up a romantic bedroom that'll get him excited?

Love,

Gia!

ANSWER

Hi Gia,

That's awesome, Gia - you've made it! There are only a few people willing to renovate their bedrooms to create an unforgettable romantic night.

It's great that you're paying attention to these details. Let's now take a look at how we can create the [perfect bedroom decor](https://www.housebeautiful.com/room-decorating/bedrooms/g41232254/bedroom-ideas-for-couples/) to suit your needs.

## Get your room lit up with candles:

A romantic night is made even more romantic with this vintage-style room decorated with beautiful candles. Dim the other lights or maybe don't use any at all. Light up your room with candles to add a lovely touch to your decor.

Make sure you place all candles in safe areas (away from clothes and easily burning materials in your room). When you feel the need for more lighting and better visibility, you might want to consider using LED lights, as they also add a cute touch to your bedroom.

## Bring new life to your bedding:

When it comes to your bedding, you should pay a great deal of attention if you want a pleasant romantic night. A soft, smooth, and beautiful bed sheet can enhance your bedroom's romantic vibe, making your partner and you feel cozy.

Investing in a luxurious bed sheet set can be the perfect way to set the mood for a romantic evening. Choose bed sheets that are made of microfiber polyester or ultra-comfortable microfiber and opt for soothing shades to create a warm and inviting atmosphere.

## Set the volume up:

Adding music to your romantic night can make it even more memorable. Putting on the right music on the night table with just the right volume (no subwoofer necessary) can do wonders for setting the mood.

![](https://www.thebuzzqueen.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/25025181_1560179454062801_8862462765286031360_n.jpg)

## Fill your room with flowers and aromatic scents:

Romance isn't always about the bed. Make arrangements for the closet, bathtub, and sofa as well.

Flowers and aromas make an excellent choice for infusing a room with passion. With flowers, you can rekindle romance, while with the right scent, you can influence your mood.

To achieve a fulfilling love life, you must take the time to create romance in your relationship. Even if you think your ideas are silly, your partner will appreciate the [effort you put](/stories/how-to-train-yourself-to-do-one-task-at-a-time) into doing something loving for them. Keep in mind that the thought is what counts.

Love,

Kiara!
