Be it, adults or kids, everyone loves ice cream and we do indulge in it whenever we get a chance. All of us have had a sneak peek tour of the fridge night at night to have our favorite flavor of ice cream. Most people also love having ice cream while watching some Netflix series along with family and friends.

Some of the very well-known brands in the US are Blue Bunny, Haagen-Dazs, Magnum, Blue Bell, Ben & Jerry’s, etc. Just like flavors, everyone has their favorite ice-cream brands too because why would someone compromise on the quality of the product and all of us can relate to this. Here are some famous and tasty flavors that will make you drool.

**1. Cookies N’ Cream:** This is probably the most popular ice cream flavor in  America as this ice cream comes in different variations of vanilla, mint, and even chocolate and it mixes with crumbled chocolate sandwich cookies which is obviously the cherry on the cake.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/cookie-n-cream-1024x683.jpg)
*www.alphacoders.com*

**2. Buttered Pecan:** Made with lots of toasted pecans, browned butter, vanilla extract and a sweet custard base this flavor is not just famous when it comes to ice cream but also cakes and cookies are well-known by the buyers of buttered pecan.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/buttered-pecan-1024x1024.jpeg)
*Nutritionpics.blogspot.com*

Here's a home-made [recipe](https://www.google.com/url?sa=i&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.scatteredthoughtsofacraftymom.com%2Fhomemade-buttered-pecan-ice-cream-recipe%2F&psig=AOvVaw3h6CkiGo5DRRPimhkw3PGu&ust=1619627784174000&source=images&cd=vfe&ved=0CAIQjRxqFwoTCJCQwentnvACFQAAAAAdAAAAABAD) for the ice cream which you can prepare and enjoy at home.

**3. Strawberry:** People who are fond of strawberries can never miss out on this one.. it consists of freshly chopped strawberries, milk, and cream. Kids and adults both love this fruit as well as the ice cream no doubt because the flavor is delicious and melts in the mouth quickly.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/strawberry-683x1024.jpg)
*pexels.com*

**4. Cookie Dough:** The flavor of cookie dough is considered a luxurious one and just like Cookies N’ Cream this is also a well-known ice cream amongst US citizens. This ice cream is more like what dessert dreams are made of with a good amount of crunchy chocolate alongside cookie bites which can make anybody’s day.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/cookie.jpg)
*Baltana.com*

**5. Vanilla:** Vanilla can actually match anybody’s taste buds and as compared to other ice-creams this is considered to be a healthier option as it doesn’t contain any kind of artificial sugar or preservatives. The flavor is very subtle and made up of natural sugar and ingredients.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/vanilla-1024x683.jpg)
*pexels.com*

**6. Mint Chocolate Dough:** A dose of refreshing mint and a mouth full of ice cream seems the best combination for any occasion. It is also ranked as the 4th most popular ice cream in the US because those want something that has chocolate chips with the extracts of mint. It’s a go-to and different flavor from the rest.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/mint.jpg)
*Baltana.com*

Nobody must have told you about the health benefits of this ice cream but we definitely will, here is the [article](https://www.freedomrc.org/hidden-health-benefits-of-mint-chip-ice-cream/) which will make you grab another bowl of this ice cream.

**7. Pistachio:** A single serving bowl of this ice cream is a good treat for anybody and the best part of this ice cream is it doesn’t need any extra toppings or added flavor. It tastes really good as it is because of the consistency and natural nutty thing which enhances the mood immediately.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/pistachio.jpg)
*Baltana.com*

We are a big fan of [Haagen Dazs](https://www.haagendazs.us/products/ice-cream/pistachio-ice-cream) pistachio ice cream.

**8. Birthday Cake:** As the name itself suggests the flavor but let us clear it for the ones who are still confused, this is basically the flavor of a vanilla cake topped with some sweet sprinkles which gives the feeling of a cake and ice cream altogether and who wouldn’t want a scoop of such delicious dessert.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/birthday-cake-ice-cream-cones-1024x682.jpg)
*Yourhomebasedmom.com*

**9. Neapolitan**: Neapolitan ice cream features the evident blocks of strawberry, chocolate, and vanilla ice cream attached side by side in the same container with no packaging in between. If you have a sweet tooth, then Neapolitan ice cream is a must-have and the best part is you get three flavors in one ice cream.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/neapolitan-1024x640.jpg)
*Hipwallpaper.com*

We think its best consumed in [bite sized variety](https://www.target.com/p/neapolitan-ice-cream-sandwich-12ct-market-pantry-8482/-/A-79555892) found at Target.

**10. Moose Tracks:** Last but not least as this ice cream is amongst the famous ice-creams of America. It consists of chocolate fudge which is loved by all and has a taste nobody will ever forget. So indulge in this lip-smacking flavor and have the most of it. Here is the new moose tracks flavor you'll find on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/moose_tracks/status/1245537949875351555).

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/moose-tracks-1024x781.png)
*Islandfarms.com*
