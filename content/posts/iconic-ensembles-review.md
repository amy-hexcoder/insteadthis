## AZALEA WANG HOT TO TROT STILETTO SANDAL IN BLUSH

This [AZALEA WANG Hot To Trot Stiletto Sandal in Blush](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/azalea-wang-lets-go-out-stiletto-sandal-in-blush) has a satiny upper accented by sparkling rhinestones. Besides the ankle-cinching rhinestone coil, the shoes feature an open-toe design, rhinestone foot band, and cushioned vegan leather insole.

**Features:**

- Fit (approx. measured from a size 7.5):
- Toe: Open
- Heel: Stiletto
- Heel height: 4.25"
- Coil height: 6.5"

![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/a/z/azalea-wang-hot-to-trot-stiletto-sandal-in-blush_blush_5_5.jpg)
*Credits@Akira*

![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/a/z/azalea-wang-hot-to-trot-stiletto-sandal-in-blush_blush_1_1_c1.jpg)
*Credits@Akira*

**My thoughts are:**

Look at those iconic rhinestones! They're so dazzling! It fits snugly enough and won't sag when you stand. A beautiful blush [pink](/stories/lace-love-is-real-review) finish gives this heel a luxurious look. It has a very chic sole and upper combination.
