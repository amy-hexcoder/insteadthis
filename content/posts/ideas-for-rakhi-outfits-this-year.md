QUESTION

Hi Petrica,

As Raksha Bandhan draws near, it is a time when me and my siblings get together to have fun. But choosing the perfect outfit to wear is always a challenge for me. Could you please give me some ideas for Rakhi outfits this year?

Love,

Jiya!

ANSWER

Hi Jiya,

From bright and vibrant to classic and traditional, there are plenty of ways to dress up for Rakhi festival this year! Check out these outfit ideas:

1. Traditional Ethnic Wear: Nothing beats the charm of traditional ethnic wear on Rakhi. A beautiful saree or a lehenga choli can make you look absolutely stunning. Consider opting for vibrant colors like red, yellow, or pink to add a festive touch to your outfit. Pair it with some statement jewelry and you're all set to make heads turn!

2. Indo-Western Fusion: If you want to add a modern twist to your Rakhi outfit, try experimenting with Indo-western fusion wear. You can pair a traditional kurta with a trendy pair of palazzo pants or opt for a fusion dress that combines elements of Indian and western fashion. This fusion look is not only stylish but also comfortable, allowing you to enjoy the festivities to the fullest.

3. Contemporary Ethnic Wear: If you prefer a more contemporary look, opt for contemporary ethnic wear. Anarkali suits, salwar kameez, or even a stylish Indo-western gown can be a great choice. Look for outfits with unique cuts, patterns, or embellishments to stand out from the crowd. Don't forget to accessorize with statement earrings or a stylish clutch to complete your look.

![](https://www.fashna.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Raksha-Bandhan-Outfits.jpg)

4. Traditional Jewellery: No Rakhi outfit is complete without the right jewelry. Traditional jewelry pieces like jhumkas, bangles, and maang tikkas can instantly elevate your look and add a touch of elegance. Opt for gold or silver jewelry depending on your outfit and personal preference. Remember, the right accessories can make a huge difference in enhancing your overall look.

5. Comfortable Footwear: While choosing your Rakhi outfit, don't forget to pay attention to your footwear. Opt for comfortable yet stylish options like juttis, mojaris, or embellished flats. These not only complement your outfit but also allow you to be on your feet throughout the day without any discomfort.

So, dress in a way that not only makes you look good but also reflects your unique style and personality. Take advantage of these ideas and make your Rakhi a memorable one with the perfect outfit!

Love,

Petrica!
