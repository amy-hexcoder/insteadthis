Hi, Petrica!

It's been a long time since I wrote anything to you. I hope things are going fine at your end? And I hope you are enjoying your holidays. I saw a couple of your posts on Instagram, and I wonder where you get such ideas every time! For someone like me who barely posts three to four times a month, it is difficult to come up with something new every time. So Petrica, will it be possible for you to help me with some ideas for my Instagram?

Love,

Evan.

Hey Evan!

Thank you for writing to me Love. I am doing perfectly fine. I hope you are doing well?

Yes, I would love to help you with this. Since winter's all in, it won't be as hard for you to look for inspiration. There is so much going on around you during winters that you can literally pick and choose anything from your surroundings!

## Lights and Decor:

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/35-079mVo_B1UCAPKrIUWC4LCiFpBENtJN3cls9gcAdx_CfilIF-9AoqXNYzQx4D1B19zvBS4jVbaHpmoakVOBh-RenjR3hKCvusQvOwH3KCrozzDp6YGDvy0PtzR9p8TTwIYCyZ)

With all the celebrations, you have a lot of decorations with beautiful [lights](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/450219293982127798/) to get that sparkle onto your Instagram feed. If you haven't got your decorations at home yet, just take a walk down the streets of your local town or city and click some pictures of the beautiful lights. It makes for such an appropriate picture during the festive season!

## Food:

You do not always need to travel far and wide to put out some good content on your Instagram. With lights and decor, winter is also the season of yummy, comforting foods! Christmas calls for a lot of baking. Yes! There is a lot, from Christmas cookies to pies and all of these make super cute inspirations for Instagram. You could bake them at home or get a bunch from a nearby store, make some coffee, and there you go!

## Gifts:

Coming to the best part of the season- Gifts! It is that time when most of us are busy wrapping and unwrapping gifts. So, why not take a chance here to click an Insta-worthy shot?

## The Fireplace:

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/nhwQCg83Nzl8XGM8rABlgY8HgpGhCASxVUxoqmX6J-ggCpyqtwdzKjwoN6adr7ZH7ihwWVRNJOZjWicFvYhEXZl5s0FCJOJ5M2WcUmFYy7vURAxwJV8MWFs_IwqQ2eT7d7whE6Qa)

With the temperatures scaling down, the fireplace is now the favorite cozy corner of the house! You can do a lot around this place for an aesthetic Insta-worthy picture. You could prepare some hot cocoa for the frame, some books here and there, a pair or two of winter boots, and a tiny Christmas tree at the backdrop. And voila! You have yet another frame ready.

There is a lot more you can play around at your own space - Pile up some sweaters, or bring your pets to your frame! So, go ahead with these ideas and do not forget to edit them for the perfect seasonal Instagram post. I hope this helps, Evan.

Love,

Petrica.
