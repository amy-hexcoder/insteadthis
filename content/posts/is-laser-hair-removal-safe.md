**How does laser hair removal work?**

The laser hair removal technique is a medical technique to remove your body hair by using a concentrated laser to impact the hair follicles where hair originates from. Before a laser hair removal technique, the professional carrying out the process will ask you to shave your body hair. [Epilator](https://www.healthline.com/health/epilator#how-to-use) or waxing is not recommended-only razor shaving is required. It is because you want the roots of the hair to be present during this medical treatment.

**Is laser treatment safe for the public area?**

Before proceeding to get your public hair lasered off, you should know how to care down there. This treatment on the pubic area should not be a decision forced on you by anyone just because it pleases men in bed. Also, proper consultation is required, and right hygiene process needs to be followed; before seeking this treatment. Though laser hair removal is a safe process, it can inspire some side effects also. It causes [burning sensations](https://www.vims.ac.in/blog/burning-sensation-in-feet/) on the skin. It can cause hyperpigmentation that affects your skin color and develops scars too. So, in that case, visit your hair expert.

**Other things to consider before laser hair removal:**

The laser hair technique is not a one-time affair. It requires multiple sittings.

- Hair will show initial thinning.

- Skin will be sensitive after hair removal, so you should not go out in sun for at least 24 hours post this treatment.
- And last but not least, there is no shame in keeping or removing your body hair. It should always be a choice!
