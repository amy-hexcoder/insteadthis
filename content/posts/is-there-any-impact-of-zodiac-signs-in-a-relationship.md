Hi Lilly!

Glad to hear that my darling. Talking about zodiac signs, it is like adding spices to a salad, for flavor. Life shapes us all in our own ways. I think that is an added insight. But should it define compatibility? Absolutely not!

Humans are creatures of free will, and we take mutual efforts in working things out. So, we are responsible for the good and bad in our relationship. And at the end of the day, it is all about how you perceive things. As long as you go into the relationship with the right intentions and let everything be known before you get into it, what you want out of the [relationship](/stories/if-my-partner-isnt-ready-for-a-serious-relationship-should-i-wait) should not have any issues. I hope that this justifies your question.

Thank you!
