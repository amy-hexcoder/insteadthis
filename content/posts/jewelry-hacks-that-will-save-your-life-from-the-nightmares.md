Untangling layered neck-pieces is now easy: Happy news girls! [Baby powders](https://www.momspresso.com/parenting/the-mommyhood-chronicles/article/6-reasons-why-i-love-using-baby-powder-for-my-kids) are like feathers that not only smell good but also untangle the tangled jewelry. All you can do is just place your jewelry on a clean plate and add some baby powder in the knot. It will make the knot loose, and you can then detangle them with the help of a needle. You can also wash your jewelry with mild soap and then sundry it.

**Tarnish-free rings? No worries!**

Whenever we street shop for something precious, we always want that item to have a long life. But, we also hate it when those pretty pieces start to show off their true colors. So, if you want to get rid of this, we have got a simple hack for you. Keep your jewelry in clear water to prevent it from tarnishing.

**Easy homemade hacks:**

When it comes to maintaining your expensive jewelry, we have got easy homemade hacks for you.

**For your silver jewelry:**

You just need to place your silver jewelry inside a ceramic bowl and fill it with water, baking powder and a teaspoon of salt, and a dash of [white vinegar](https://moralfibres.co.uk/uses-vinegar-13-unexpected-uses/). Keep your jewelry in the mixture for 5 mins and observe the polish. Your jewelry shine will return to its place.

**For your gold jewelry:**

You just have to mix few drops of mild detergent with club soda. Then place the jewelry in the solution for 10-20 minutes. If required, scrub away the leftovers with the help of a toothbrush. It will bring back your required polish.

**Your pearls need a shampoo:**

Never soak your pearls in water. Instead, take a brush, dip it in warm water by adding some shampoo and then gently brush each pearl. You can wipe each pearl with a dampened cloth and then follow another wipe. You can then leave it to dry or can blow dry it on a cold setting.

**Sellotapes work:**

Who said sellotapes are for office use only? We have got those tricky [bracelets](https://www.voylla.com/dare-blog/5-health-benefits-wearing-copper-bracelets/) that would not clip on without help. So, there is a simple hack to it. Just stick your bracelet onto your wrist with the help of a sellotape, and suddenly clipping it on becomes easy.

**Vacuum your earring:**

Have you ever lost an earring or a ring under your bed? Well, then, pop a stocking or a nylon sock over your vacuum and fix it with a rubber band. Your jewelry will get trapped by the air but will be caught in the stocking.

**Detangle your long hair from knotting around your necklace clasp:**

So, if you have got long hair, then this is for you. Times you put on your favorite [necklace](https://www.romadesignerjewelry.com/blogs/education/8-benefits-of-wearing-silver-necklaces-with-your-outfits) and your hair gets entangled around the clasp, it becomes difficult for you to detangle it. So, to prevent this, buy a short length of plastic tube from a hardware store and cut off a small piece. Then you can slide it onto your necklace chain to fasten the clasp.

**Cleaning silver with stuff from your kitchen:**

It is easy to sparkle back your silver jewelry. Just take a clean ceramic bowl, put tin foil on the bottom. Then fill the bowl with water, add baking soda, a teaspoon of salt, and a dash of white vinegar. Now, place your silver jewelry in the mixture for five minutes, take it out and then rinse it with water.
