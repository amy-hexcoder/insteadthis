Hi Vienna!

Thank you so much girl, for writing me. Yes, I will make sure that I speak to Rosie now and then and understand her situation. But, hey, your situation is so similar to mine. Well, to answer your question bluntly, he is obviously not interested in texting you. Someone [interested](https://www.independent.co.uk/life-style/love-sex/how-tell-someone-interested-fancy-you-signs-tips-a9017151.html) would not leave you seen and then would not reply after that. It also shows that he is not putting in any effort at all.

Also, if you are the one who keeps on double texting after being left on road then I would suggest not to text him. It would make it seem as though you are the one who needs a reply. Typical boys would not like girls who are too needy, and the more you crave his attention, the less she would give it to you because that is very unattractive.

***So, should you keep trying with this person or let him go?***

Well, you can try continuing to text her but, only if after he sees your message, only if he replies to you a couple of hours later without having you double text.

But, if you have to double text him every time just to get his reply, then I would suggest that do not bother. You are just wasting your time and energy on someone who is not interested in you. Find someone willing to put in the effort to know you better and carry on those conversations with extreme delight. I hope this answer helps you make the right move and solve all your confusion. Meanwhile, take care of Rosie, and we will meet soon, over some hot donuts and a cup of coffee in New Jersey!

Love,

Kiara!
