## CHIC LIFE ALL OVER LACE BLAZER

Fashion-forward? The [AKIRA Label Chic Lace Blazer](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/all-over-lace-blazer) will do the trick. There are long sleeves, faux flap front pockets, a fold-over collar, and a double-breasted front closure on this lace piece. A satin cuff and scalloped hem complete the look.

**Features:**

- Self Lining: 55% Polyester 45% Polyurethane
- Contrast Lining: 90% Nylon, 10% Spandex
- Care: Hand Wash Cold
- Length: 28" Shoulder to Hem
- Sleeves: 26" (approx. for a small)

**My comments:**

Wow! It's a jaw-dropping blazer. It is something every girl wants in her wardrobe. The color is superb, the lace is soft yet durable, and it does not itch. Chic Life All Over Lace High Waist Wide Leg Trousers goes well with this piece.

## CHIC LIFE ALL OVER LACE HIGH WAIST WIDE LEG TROUSER

These [Chic Life All Over Lace High Waist Wide Leg Trousers](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/all-over-lace-high-waist-wide-leg-trouser) from AKIRA Label are crafted from sheer, luxe lace. There's an exposed side zipper closure, wide-leg pants with scalloped hems, and an attached inner short, giving it a killer look.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 100% Polyester
- Lining: 96% Polyester 4% Spandex
- Care: Hand Wash Cold
- Length: 44" Waist to Hem
- Rise: 15"
