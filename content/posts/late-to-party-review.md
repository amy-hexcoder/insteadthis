## LATE TO PARTY FRONT SLIT LEGGING

With a slick, stretchy fabrication and an exposed back zipper closure, the AKIRA Label [Late To Party Front Slit Legging](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/late-to-party-front-slit-legging) has a high rise waist with a wide elastic waistband, front seam detailing accentuating the legs, a sexy bodycon fit, and an exposed back zip.

**A few thoughts:**

[Owning one black](/stories/hot-black-stuff-review) legging is definitely a must. Despite its stylish appearance and comfort, wearing this legging is truly a pleasure. Due to the high waistline, they give a slimming appearance and sculpt the waistline. Fits like a dream, just the way it should.

## IT'S GETTING LATE LONG SLEEVE V NECK CHEEKY BODYSUIT

The AKIRA Label [It's Getting Late Long Sleeve V Neck Cheeky Bodysuit](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/its-getting-late-long-sleeve-vneck-cheeky-bodysuit) is a slick and stretchy bodysuit made of a soft, silky fabric that features a plunging V neckline, long blouson sleeves, feminine ruched details on the body, an inverted cheeky bottom, and snap closure.

It is not easy to find a bodysuit that is perfectly suited to your body type, lifestyle, and sense of style. But this bodysuit is simply stunning. It is a well-made body-hugging bodysuit. It can be a wardrobe staple that fits many different needs.
