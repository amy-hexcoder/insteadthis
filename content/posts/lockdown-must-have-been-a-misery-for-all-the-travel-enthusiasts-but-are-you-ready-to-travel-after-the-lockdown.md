A nightmare none of us ever thought about, a pandemic that took over our lives. Over a year but we still find the cases of the corona, meanwhile, everyone is planning their "AFTER" lockdown trip…a lot of things have to be kept in mind looking at the current situation.

Life seems so dull without exploring places and for our travel buddies it's probably the worst phase of their lives but now that things are resuming back to normal with "PRECAUTIONS" people can plan a few trips to their favorite destinations.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/image-1.png)
*Image courtesy : pexels.com*

There’s always a risk factor included whenever you step out but let’s see what are some things you can keep in mind if you wish to travel after lockdown. Some of them are as follows :

1. Social distancing: While boarding a flight or any other public transport make sure you keep enough distance from the person standing or sitting ahead, behind/beside you. Social distancing is a lifesaver… practice it. It can help you from getting infected through someone's touch or anything else that has to do with transmitting the virus from someone else as we might not know who is infected where and when.

2. Follow good hygiene: Wash your hands before and after eating in a crowded place, keep a sanitizer handy as you might need it anytime and anywhere. Sanitizing before and after can help you know that the viruses that are present won't be going inside your body through what you are eating. Even when you reach your destination wash your hands properly so as to keep everything at bay.

3. Manage all the transactions online: Pay for the things online as much as possible so that you don't have to exchange cash and you'll save a lot of time also, you'll have a hassle-free journey with no free of getting infected and you'll be able to travel hasslefree.. Voila!

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/image-2.png)
*Image courtesy : pexels.com*

The majority number of cases are still found in the younger and older generation as they’re more prone to catching such virus/infections as their body being extra sensitive to such illnesses and more precautions are supposed to be taken in such situations. Especially the elderly people are advised to stay back home until and unless an emergency arises... also if they plan to travel anywhere their medicines and anything such as an emergency kit as to be kept handy so to avoid any problems while traveling.

Hence in such times, these age group people should take extra precautions to prevent themselves from getting infected by the deadly disease.

![](/images/uploads/2021/04/image-3.png)

Scientists, healthcare and other lab experts are still on the go to find a permanent solution for this life-threatening disease, they have succeeded to an extent but as we all know prevention is better than cure so for our safety we must follow certain measures to keep the virus away from us and our families.

Also, alongside the healthcare professionals, several others are working at the frontline for us to live happily at home and hence it should be our duty to support and appreciate their help towards us and the well-being of the country.

Let go of the stress with a few safety measures in mind and you're good to go. Tick all the things in your budget list as there is still hope and sooner or later things will fall into place and the world will again be a safer place for all of us. Until then travel but do take care of everything so that you have a happy and healthy journey.
