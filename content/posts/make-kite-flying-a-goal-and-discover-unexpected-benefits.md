Here's a short story before we start.

One day, a young boy and his father went kite flying. It took a [long time](/stories/sunshine-moment-must-see-looks-of-2021), but they finally got their kite in the air. As the kite rose higher and higher, the little boy held on to the string. A curious little boy asked, "Dad, what holds the kite in the air?" "It's the string you hold onto," his father replied. After jerking the string a couple of times, the boy smiled and said, "No, Dad, the string holds it down." "I wonder what keeps it up there?"

He replied, "The string." "If you don't believe me, let go of the string."

As soon as he let go of the string, the kite started tumbling down. Even though the kite seemed tied down, the very thing that held it down allowed it to [soar higher](/stories/yet-another-365-days-of-merriment) and higher. Like without a string, kites cannot fly very high. In our lives, plans serve as the strings that bind our goals together and help us achieve them.

## Kites: Healthy Reasons To Fly One

Taking a look at these kite flying benefits can help you make the most of your experience.

### 1. Inspires mindfulness

The term mindfulness refers to the state of being aware or conscious of something. Kite flying is an excellent activity for enhancing your awareness. As you move the kite, you must focus on its free-flowing movement.

According to scientific research, this activity can effectively [reduce pain and stress](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/minding-the-body/201104/kite-flying-health-and-happiness). By flying a kite, you expand your awareness of the world around you. Even watching a kite fly can capture your attention and keep you focused on the present.

### 2. Reconnects with nature

Thanks to mobile technology, food, clothes, books, and a whole lot more can now be made available inside our homes. However, this innovation is not without its downsides. As a result, we constantly rely on our electronic devices.

If you fly kites, you are inspired to venture outside. A bonus is that you can enjoy the beauty of the natural world.

### 3. Encourages physical activity

We cannot emphasize enough how important physical activity is to the human body. However, flying kites can help you get in shape if you are a couch potato. It may be easy, but it also demands a lot of physical exertion.

A person becomes more cautious and flexible when exposed to it. After an activity, it has been noted that most people are inclined to take a walk or play a sport.

### 4. Promotes socialization

I agree that swiping left or right is an easy way to meet people. However, in real life, such activities provide more opportunities for friendships to grow and flourish. In a group setting, kite-flying is a great way to reconnect with old friends, catch up with family, and make new friends.

### 5. Cherish the beauty around you

There is nothing more beautiful than gazing at the sky. And imagining the sky filled with hundreds of hues is spectacular. It's as beautiful as it looks in a picture, probably even more so.

Kites are mesmerizing to watch, and they make you feel tranquil. It is a magnificent chance to marvel at the sky and the colors, among many other things.

### 6. Promotes healthy eyes and neck

If you regularly hit the gym, we are sure you don't take as much care of your eyes as you do of your arm muscles. Flying a kite encourages the eye muscles and nerves to function optimally. Moreover, since the whole purpose of flying a kite is to look up, it is an excellent neck exercise. This exercise strengthens your ligaments and joints as well as your bone metabolism.

### 7. Relieves stress

The next time you're feeling stressed, don't watch a movie. Instead, watch a kite soar through the blue sky. The sight alone will rejuvenate you.

Flying kites is a great way to let the mind wander and disengage from worries. It will allow you to relax. Taking part in the activity could cheer you up and stimulate 'feel-good' hormones.

### 8. Boosts your creativity

When you fly a kite, your right brain is engaged. In turn, this teaches you how to adapt to changing winds, which makes you a more creative person.

## Words from Us

Kite-flying is more than just a simple childhood pursuit. Kiting can be a lot of fun when the skies are clear. However, when it comes to self-improvement, kite-flying becomes crucial; since it symbolizes freedom and unification.

Kites are at their most free when they are flying. Therefore, let kite flying be your response to the celebration and be your most spontaneous self. Enjoy the thrill and reap a multitude of health benefits.
