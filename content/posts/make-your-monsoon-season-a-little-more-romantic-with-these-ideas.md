Gray, rainy days make love bloom even more. As if the drizzle, rainbow, and turquoise sunlight are whispering in your ear to love and be loved. Rainy days are the perfect time to rediscover romance in a relationship. Being more romantic and bringing in sensual romantic elements will make your loved one feel extra special. Unfortunately, a rainy day can quickly turn into a day for snuggling under a blanket. So how can you make the most of this romantic season? Here are [some ways](https://www.brides.com/rainy-day-date-ideas-4777268) you can spice up this monsoon with your love to make it even more interesting:

## Candlelight Dinner on the Balcony

The balcony is an ideal spot for a romantic dinner by candlelight. Just a cozy couch, a delicious meal, wine, or whatever you prefer will do the trick. Moreover, you can enhance your date experience by decorating your balcony with flowers, candles, and green plants.

## Have A Rainy Photoshoot

Taking photos with your partner while it's raining is fun. The best way to capture rain moments is to just head out on the road, find some beautiful spots, and capture them. You can also photograph your loved one drenched with rain to give them as a birthday present.

## Cozy Up to A Fireplace

A cozy fireplace during a wet day is romantic, isn't it? Get a bottle of wine, some snacks, and a fluffy blanket to enjoy a romantic rainy day date with your partner. It will revitalize your love life and add some romance that has been missing.

## Enjoy A Hot Tub Date Night

A hot tub date night with your partner is the best way to make a rainy date more romantic and relaxing. Just fill-up the bathtub with hot water, decorate the room with candles and pop the champagne cork to make this a romantic evening to remember.

## Dance 'Til You Drop

When it is raining, embrace it with your partner. Yeah, that's the perfect time to have an Ashiqui 2 rain moment with your boo. You can dance with your partner on your terrace if you are shy to do that in public.

## Netflix And Chill with Bae

Watching a new series on Netflix is a popular pastime for many, but it's also an excellent indoor date idea. Watch a new series with your date and continue to do so!

## Get Your Street Food Fix

It's hard to beat street food when it's raining outside. Monsoon is synonymous with chai & samosas. Food lovers are sure to enjoy this food date idea, so get planning.

## Walking in Rain

Have you ever strolled aimlessly with your partner in the streets for a while? Not yet? Then, it's time to take a walk with your significant other. All you need is an umbrella. The more often it rains, the closer you will get to protecting yourself from drowning.

## Mud Football

Playing sports in the rain is an unforgettable experience. A rainy day spent playing football with your soulmate brings out your inner child. Running barefoot through mud is a moment of being one with nature. It will bring you into harmony with them.

## Swim in the Pool

It sounds insane, but if you take a dip in a pool with your beau or head to a water resort with him, you'll understand. Sensual and certainly not to be missed!

## Rainy Day Getaway

Take your partner on a weekend getaway to a beautiful location where you can spend quality time together on a rainy day. Get to know each other a little better by doing things you and your partner enjoy. To make your date more memorable, you can go camping or sit by a fire or stargaze together.

So, next time you find yourself caught off guard by a heavy downpour, choose one of these rainy day date ideas for inspiration or use them for a fun and romantic connection with your partner. This monsoon, get romantic with these date ideas to celebrate love like never before. Don't forget to tell us what you plan on doing in the comment section below.
