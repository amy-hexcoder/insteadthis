There is a pH-balanced formulation free of harsh chemicals suitable for oily to sensitive skin types.

**Benefits:**

- Removes dirt and grime from the skin.
- Peels off dead skin cells.
- Shows off radiant skin.
- Removes clogged impurities.
- Softens and smoothes the skin.
- Hydrates the skin.

**Thoughts:**

In essence, using this face wash regularly is a great way to keep the skin clear. Its natural ingredients make it [compatible](/stories/real-men-cry-too) with most skin types. It contains no artificial fragrance or harmful chemicals. The cream keeps the skin hydrated for a long time and retains the natural moisture of the skin.

For complete care of your precious mane, the product contains eight essential herbs and oils. Its natural formula will keep your beard tamed and defined while imparting a soft sheen. Also, it moisturizes your [beard](/stories/ditch-the-razor-and-grin-the-november) and encourages faster growth.

**Benefits:**

- Gives beard and mustache a lustrous shine.
- Encourages beard growth.
- Prevent hair loss.

**Observations:**

The oil comes in a black bottle with a dropper cap that helps dispense the correct amount of it. It's pretty convenient to use. It offers a very soft, manageable beard without causing any side effects. As well as moisturizing the facial hair, it promotes hair growth. It helps in keeping the beard groomed and shiny.
