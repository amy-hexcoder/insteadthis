How will your [Halloween party](https://www.care.com/c/stories/3709/22-halloween-party-themes/en-gb/) be? Is it going to be creepy or mysterious? I'm sure you've already planned how to [terrorize your neighbors](/stories/trickiest-halloween-desserts-ever-sweet-and-spooky) and [play pranks](/stories/youre-sure-to-go-bonkers-with-these-maddening-pranks) on your friends. But what about Halloween costumes? Are you stuck on ideas? If so, this post is for you. It's got you covered.

It's time to move beyond the usual superhero dresses and video game characters. Get a little more daring this year by donning the scariest Halloween outfit. It's [All Hallows' Eve](https://www.bbc.co.uk/religion/religions/christianity/holydays/halloween_1.shtml), after all, so it ought to be [eerie](/stories/youre-sure-to-go-bonkers-with-these-maddening-pranks). You can do so much more if you go with spooky instead of cute or cool.

No matter if you're on a tight budget, invited to a last-minute Halloween party, or if you have tons of time and resources to prepare for the big night, these ideas will inspire you. Several options are available for [Halloween costumes](https://www.halloweencostumes.com/), so we have something for everyone. Let's celebrate Halloween like never before!

## Dress up as Squid Game

It's no coincidence that people have started planning their Halloween [costumes](https://www.amazon.com/squid-costume/s?k=squid+costume) just when this Korean thriller show hit Netflix. A brutal competition in the series pits debt-ridden individuals against one another for a chance to win millions of dollars. Among the costumes that are sure to be popular are the red onesie and mask worn by the soldiers.
