Many of us have only a vague understanding of how to maintain our health. Everyone knows the importance of exercising regularly, focusing on our mental health, and eating healthily. But did you know that your teeth are also a valuable asset? [Oral health](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/medicine-and-dentistry/oral-health#:~:text=Oral%20health%20is%20often%20a,of%20many%20chronic%20systemic%20diseases.) is often overlooked when defining healthy living.

## A healthy toothy smile is undeniable!

Teeth are essential for our everyday lives. They indeed help us digest food, verbalize properly, and sculpt our faces. Perfect teeth also contribute to a great smile. We exhibit our best selves when we have a strong sense of self-esteem, which in turn impacts our professional and personal lives. Therefore, we ought to give our oral health the best care we can afford.

## How Do Teeth Affect Your Social And Emotional Status?

A damaged smile can negatively impact a person's self-confidence, as well as reducing their self-esteem. People with stained, crooked, or missing teeth are hesitant to smile for fear of being judged.

If you meet someone, you first notice their mouth. It's unfortunate, but the first impression remains etched into a person's mind. We live in a society where being prettier is becoming a fundamental characteristic. Consequently, [people fear](https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/health/conditionsandtreatments/dental-anxiety-and-phobia) getting up and talking in public. Saggy cheeks may make them seem older than they are. People with crooked teeth have difficulty deciding whether to get braces or not due to peer pressure.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/09/iStock_000014790114_Medium-1024x682.jpg)
  *Credits@istock*

Moreover, a person's appearance significantly impacts their career prospects. In meetings or during job interviews, a person's mouth always catches the eyes of those in attendance.

According to a consumer study conducted on behalf of the [American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry](https://aacd.com/proxy/files/Publications%20and%20Resources/Can%20a%20new%20smile%20make%20you%20appear%20more%20intelligent%20article.doc), a beautiful smile can make people appear more intelligent, courageous, and successful.

A perfect smile can add fullness and support to your features. Over time, this becomes especially noticeable as your cheeks lose their elasticity and your teeth begin to recede. Most patients in their 40s and 50s complain that their smiles are fading. Teeth form the foundation of 1/3 of your face, determining the shape of your face.

Self-esteem can be affected by the quality of one's teeth, either superficially or deeply. It is important not to let other people's opinions affect your smile. You only have to identify your specific needs when it comes to oral hygiene.

## Smile-sparkling Hacks:

The last few years have seen a paradigm shift in wellness and beauty. Many people are becoming aware that their health relates to their self-confidence.

Cleanliness has become trendy lately. Fortunately, maintaining the health of our mouth isn't tough. Cleaning your teeth and flossing often takes mere minutes. You should consume [nutrient-rich foods](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/320310#:~:text=Dietary%20sources,-A%20person%20can&text=oily%20fish%20and%20fish%20oils,eggs), which means eating foods high in fat-soluble vitamins A, D, E, and K. These vitamins help you absorb nutrients needed for healthy teeth and bones.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/09/women-1632829778314-9094-1024x680.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

Additionally, it is advisable to [eat crunchy, chewy foods](https://www.prairiemalldental.ca/site/dental-blog/2019/11/18/these-crunchy-foods-are-good-for-your-teeth#:~:text=It's%20true%2C%20eating%20crunchy%20and,when%20we%20eat%20crunchy%20things.). These days, most of the food we consume is soft, sticky, and sweet. So, let your teeth and jaw work as nature intended. By doing so, you will stimulate healthy saliva, which neutralizes acids and prevents tooth decay.

Instead of soft drinks, fruit juice, and energy drinks, [drink water](https://mettlergriego.com/how-drinking-water-is-good-for-your-teeth-and-gums/#:~:text=Drinking%20water%20is%20good%20for%20your%20teeth%20because%20it%20keeps,to%20help%20protect%20your%20teeth.). Likewise, sparkling mineral water is acidic and can lead to tooth decay.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/09/Should-You-Be-Rinsing-Your-Mouth-With-Water-After-Brushing.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

It is also best to practice [nasal breathing](https://www.healthline.com/health/nose-breathing). Sleep apnea, snoring, tonsillitis, and tooth crowding occur more frequently on mouth breathing.

For extra protection, try [oil pulling](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/features/oil-pulling) sometimes. You simply need to swish coconut oil around in your mouth for 15 minutes. Oil soothes gums by removing plaque. This mouthwash is effective against bad breath, reduces gum inflammation, and helps to prevent decay.
