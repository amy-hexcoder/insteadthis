## UNSTOPPABLE TULLE BLAZER MAXI DRESS- New Year [Outfit](/stories/christmas-outfit-review)

This new year, get your dream fit in your wardrobe! With its classic blazer fabric, the [AKIRA Label Unstoppable Tulle Blazer Maxi Dress](https://www.shopakira.com/dresses/unstoppable-tulle-blazer-maxi-dress) looks effortlessly chic. This maxi dress exudes softness with its voluminous tulle hem. A collared neckline, snap buttons on the front make this a dreamy-looking dress. An exaggerated shoulder and long sleeves distinguish this dress. A matching belt will add a touch of glamour to your look.
