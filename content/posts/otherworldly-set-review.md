## FEELING AN OTHERWORLDLY PRINTED TOP AND SKIRT SET

Made from a luxe satin base fabrication, the AKIRA Label [Feeling Otherworldly Printed Top And Skirt Set](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/feeling-otherworldly-printed-top-and-skirt-set) feature a vintage scarf-inspired graphic print. A pointed collar, long sleeves with buttoned cuffs, a longline silhouette, and a button-down front distinguish this top from the rest. There is a skort-style skirt, a wrap front, self-tie ribbons at the side, and a zipper closure on the side of the skirt. Make your vibrant daytime look complete with pointed-toe pumps and a mini purse.
