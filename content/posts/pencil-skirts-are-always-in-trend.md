***"No women can ever have enough pencil skirts"…***

Well, Pencil skirts are trendy. I say they are timeless too. No matter what, they will always have our hearts. They are great for the office as well as parties or formal gatherings. For working women, they are always the #1 office-style staple. They are simply like a sartorial uniform that is never outdated. Also, it quickly polishes up a look, regardless of what you pair it with.

But how to wear pencil skirts? Due to their flattering cut, they can go on any body type. They are elongated, slim, and they follow your body in a very refined way. So, while styling them, one thing is sure, just wear heels with them because they will make you look very classy. Also, stilettos best flatter any outfit. So, go for [stilettos](https://airpufs.com/blogs/news/how-to-walk-in-high-heels-for-beginners) also because they give you an extra height. Of course, you can also wear flats with them, or also you can sport sneakers too.

**HISTORY OF PENCIL SKIRTS:**

A pencil skirt is a long evocative garment that adds admiration to your personality. Its origins are said to lie in an incident when Mrs. Edith Hart O.Berg became the first woman

to wear this fabric as a skirt. She started this as a trend for hobble skirts, and later other women also started wearing this attire. The pencil skirts entered the world in 1954 when Christian Dior’s H-line collection introduced another new shape for the modern woman. It is named after its silhouette resembled a capital 'H', complete with straight lines and a slight emphasis on the waist. It also offered something tighter and streamlined to make a woman look more appealing.

Later, Pencil skirts became a defining garment in the early 1960s. It could exude sex appeal. It was demonstrated by Sophia Loren who paired it up with strappy tops and tailored jackets. Just like hobble-skirts, these also required a very particular way of walking.

**HOW TO STYLE PENCIL SKIRTS?**

If you are thinking of pairing your pencil skirt with something different, try pairing them with crop tops, they work the best option. From fitted to loose crop tops, you can pair them with anything. You can also think about uber-trendy Deux-pieces, or try wearing a coat on your shoulders. Never pair it up with jewelry, it does not go well with pencil skirts. Also, posh pencil skirts are paired with simple t-shirts, sweaters, or [sweatshirts](https://www.russellathletic.com/blog/sweatshirt-outfit-ideas). They look effortlessly sophisticated. Try leather pencil skirts, and pair them with a bit of bling, like a statement necklace. One look which I think would be killer is pairing a pencil skirt with thigh-high boots.

**WHY DO WE LOVE WEARING PENCIL SKIRTS?**

Pencil skirts are empowering. They look amazing on all body types. They are easy to style and effortlessly bang on. We can wear them for work or cocktail parties, dates, or fancy gatherings. So, when we want to look good, we get an idea of what will look good on us.

**THE IDEA OF NOT WEARING PENCIL SKIRTS:**

You should not wear pencil skirts to bed and beaches and clubs. I understand you want to look professional and need to be taken seriously, but what about the time when you want to go clubbing? Then pencil skirts do not work.

I guess pencil skirts are sheer love. You do not need any special occasion to flaunt them. Yes, exceptions exist. But pencil skirts can be paired up with anything. It is a styling statement that gives you a sharp personality. So, wear them anywhere and where you feel!
