To smell good, a good perfume on your body is not enough. You need to understand that how perfumes work to use them properly. You can use various tricks to let your favorite fragrance last more. Also, if you are a perfume user, you can make that small bottle of scent last more than a couple of months. So, one such trick to smell good and stand out is to change your scent often. Yes, you might have that one favorite [aroma](https://organicaromas.com/blogs/aromatherapy-and-essential-oils) but wearing it every day can be boring and can make it invisible. Using the same aroma every day can make your senses accustomed to the perfume, plus others will also start ignoring you. Hence, it is essential to change your perfume daily and try something different and unique every time. So, here is how changing the perfume every day can work in your favor.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/11-1-1024x576.jpg)

- **You get noticed easily:**

When you switch the perfume, it alerts your senses to notice the fragrance more. The fragrance will be more vibrant. People around you will be able to smell the good fragrances on you. It also comes in handy especially, when you are going to a party or making your first move. You can also try to wear a different scent for work. But always make sure that you use a scent to which you are accustomed.

- **Your perfume stays fresh:**

When you wear the right perfume, it makes you more vibrant. You will feel that your perfume is more noticeable. So, here are the tips for changing your fragrances more often:

- Always wear [fragrances](https://www.catherinedenoual.com/blog/the-importance-of-fragrances-at-home/) according to your mood and occasions. It helps you attract people and makes you vibrant. Also, try to keep one perfume for every occasion- such as work, party, hangouts, etc. You can use a special fragrance for date nights and weddings too.
- Instead of spending money on ten different perfumes, try to spend money on only good-quality perfumes. Some online perfume stores supply an assortment of various scents in one box,  so you can try them and save money accordingly.
- You can change your perfumes weekly, if not daily. Think of this task as a regular grooming habit instead of a difficult onetask. We promise people will start noticing you more often.
- Try the scent on you before sporting it outside. Never wear a new perfume while stepping out. The reason being, some perfumes can give you a severe headache or will make you feel heavy. So, this trick works like magic.

**SECRETS TO MAKE YOUR PERFUME LAST LONGGGGGG:**

Applying your fragrance is the final process in your beauty routine-a dab there, a spritz here, and you are ready to go. But you would never think about how you can apply the perfume well? So, here are the secrets to make your perfume last long:

- **Never rub your perfume on your skin:**

You might have seen your friends dabbing perfumes on their wrists and rubbing them together. It is one of the first fragrance habits we have learned. But rubbing perfume into your skin causes the perfume to evaporate before settling down. It also means the notes that made you fall in love with the scent will never translate onto your skin. Also, rubbing perfume onto your skin causes a lot of friction, changing the fragrance of the perfume too. So, just apply perfumes to your [pulse points](https://kissthemoon.com/blogs/bedtime-blog-kiss-the-moon/the-inside-line-on-pulse-points).

- **You can spray it on your pulse point:**

So, what is a pulse point? These points are internally located- on your wrists, below your belly button, inner elbows, and behind your knees and lobes. These tender spots on your body emit extra body heat, which helps to diffuse a fragrance naturally. So, to apply perfume to your pulse points, dab a few of them on your pulse points, and these fragrances will linger all day.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/13.jpeg)

- **Store your perfume at a good place:**

Fragrances are pretty and precious too. So, storing your fragrance in your bathroom where steam from daily showers can interact with them is a big no. Different weather conditions can change a perfume's composition and cut the shelf life of your favorite perfume bottle. So, always store your fragrance bottles in a cool and dry place.

- **Feel free to spray on your clothes:**

You can apply a little perfume to your clothing. It is a great way to keep your fragrance going all day long. Fragrance helps in interacting with your [clothes](https://www.eurofragance.com/press-room/perfumery-blog/the-underestimated-fragrance-of-our-clothes/), so it will make you smell lighter or slightly different.
