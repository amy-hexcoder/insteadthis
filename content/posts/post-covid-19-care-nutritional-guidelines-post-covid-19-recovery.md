**DIETARY GUIDELINES THAT YOU SHOULD FOLLOW:**

- **The calorie intake:**

In Covid-19, as the body has drained a lot, it is essential to add calorie-dense foods to get the energy levels back to form. It includes whole-grain cereals such as oats, brown rice, millets, wheat, and starchy roots such as potato, yam, sweet potato, and tapioca in your meal. These foods are essential for your immune system and help in boosting your immune system. It is also important to avoid consuming foods with empty [calories:](https://www.menusano.com/blog/understanding-calories/) such as junk food.

![](/images/uploads/2021/06/2.jpg)

- **Protein consumption:**

[Proteins](https://pharmeasy.in/blog/15-protein-rich-foods-for-a-healthy-life/) are the building blocks of life that are helpful in faster Covid-19 recovery. They have the essential amino acids that guard your body against harmful pathogens. So, consume a high protein diet that will make you strong. Consume lentils, milk, legumes, soy, nuts, and seeds. You can also include animal sources like lean meat, fish, eggs, and chicken in your meal plan.

- **Fruits and Vegetables are helpful:**

When we talk about natural fresh fruits and vegetables, they are a must for your post-Covid-19 recovery. They boast a dense nutritional punch such as vitamins, dietary fiber, minerals, and antioxidants. These nutrients help to build a robust immune system and fight infections.  You should have 2 cups of fresh fruits and 2.5 cups of vegetables daily for your good health and mental well-being. Fruits like oranges, strawberries, kiwi, papaya, and guava are power-packed with vitamin C. Thus, helping in the formation of antibodies and speedy recovery from COVID-19 infection. [Green leafy vegetables,](https://www.vitacost.com/blog/green-leafy-vegetables-benefits/) gourd varieties, and carrots support to trigger the immune system.

- **Fats and oils required:**

When you recover from Covid-19, make sure that you consume unsaturated fatty acids like sunflower, soy, canola, gingelly, and corn oils. You should avoid consuming saturated fats such as processed meats, butter, cheese, fatty meat, and ghee. Also, avoid consuming all trans fats found in processed [fast foods](https://www.healthline.com/health/fast-food-effects-on-body#respiratory-system), deep-fried snacks, and bakery foods.

- **Just limit the consumption of sugar and salt:**

You should also limit the salt intake to less than 5 grams per day and use [iodized salt](https://www.ign.org/blog-salt-myths-2019.htm). Also, while cooking, just add limited; salt and avoid using high-sodium condiments. You should also avoid snacks that are high in sugar like pastries, chocolates, cookies, and cakes, instead, consume nuts and fruits.

- **Fluid intake:**

Water is one of the most quintessential elements of life. It carries nutrients in the blood, helps in regulating body temperature, and eliminates waste. Hence, experts advise you to stay hydrated at all times. You should drink at least 8-10 glasses of water daily. Also, an infection helps to dehydrate the body. So, keep yourself hydrated when you are recuperating. Just restrict too much intake of caffeine and carbonated beverages. You can consume other fluids like hydrating soups, food, and herbal teas.
