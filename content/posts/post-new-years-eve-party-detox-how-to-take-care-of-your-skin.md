Now that [January](/stories/yet-another-365-days-of-merriment) 1st has rolled around, a sour feeling has settled in. You might have binged on chocolate or overindulged in alcohol to start the new year. Don't worry, honey — it's not just called [party season](/stories/new-year-outfit-review) for nothing.
The excitement of an event, party, or gathering at the time may wear off after a while. We never want to experience a dreaded hangover again, but somehow, we always seem to bounce back. Our energy usually gets sapped after new year's eve. To help you recuperate after such a wild night, we've compiled a few skincare tips.
How about giving them a shot this weekend? **Bonus**: you can do these in any order.

# Post-New Year Skin Detox:

Congested skin is one frustrating side effect of too much fun. Breakouts on [New Year's Day](/stories/things-to-leave-behind-and-bring-with-you-in-the-new-year) can be discouraging, and they can put a damper on your self-care goals for the year. The good news is that there are a few things you can do to revive your skin after a season of excess.

## Let's Cleanse (Time to Refresh)

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/IMG_2176-1024x735.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

Have you heard of the double cleanse method? Double cleansing is the key to clearer, more flawless skin. In 2022, double cleansing will be your skin's new best friend. Using an oil-based cleanser, remove all the makeup and bad stuff lurking on the surface of your skin. Regardless of your skin type, oils can help remove impurities.
The next step is to select a cleanser that is right for your skin type, whether it's moisturizing, anti-aging, or acne-fighting. After cleansing with oil, any cleanser you use will be able to penetrate deeply into the pores and make a difference to your skin. Goodbye, dull, tired skin!

## Scrub Off the Sins

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/87545936-1024x768.webp)
  *Credits@Google*

The skin can dry out when we wear makeup for a few days (and drink at the same level). In turn, breakouts will often cause discomfort or pain due to the dryness of your skin. We're not saying you have to give up the NYE shindig next year, but it's wise to take early action to restore your skin's radiance.
A good exfoliant is essential after a night out. While chemical exfoliants are great, what you'll need is a [manual exfoliant](https://www.wanderbeauty.com/enroute/difference-between-manual-and-chemical-exfoliation#:~:text=What%20is%20manual%20exfoliation%3F,about%20a%20good%20face%20scrub.), a.k.a. a scrub. Rub the granules gently over problem areas, and rinse off for smoother, glowing skin!
You will also get the most from your skincare routine when you use scrubs enriched with key acids, such as glycolic and lactic acids.

## Mist of Solace

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/pexels-photo-6181903-1024x682.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

Give your skin some love by trying your favorite facial mist or toner. The quick and easy solution to dull skin, toners, and mists is packed with antioxidants.
They also help to keep the skin moist. In addition, toners help balance your skin, so they're essential to a detox regimen.

## Multi-Mask

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/970-multi-masking.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

It's no secret that attending events and partying your butt off can wreak havoc on the skin. Along with breakouts, you'll probably have congestion, uneven texture, bags under your eyes (ugh), and dull, tired skin. Try multi-masking instead of just slapping on a mask all over.
By applying masks with different benefits to different areas of your face, you can treat multiple concerns at once. Starting, you'll only need a clean brush and some good masks. For acne-prone zones, like the cheeks or specific spots, apply charcoal. You can moisturize dry areas with [Hyaluronic Mask](https://www.nivea.co.uk/advice/skin/hyaluronic-acid-benefits-for-skin), whereas [Oxygen Mask](https://www.healthline.com/health/oxygen-facial) will illuminate your skin.

## Boost Skin Radiance and Rejuvenation

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/pexels-photo-8062997-683x1024.webp)
  *Credits@Google*

With a serum or booster designed to brighten and rejuvenate, you'll regain your glow. Despite the vast selection, anything with a [good AHA](https://www.healthline.com/health/beauty-skin-care/alpha-hydroxy-acid) is sure to win you over.
AHA Radiance Serum is one of the most effective ways to treat pigmentation, and it accelerates the skin's cell turnover.
It is an ideal 'glow booster' since the more your skin renews itself, the brighter it appears. The kicker ingredient is vitamin C, which promotes cell turnover, resulting in a more youthful, healthier complexion. Make sure you also swipe rejuvenating serum under your eyes.

## Pumped-in Hydration

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/image_TYo4x2u-1024x683.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

A final step in your skin detox regimen is moisturizing. A necessary step in the day-to-day routine (particularly after a late night) would be hydrating the skin to make it soft and plump. Not all moisturizers are the same, so we've selected a few that are ideal for bad skin days.
Skincare products enriched with antioxidants and vitamins, such as A, C, and E, will prevent cracking, peeling, and flaking. They're the real deal, keeping skin hydrated and nourishing. Once or twice daily, smooth a little on your skin for unparalleled hydration.

I hope that this Post-NYE beauty regimen detox will help you to restore your skin's radiance. 'Tis the season for cozying up, pampering yourself, and embracing a natural, radiant appearance. I am sure your skin will thank you in 2022. It's a great way to cap off the new year.
