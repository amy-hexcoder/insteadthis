## PRETTY GIRL LOOKS HALTER MINI DRESS

This [Pretty Girl Looks Halter Mini Dress](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/pretty-girl-looks-halter-top-romper) from the AKIRA Label is a must-have for [girls who love to slay](/stories/puma-x-dua-lipa-review). With a swirl print fabrication, tie-back backless style, side cutouts, and side ruffles, you'll be the talk of the party in this romantic mini dress. It features a voluminous tulle lining, a smooth inner slip, and a side zipper closure.

![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/p/r/pretty-girl-looks-halter-mini-dress_red-multi_6_6.jpg)

![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/p/r/pretty-girl-looks-halter-mini-dress_red-multi_5_5.jpg)
*Credits@Akira*

**Features:**

- Fabric: 100% Polyester
- Lining: 96% Polyester 4% Spandex
- Care: Hand Wash Cold
- Shoulder to hem: 32"
- Size: Small

**My thoughts:**

It's undoubtedly a head-turner. There is something so pleasing about the way the dress fits every inch of the body. It makes the perfect statement look when paired with strappy sandals and a stylish mini bag.
