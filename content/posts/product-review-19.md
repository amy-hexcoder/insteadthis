**Dior**

**[Miss Dior Eau de Parfum](https://www.sephora.com/product/dior-miss-dior-eau-de-parfum-P474316?skuId=2467371&icid2=products%20grid:p474316:product)**

This amazing perfume is based on the note of Centifolia rose. It has honey and peppery notes that reigns over an extremely creamy velvety heart. It is also embroidered with fresh lily of the valley and powdery notes of iris. It has got earthy and vanilla aromas. Though this version is a little spicier, it comes with fruity aromas of apricot and peach.

So, after spraying this, its fragrance will stay on for a long time and work like magic. It is exclusive and has got an utterly feminine fragrance that females love to wear. It can be sported for all occasions and can make you assured that your perfume is the best.

**TWEEZERMAN**
