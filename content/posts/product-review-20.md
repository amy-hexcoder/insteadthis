**Fenty Beauty by Rihanna**

**[Match Stix Matte Contour Skinstick](https://www.sephora.com/product/match-stix-matte-skinstick-P18975403?skuId=1925577&icid2=products%20grid:p18975403:product)**

If you are someone who have dark under eyes, whether from lack of sleep, or genetics, then this product is meant for you!

Trust me, I absolutely swear by it. This color correct does a wonderful job of color correcting the darkness. The creamy finish of the product makes it super long-lasting for all-day wear.

It can stand alone or you can add this with a concealer. This is a life-saving product. It's a must-buy.
