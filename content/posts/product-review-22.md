**Smashbox**

**Smashbox X Becca Under Eye Brightening Corrector**

This corrector is a very popular option and for good reason. Not only is the cream corrector amalgamated with moisturizing ingredients to keep your under-eye area smooth and hydrated, but it is also waterproof, sweat proof too. Also, there are different shades you can choose from, so you should be able to find the best pick that works for your skin tone. Also, each shade has a pink or peachy base, which is ideal for concealing out the bluish-purple tone. You will look well-rested and great in mere seconds.

**Fenty Beauty by Rihanna**

**Match Stix Matte Contour Skinstick**

Are you looking for a long-lasting color corrector which would not break? Then, this is the right pick. This color corrector is specially formulated to provide natural-looking coverage for hours at a time.

It has tons of different shades so that you can easily tackle whatever type of discoloration that you are dealing with. It is very creamy that blends into the skin. But, a little amount goes a long way as it is highly pigmented.
