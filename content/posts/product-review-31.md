**Kérastase**

**[Chronologiste Hair Mask for Dull and Brittle Hair](https://www.sephora.com/product/kerastase-chronologiste-mask-dull-brittle-hair-P466881?skuId=2411205&icid2=products%20grid:p466881:product)**

This product quickly reaches cult-like status. It is more of a treatment than a mask. But its reparative effects on damaged hair work like a miracle. It repairs the broken disulfide bonds which are broken when your hair is colored or chemically treated. It leaves it stronger and healthier. Also, this works on all hair types and textures.

**Olaplex**

**[No. 8 Bond Intense Moisture Hair Mask](https://www.sephora.com/product/olaplex-no-8-bond-intense-moisture-mask-P469856?skuId=2441095&icid2=products%20grid:p469856:product)**

When it comes to its pricing, it is a bit costly. But it is a great option if you are looking to invest in a mask that can do wonders. It eliminates frizz, split ends, dryness, and damage in one wash. The extract present in this mask helps to hydrate and protect your hair. It smells amazing and is one of the best masks you can use for your hair.
