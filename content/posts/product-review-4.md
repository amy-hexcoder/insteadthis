**Too Faced**

**Better Than Sex Volumizing Mascara:**

This [Better Than Sex Volumizing Mascara](https://www.sephora.com/product/better-than-sex-mascara-P381000?skuId=1533439) comes in a black tube. It gives your eyes a good lifting effect and your eyelashes a black color. The curling effect looks quite natural and does not look too made up. Also, as the name says, this mascara gives you a volumizing effect. This mascara is very noticeable and gives you an instant glam into the eye look. This mascara is waterproof and does not come off at all with plain water. It is vegan and cruelty-free too.

**Features:**

- It creates a false lash effect.
- It gives a dramatic volume
- It gives you longer lashes

**Dior**

**Dior Addict Lip Maximizer Plumping Gloss:**

The **[Dior Addict Lip Maximizer Plumping Gloss](https://www.sephora.com/product/dior-addict-lip-maximizer-plumping-gloss-P186805?skuId=2473296)** is a quick one. It comes with large, angled applicators that soak lips in the hydrating formula in just one swipe. They give your lips a creamy smooth and instantly moisturized tint on your dry lips. They also give you a bit of volume due to their glossy finish. Overall, if you are looking for a sheer, high-shine lip gloss then try fetching this. It's affordable and can be used every day.

**Features:**

- It has a wearable shade.
- It has a formula that is smooth and even.
- It hydrates and softens the lips.
