**Supergoop!**

**[Unseen Sunscreen SPF 40 PA+++](https://www.sephora.com/product/supergoop-unseen-sunscreen-spf-40-P454380?skuId=2535367&icid2=products%20grid:p454380:product)**

This sunscreen is easy to rub in, and it is nearly invisible after application. It has SPF50 whose rating is higher than any other sunscreen. Even though SPF does not increase linearly, this is one of the best sunscreens to apply. It provides silver more protection than the lower SPF picks.

**Supergoop!**

**[Daily Dose Bioretinol + Mineral SPF 40 with Bakuchiol](https://www.sephora.com/product/supergoop-daily-dose-bioretinol-mineral-spf-40-with-bakuchiol-P504045?skuId=2637163&icid2=products%20grid:p504045:product)**

This has a nice texture to it. It smells really good but isn't overpowering. Upon application, this sunscreen does not immediately meld into the skin, it takes a little elbow grease to fully blend. And it leaves a dewy finish that most of our testers appreciated. It feels weightless on the skin.
