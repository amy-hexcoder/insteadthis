**Soft Pinch Liquid Blush by Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez**

A weightless, finely blended, long-lasting liquid blush that builds for a delicate, healthy glow. both matte and dewy finishes are available. With the help of this lightweight formula's long-lasting pigments, you may achieve the ideal pinch-perfect flush. This liquid blush, which comes in matte and dewy finishes, combines nicely to produce soft, build-able color with a second-skin-like finish.

**Product Features:**

- Comes in Liquid
- Medium to Build-able coverage
- Comes with radiant finish
