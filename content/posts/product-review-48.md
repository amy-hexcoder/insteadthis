**Intense Hydrating Mask - Moroccanoil**

Product Descrption

a high-performance, argan oil-rich mask that enhances manageability, hydration, texture, elasticity, and shine for medium to thick dry hair
The Moroccanoil Intense Hydrating Mask helps moisturise and condition medium to thick, dry hair in about five to seven minutes. The nourishing, creamy composition, which contains argan oil, linseed extract, and moisture-attracting glycerin, helps replenish moisture.

![](/images/uploads/2023/02/p412097-av-01-zoom.webp)
*Credits@Sephora*

**Product Highlights:**

- Glycerin: A natural humectant that has hydrating effects on hair.
- Linseed Extract: Made from flaxseed, which is a good source of the necessary fatty acid alpha linolenic acid, which helps to promote the health of hair.
- Argan Oil: Gives hair back its manageability, elasticity, and smoothness.

**Moroccanoil Treatment Hair Oil - Moroccanoil**

A multipurpose hair treatment with argan oil that improves shine, softness, and hydration while taming frizz and flyaways.
A flexible hair treatment and styler that works on damp or dry hair to encourage a healthier look and feel is the Moroccanoil Treatment Hair Oil. It is packaged in a bottle composed of 40% recyclable glass and scented with the distinctive Moroccanoil fragrance.
