**All Day Makeup Setting Spray**

A lightweight, seamless cosmetic adhesive that is vegan, refreshing, transfer- and sweat-proof, and keeps your makeup in place for up to 16 hours.
For any event, this vegan, long-lasting, humidity-proof, and lightweight spray from natural ingredients keeps makeup looking great. The ginseng in this spray, which is thought to have revitalising qualities, is suited for all skin types.
