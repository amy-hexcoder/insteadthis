**Slip Tint Dewy Tinted Moisturizer SPF 35 Sunscreen**

A tinted moisturizer that provides 100% mineral UV protection, skin-loving moisture, and sheer, dewy coverage in uncomplicated colours. This tinted moisturizer looks fantastic but thanks to its abundance of skincare superhero components, feels even better. The portable tube is easy to carry and doesn't require shaking.

Avoid choosing your color too carefully! Each adaptable, unambiguous tint blends seamlessly into a variety of skin tones.

**Product Features:**

- \*Hyaluronic Acid: Aids in drawing water to the skin and keeping it moist.
- \*Pansy Flower: An antioxidant that increases hydration by collaborating with hyaluronic acid.
- \*Licorice root extract: Aids in enhancing the skin's general brightness.

**The Fluffy Setting Powder Brush by Saie**

A multipurpose powder brush with a tapered tip and a sleek, palm-sized handle for both precise and general application.
The Fluffy Brush is expertly made to provide focused application. With its tapered tip, you can apply powder to specific regions, and the soft bristles make it easier to produce a smooth finish. For beginners using powder, the palm-sized design is great as it makes product blending simple.

Product Highlights:

- Vegan Product
- Cruelty free Product
- Formulated for loose powder
- Synthetic Bristles
