**Benefit Cosmetics**

**[BADgal BANG! Volumizing Mascara](https://www.sephora.com/product/bad-gal-bang-mascara-P427517)**

This mascara promises to deliver intense black volume while curling, lengthening, and separating the lashes.

It builds up easily to create clump-free volume. It adds thickness to your lashes without making them look overdone. Overall, this is a great choice for every day if you like a bold lash look and it also is a solid option if you are creating a glam look.

**Lancôme**

**[Lash Idôle Lash-Lifting & Volumizing Mascara](https://www.sephora.com/product/lancome-lash-idole-lash-lifting-volumizing-mascara-P467208?skuId=2417145&icid2=products%20grid:p467208:product)**

If you are looking for a drugstore mascara that delivers extra volume, then this is worth picking up.

It can be layered up to create an intense fullness on the lashes. It has got a formula that works well without smudging or clumping, making it an excellent drugstore buy.
