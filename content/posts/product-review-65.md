## CLEMENTINE BUTTERFLY SLEEVE MINI DRESS IN ORANGE

The AKIRA Label [Clementine Butterfly Sleeve Mini Puff Dress In Orange](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/clementine-butterfly-sleeve-mini-dress) features an iridescent, taffeta fabrication and a plunging neckline, puffed shoulder details, a cutout back, and a concealed back zipper. In addition to its mini length and bubble hem, it is lined with a luxurious, satin inner lining. Embrace your inner fashionista with stilettos, a clutch, and this shining piece!

**Features:**

- Shell: 50% Polyester 45% Nylon 5% Spandex
- Lining: 95% Polyester 5% Spandex
- From size small, all measurements are approximate.
- Shoulder to hem: 32.5"
- Sleeves: 6.5"
- Model: 5' 10.5''
