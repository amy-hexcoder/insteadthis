**SEPHORA COLLECTION**

**Pop Fizz Scented [Candle](https://www.sephora.com/product/sephora-collection-pop-fizz-scented-candle-P458728?skuId=2307130&icid2=products%20grid:p458728:product)**

It features orange, cinnamon, patchouli, clove, and sandalwood undertones, and despite the absence of nicotine, the smoky-sweet scent is undoubtedly addictive. It inspires us to knit a patchwork quilt and put on a bluegrass album. Warm, spicy, earthy (but not musty), and faintly fragrant, like a jasmine sprig, describe the aroma. This candle is unbranded, strong, fashionable, and has a ceramic lid that matches, making it well worth the cost. It is simple to recycle it as a trinket holder, or if you're crafty, you can quickly refill it with a fresh candle. In addition, this candle features two wicks, which, given its size, let it burn more evenly.

**Boy Smells**

**LES [Candle](https://www.sephora.com/product/size-up-mascara-P466443?skuId=2339539&icid2=products%20grid:p466443:product)**

After a big rain, the air smells like burnt cedar and sandalwood. Although it has a strong scent, it is not heavy or overbearing. This candle, like the one we chose from Peacesake, comes in a matte-black container that is good at masking smudges like fingerprints and soot. Being constructed of black wax, it has a monochrome appearance that feels both modern and timeless. Other than the Voluspa candle, this candle has the longest burn period (39 hours, according to our estimates), and it sheds the least amount of wax during testing (8 grammes over an hour). The soy-blend wax has been carefully poured, and the wick seems to be perfectly centered in the container.
