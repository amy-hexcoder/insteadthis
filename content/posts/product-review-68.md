**KVD Beauty**

**Tattoo Liner Vegan Waterproof Liquid [Eyeliner](https://www.sephora.com/product/tattoo-liner-P245205?skuId=1177567&icid2=products%20grid:p245205:product)**

It's difficult to imagine this liner is so excellent and so reasonably priced. Because of how creamy it is, it applies smoothly, and the thin tip makes drawing delicate lines simple. To adjust your makeup to your mood, it also comes in a variety of colours (think gold, mint green, lavender, and bronze), and at that price, who wouldn't want to?

**HUDA BEAUTY**

**Creamy Kohl Longwear [Eye Pencil](https://www.sephora.com/product/huda-beauty-creamy-kohl-longwear-eye-pencil-P504900?skuId=2651834&icid2=products%20grid:p504900:product)**

The name says it all. This eyeliner is a pencil that has an almost liquid-like look since it contains silk powder. Although it smudges a little, it works best to provide a smooth, clear definition around the eye area. Additionally, it has a tonne of pigment.
