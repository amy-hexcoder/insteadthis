**VOLUSPA**

**Barbados Grapefruit Jar [Candle](https://www.sephora.com/product/barbados-grapefruit-jar-candle-P504704?skuId=2645604&icid2=products%20grid:p504704:product)**

The predominant aroma of this candle is pine, with undertones of wild sage, warm honey, and sea salt. This is more intricate and satisfying overall, but this one is a fantastic substitute.

![](/images/uploads/2023/07/rr1.webp)

**VOLUSPA**

**Santal Vanille Glass Jar [Candle](https://www.sephora.com/product/voluspa-santal-vanille-glass-jar-candle-P455593?skuId=2317956&icid2=products%20grid:p455593:product)**

The vanilla candle also has a lovely vessel, but this one has a different geometric pattern and a green glaze rather than white. This one is considerably stronger than the last. It smells to us like thickets of marsh grass, cantaloupe, honeysuckle, bee pollen, rainwater, and creeping vines.
