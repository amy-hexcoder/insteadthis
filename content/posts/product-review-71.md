**SEPHORA COLLECTION**

**[Waterproof 12HR Retractable Eyeliner Pencil](https://www.sephora.com/product/waterproof-12h-retractable-eyeliner-pencil-P230234?skuId=2616969&icid2=products%20grid:p230234:product)**

Since it is comprised of silicone, it glides across the eyelid effortlessly. If you want a sharp line, it works wonderfully, but if a smokey eye is more your style, you can smudge and soften it before it dries. Additionally, it is highly pigmented, so you don't need to keep tracing over it.

**Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez**

**[Perfect Strokes Longwear & Waterproof Gel Eyeliner](https://www.sephora.com/product/perfect-strokes-longwear-gel-eyeliner-P506844?skuId=2689313&icid2=products%20grid:p506844:product)**

This pencil's microtip may be used to create a cat eye, and it even enables you to get near to the lash line—a difficult feat—and it stays in place. The pencil automatically twists up and lasts for a remarkable 24 hours of wear, so you never have to worry about having a sharpener on hand. It is also simple to use due to the soft gel feel.
