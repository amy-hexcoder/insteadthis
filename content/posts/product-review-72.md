**NARS**

**[Radiant Creamy Concealer](https://www.sephora.com/product/radiant-creamy-concealer-P377873?skuId=2172310&icid2=products%20grid:p377873:product)**

Although there are many excellent choices available, Nars Radiant Creamy Concealer is currently the best. The award-winning formula, which is hailed as the best by both makeup professionals and beauty enthusiasts, performs everything from contouring and highlighting to color-correcting.

According to former Byrdie Editorial Director Faith Xue, "Not only does it give your skin a natural and soft finish, but it's also the easiest product to use with any beauty tool of your choice—fingertips included."

This superstar concealer does an excellent job covering dark circles, reducing redness, and hiding blemishes for up to 16 hours because of skin-loving botanicals, a tone-balancing mineral powder, and tiny light diffusers. We also appreciate that a tiny version is offered. However, some reviewers do point out that it can be a little too drying for skin that is more mature.

**Too Faced**

**Born This Way Super Coverage Multi-Use Longwear [Concealer](https://www.sephora.com/product/born-this-way-super-coverage-multi-use-sculpting-concealer-P432298?skuId=2084408&icid2=products%20grid:p432298:product)**

It has been a bright spot in the pharmacy section. Reviews claim that this dependable recipe provides a concealer that works well in all situations. Dryer skin types can benefit from its creamy texture.
