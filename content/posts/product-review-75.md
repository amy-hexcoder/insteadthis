**Charlotte Tilbury**

**[Glow Glide Face Architect Highlighter](https://www.sephora.com/product/the-ordinary-salicylic-acid-2-masque-P448530?skuId=2267581&icid2=products%20grid:p448530:product)**

It's truly miraculous what the Charlotte Tilbury Glow Glide Face Architect Highlighter can do. Although it is a powder, it applies to the skin like a liquid and leaves a beautiful finish behind. It really changes the game!

This Charlotte Tilbury highlighter is made with multifaceted pigments that give the complexion a "blurred" appearance, enhancing the skin's appearance.

**Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez**

**[Positive Light Liquid Luminizer Highlight](https://www.sephora.com/product/rare-beauty-by-selena-gomez-positive-light-liquid-luminizer-highlight-P38855877?skuId=2362168&icid2=products%20grid:p38855877:product)**

Once done, your face's high points are exquisitely brought out, and your skin looks radiant with a sparkly (but never gritty) radiance.
