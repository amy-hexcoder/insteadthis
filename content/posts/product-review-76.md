**Prada**

**[Paradoxe Eau de Parfum](https://www.sephora.com/product/paradoxe-eau-de-parfum-P501198?skuId=2591170&icid2=products%20grid:p501198:product)**

The firm chose the ultimate sensual but sweet, flower power aroma for Prada Paradoxe. With just a few spritzes, it taps into that seductive feminine energy while securely residing in the flowery ambery olfactory family. The fragrance's top notes include the much-loved bergamot, a tangy tangerine, and pear accord, the scent's center is dominated by neroli and flower bloom, and the base is comprised of vanilla and amber.

**Yves Saint Laurent**

**[Libre Eau De Parfum](https://www.sephora.com/product/libre-eau-de-parfum-P448102?skuId=2249738&icid2=products%20grid:p448102:product)**

Think has a powerful and lingering perfume that begins with strong lavender and orange blossom notes before settling into the coziest, warmest vanilla/musk skin scent. The vanilla in this scent is of exceptional quality. A simple fragrance that is safe for the workplace. The base notes lingered forever; the next day, I could still smell them on my body. Although it's not a smell you wear every day, the flowery notes last for the better part of a day. It has a strong, mature scent that is pleasant.
