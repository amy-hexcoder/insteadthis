**SEPHORA COLLECTION**

**[3-in-1 Pencil Sharpener](https://www.sephora.com/product/sephora-collection-3-in-1-sharpener-P472055)**

**What is it?**

A professional-grade three-in-one dual-blade pencil sharpener that provides accurate, sharp points on your essential beauty tools.

**What Else You Should Know?**

The practical design of this professional sharpener includes a removable pencil-size adapter, a 1 1/8-inch connected cleaning stick, and a tight-fitting lid to capture shavings and prevent spills. It has a precise tip for pencils with 8, 10, and 12 mm diameters.
