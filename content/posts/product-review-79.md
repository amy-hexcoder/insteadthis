**SEPHORA COLLECTION**

**[Lip Liner To Go](https://www.sephora.com/product/lip-liner-to-go-P395532)**

Sephora's brilliant product is a colorless lip liner, hence the name Universal Lip Liner. It leaves the lips colorless and can be worn beneath any lipstick. The best aspect is that it doesn't change the color of the lipstick at all while producing the same results as a traditional lip liner, namely adding definition, making it last longer, and making the color pop.

It has a waxy feel to it. When I applied it to my lips, it felt like I was using a wax crayon, which isn't the most pleasant of sensations. It's also quite matte. When you apply it, you will notice some pulling and tugging of the lips. Aside from that, it is colourless, so you can't see it.

**SEPHORA COLLECTION**

**[All Day Makeup Setting Spray](https://www.sephora.com/product/sephora-collection-makeup-setting-spray-P474100)**

**What it is:**

A 16-hour makeup setting spray that is refreshing and transfer-proof.

**What it does:**

This cosmetic setting spray is a lifesaver since it improves the performance of your makeup and extends its wear time by up to 16 hours. It forms a protective barrier that prevents makeup from transferring and is resistant to heat and humidity.

This setting spray keeps makeup from migrating into pores and fine wrinkles all day, reducing the amount of touch-ups required. It will keep your makeup looking flawless in any situation.
