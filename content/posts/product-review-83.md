**The Ordinary**

**[Glycolic Acid 7% Exfoliating Toner](https://www.sephora.com/product/the-ordinary-deciem-glycolic-acid-7-toning-solution-P427406)**

Customer reviews for The Ordinary's Glycolic Acid 7% Toning Solution generally highlight its effectiveness as an exfoliating product. Many users appreciate its affordable price point and report positive outcomes in terms of improved skin texture, reduced dullness, and a more radiant complexion.

The toner's glycolic acid content, known for its exfoliating properties, is often praised for helping to minimize the appearance of pores and addressing issues like uneven skin tone. Some users mention a visible reduction in acne scars and hyperpigmentation with consistent use.
