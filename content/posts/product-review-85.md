Its dependable sidekick, the Bamboo Booster, is a delicate and strong exfoliator that effortlessly refines the appearance of the skin.

The Slaai Makeup-Melting Butter Cleanser contains antioxidant-rich fruit extracts and oils to soothe skin, while an African oil blend of marula, baobab, Kalahari melon, mongongo, and ximenia intensively moisturises and restores the appearance of your skin's elasticity and luminosity. Use the accompanying Bamboo Booster (a physical exfoliator made of ultra-fine bamboo and charcoal spheres) once or twice a week to gently slough off dead skin cells.
