**innisfree**

**Daily UV Defense Invisible Broad Spectrum SPF 36 [Sunscreen](https://www.sephora.com/product/innisfree-daily-uv-defense-sunscreen-spf-36-P456392?skuId=2338325&icid2=products%20grid:p456392:product)**

For a sunscreen made only of physical UV blockers, which typically have a gritty texture and are challenging to rub in, the recipe distributes and absorbs quite rapidly. This sunscreen has titanium dioxide and zinc oxide as its main ingredients and no additional smell.

![](/images/uploads/2023/06/P1.jpg)

**Glow Recipe**

**Watermelon Glow Niacinamide [Sunscreen](https://www.sephora.com/product/glow-recipe-watermelon-glow-niacinamide-sunscreen-spf-50-P481989?skuId=2549400&icid2=products%20grid:p481989:product) SPF 50**

This is our preferred chemical sunscreen that does not include oxybenzone, a frequent active component that certain advocacy groups have voiced concerns over. It is non-greasy, lightweight, and easily matches different skin tones. Even though it has additional fragrance, testers didn't find its fruity scent offensive. It is considerably more expensive than our budget selection, but it costs less per ounce than our physical sunscreen picks.
