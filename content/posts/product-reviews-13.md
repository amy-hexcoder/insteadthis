**ILIA**

**Fullest Volumizing [Mascara](https://www.sephora.com/product/ilia-fullest-volumizing-mascara-P475110?skuId=2475259&icid2=products%20grid:p475110:product)**

Any drugstore with a beauty section will have this well-liked mascara on display. It has been praised for years as a top-tier volumizing mascara, and despite the introduction of numerous other versions after this one, it is still a customer favorite. It guarantees to thicken and define every lash without clumping or smudging.

We can attest that it lives up to its clump-free promise. The lashes were big and voluminous while still looking natural, and because the product isn't excessively thick or gooey, it works well for defining the lower lashes.

Although we wish it didn't, it shouldn't be a deal-breaker if you're not particularly sensitive to scent. It smells strongly of paint.

**Benefit Cosmetics**

**Roller Lash Curling & Lifting [Mascara](https://www.sephora.com/product/roller-lash-curling-lifting-mascara-P392516?skuId=1665082&icid2=products%20grid:p392516:product)**

Tubing mascaras are fantastic for lengthening lashes, and this one does just that. In order to separate, define, and lengthen each lash, the formula forms "tubes" that wrap around them. The benefit of increasing really stands out, even if it also adds a little volume and curl.

We value the slender, plastic wand since it makes coating the lashes in challenging places, such as the inner corners, simple. We particularly appreciate the length this mascara gave us, the fact that it didn't budge or smudge throughout the day, and how easy it was to take off.
