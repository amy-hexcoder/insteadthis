**stila**

**Stay All Day® Waterproof [Liquid Eye Liner](https://www.sephora.com/product/stay-all-day-waterproof-liquid-eye-liner-P253818?skuId=1221084&icid2=products%20grid:p253818:product)**

There is something for everyone under this brand, with finishes ranging from shimmer to matte to satin to glitter. But things don't stop there. Because of how creamy and pigmented the pencil is, it works well to draw bold, dramatic lines. And after it sets, it won't move again.

**Lancôme**

**DRAMA LIQUI-PENCIL™ [Longwear Eyeliner](https://www.sephora.com/product/drama-liqui-pencil-longwear-eyeliner-P399503?skuId=2480051&icid2=products%20grid:p399503:product)**
