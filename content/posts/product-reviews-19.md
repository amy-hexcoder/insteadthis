**TOM FORD**

**[Tobacco Vanille](https://www.sephora.com/product/tobacco-vanille-P393151?skuId=1449289&icid2=products%20grid:p393151:product)**

Tobacco Vanille is the right combination of sweet and fruity, as well as deep and smokey. It's as enticing as it is mysterious. A true puzzler that will keep you feeling confident and in control while capturing the attention of others around you.

Opulent. Warm. Iconic. Tom Ford's love of London inspired this spicy scent reminiscent of an English gentleman's club. He reimagines a classic fragrance genre by incorporating creamy tonka bean, vanilla, cocoa, dry fruit notes, and sweet wood sap for a modern, luxurious, and nearly dizzying impression that is all man unless worn by a woman.
