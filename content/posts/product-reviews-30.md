**[Limitless Lash Lengthening Clean Mascara](https://www.sephora.com/product/limitless-lash-mascara-P431750)**

It's a great alternative for all the reasons listed above, but it's starting to severely irritate my eyes, which is why it's dropping from my list of favorites. This is supposed to be safe for delicate eyes, but it appears that what is "safe" for one eyeball may not be safe for another, which is confusing. The lash's flip or curl at the end is unparalleled, and the length is astounding.I can't get beyond the wet eyes, though. Oh, and I discovered a fantastic substitute that is less expensive and hasn't bothered my eyes.

![](/images/uploads/2024/04/p1-1-1024x1024.webp)

**Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez**

**[Warm Wishes Effortless Bronzer Sticks](https://www.sephora.com/product/rare-beauty-by-selena-gomez-warm-wishes-effortless-bronzer-sticks-P479343)**

This unique, liquid-like cream bronzer stick blends seamlessly into skin to give the appearance of sun-kissed warmth that lasts all day. Softly sculpting the cheeks, eyes, and entire face is made simple by the texture's weightlessness and seamless glide. This product goes on smoothly and seamlessly with a natural finish, making it incredibly blendable and buildable. The water-resistant, non-greasy composition won't clog pores or cake.
