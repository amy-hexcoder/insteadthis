# Pore Diffusing Primer - Always an Optimistic Collection

Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez

![](/images/uploads/2022/09/s2448124-av-01-zoom.jpg)
*Credits@Sephora*

Looking for a soothing, hydrating and fine base for your makeup routine? Rare Beauty's primers never miss the mark! Comes in a ultralight formula that absorbs shine and blends evenly into your skin to give you that flawless base. This hydrating primer blurs out pores, visibly evens out fine lines and soothes the skin texture that helps your makeup stay longer throughout the day!

![](/images/uploads/2022/09/s2448124-av-05-zoom.jpg)
*Credits@Sephora*

Highlights:

- Packed with Hyaluronic Acid, Green Tea extract and Vitamin E
- Works well on Normal, Oily, Dry and Combination Skin types
- Finish: Matte
- Vegan, Paraben free Product

## Stay Vulnerable Melting Cream Blush

Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez

Did anyone say Blush??

Rare Beauty's Stay Vulnerable Melting Cream Blush is a dream come true for all blush lovers! The creamy formula of this blush is almost like a liquid that melts into the skin and leaves with a lit-from-within glow. Another good thing about this product is that it is water-resistant, making it a first of it's kind product! You can choose from a range of five beautiful shades and complete your makeup look with a blush that's non-greasy, non-cakey and crease free!

![](/images/uploads/2022/09/s2418663-av-12-zoom-3.jpg)
*Credits@Sephora*

Highlights:

- Light coverage Creamy Blush
- Works Best on Dry, Normal and Combination Skin Types
- Finish: Satin
- Paraben Free, Vegan Product
