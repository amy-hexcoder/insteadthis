**Almost Lipstick by Clinique**

![](/images/uploads/2023/01/p122751-av-06-zoom.webp)
*Credits@Sephora*

An ultra-light, universally pleasing lip balm in a well-known colour that everyone may use since it gives each person's lips a distinctive appearance. This clear [lip balm](https://www.sephora.com/product/almost-lipstick-P122751) blends with the natural tone of your lips to give you a customised lip look that's neither quite a gloss nor nearly a lipstick. This emollient-rich, sheer solution applies smoothly for simple layering. This item has been allergy-tested and is completely free of gluten, alcohol, silicone, mineral oils, and fragrances.

**Product Highlights:**

- Paraben Free
- Phthalates Free
- Contains Ricinus Communis (Castor) Seed Oil

**Hyaluronic Acid 2% + B5 Hydrating Serum by Ordinary**

A solution that moisturizes using vegan, ultra-pure [hyaluronic acid](https://www.sephora.com/product/the-ordinary-deciem-hyaluronic-acid-2-b5-P427419). For multi-depth hydration, this formulation blends two percent of low, medium, and high molecular weight hyaluronic acid with a next-generation H.A. crosspolymer. The inclusion of B5 for improved hydration supports this system. This product doesn't include any oil, alcohol, silicone, nuts, or gluten and is vegan and cruelty-free.

Suits best for these skin types:

- Dry
- Normal
- Combination

**Product Highlights:**

- Silicon Free

- Deep Hydration and Moisturization
