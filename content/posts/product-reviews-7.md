**The Ordinary – AHA 30% + BHA 2% Exfoliating Peeling Solution**

![](/images/uploads/2023/01/s2210607-main-zoom.webp)
*Credits@Sephora*

A product that exfoliates the skin to help reduce visible blemishes and enhance the texture and brightness of the skin. Alpha hydroxy acid (AHA) and beta hydroxy acid (BHA) are mixed in this 10-minute exfoliating facial to reduce the appearance of blemishes. It makes skin texture appear smoother for a more radiant appearance.

Also, this works best for Oily to Normal Skin Types. Definitely a must try.

Product Highlights:

- Liquid Formula
- Treats uneven tone, blemishes & dullness
- Contains Salicylic Acid, Glycolic Acid and Lactic Acid
- Paraben free

**The Ordinary – Niacinamide 10%**

A literal pore minimizer!! A high-potency vitamin and mineral blemish treatment including 10% pure niacinamide and 1% zinc PCA The vitamin B3 niacinamide lessens the visibility of skin congestion and pimples. Zinc PCA supports a high 10 percent concentration of this vitamin in the formula to balance the visible elements of sebum activity. Alcohol, silicone, nuts, gluten, and scent were not included in the formulation of this vegan and cruelty-free product.

Product Highlights:

- Vitamin B3
- Deals with oil production and pores.
- Works for most skin types – oily, dry, combi and normal
