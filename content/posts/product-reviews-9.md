## Stay Vulnerable Melting Cream Blush

Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez

Did anyone say Blush??

Rare Beauty's [Stay Vulnerable Melting Cream Blush](https://www.sephora.com/product/P467451) is a dream come true for all blush lovers! The creamy formula of this blush is almost like a liquid that melts into the skin and leaves with a lit-from-within glow. Another good thing about this product is that it is water-resistant, making it a first of it's kind product! You can choose from a range of five beautiful shades and complete your makeup look with a blush that's non-greasy, non-cakey and crease free!
