How many of you have uncontrollable hair loss during the rainy season? I'm confident that many would raise their hands and declare, "I'm one of them." While the monsoonal breeze may be refreshing, the humidity, dust, and pollution that it brings with it undoubtedly cause little but obvious hair damage. This can be described as clingy, frizzy, difficult-to-manage, coarse, and falling hair.

It's essential to establish a well-planned hair care routine so that we can take care of our distinctive crown before these seasonal problems have a significant negative impact on the quality of the hair and result in long-term damage. After all, this crown draws attention to our personality and physical characteristics. We must therefore take our time.

**TO PROTECT YOUR HAIR FROM THE RAINY SEASON, USE THESE BEST HAIRCARE TIPS:**

1. **GUARD AGAINST RAINWATER FROM YOUR SCALP**

Acidic monsoon waters can cause your hair to become brittle and frizzy. Even if your hair is in contact with it, be careful to quickly dry it with a soft towel. This is due to the fact that the pH of rainwater is completely bad for your hair. Therefore, if you have been wet from a strong rainstorm, you should purportedly take a head bath with a clarifying [shampoo](https://skinkraft.com/blogs/articles/how-to-choose-right-shampoo). Look online for the greatest shampoo that will fit you and will purport to fix your hair problems.

Men typically aren't aware of the best shampoo for them in these situations, but women are quite familiar with their options.

2. **HEALTHY MEALS ARE EQUAL TO HAIR HEALTH.**

"We are what we eat," is a cliche that you've probably heard. A balanced, nutrient-rich diet promotes the health of our hair and scalp from the inside out. Every day, eating foods high in fatty acids, vitamins, and minerals fuels the development, durability, and quality of our hair. They have a strong defense against dangerous things coming their way because of this.

3. **CONSIDER HAIR PRODUCTS IN LIGHT OF THE CHANGING WORLD**

Your scalp and hair respond differently to the seasons much like various skin types do. This is why it is a good idea to review your hair care regimen as the monsoon season begins. The following guidance should be helpful:

Use oils for your hair that balance sebum production and hydration without over-oiling it or disrupting its moisture balance.

To eliminate hair fall and pollutants that persist in your hair during this season, choose the appropriate shampoo. While a shampoo with activated charcoal can effectively purify your scalp and hair, an anti-hair fall shampoo can reduce the excessive amounts of strands that are falling out.

Use a conditioner without oils, and exclusively on your face.

4. USE YOUR COMB IN THE RIGHT MANNER.

Using the appropriate comb for your hair's kind and texture—curly, wavy, or straight— Instead of using a plastic comb that makes your hair static, use a wooden comb or a brush. To avoid hair breakage, your comb's teeth should be large, especially if you have thick, long hair. To maintain your comb clean, and free of debris, and germs, wash it frequently.

**WHY SHOULD YOUR MONSOON HAIR CARE ROUTINE INCLUDE HAIR OILING AS A PRIORITY?**

The monsoon, which transforms the landscape into something lush, green, and colorful and refills groundwater reserves all across the nation, is regarded as the most significant season for Indians. Additionally, the monsoon has a number of negative consequences on our scalp, skin, and hair. Our hair becomes frizzy and dries out in the monsoon's humid air. This problem can be solved by progressively integrating hair oils into your hair routine to offset the negative effects of the season.

With the oils and treatments we apply, we frequently fight to maintain its strength and vitality. There is no need to panic, though, since this blog will give you advice on selecting the proper oil for the monsoon season as well as discuss the advantages of oiling your hair.
