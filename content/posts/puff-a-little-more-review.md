## LADYLIKE PUFF SLEEVE BLOUSE

Chiffon creates the sheer appearance of the AKIRA Label [Lady Like Puff Sleeve Blouse](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/lady-like-puff-sleeve-blouse). A pleated collar, long puffed sleeves, flounced cuff details, and a rhinestone-studded button placket highlight this shirt.

![](/images/uploads/2022/04/lady-like-puff-sleeve-blouse_grey_2_2-678x1024.jpg)
*Credits@Akira*

![](/images/uploads/2022/04/lady-like-puff-sleeve-blouse_grey_3_3-2-678x1024.jpg)
*Credits@Akira*

**Features:**

- Fabric: 100% Polyester
- Care: Hand wash cold
- Measurements: 13" shoulder to hem, 30" sleeve
- Size: Small (approximately)

**My thoughts are:**

The fabric is incredibly comfortable and wrinkle-free. Moreover, the blouse has lovely sleeves and a flattering cut. Buy it once, and you'll be hooked. For a polished look, opt for a sleek bodysuit and high-rise pants.

## GET IT RIGHT, GET IT TIGHT FAUX LEATHER BIKER SHORTS

[Get It Right, Get It Tight Faux Leather Biker Shorts](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/get-it-right-get-it-tight-faux-leather-biker-shorts) by AKIRA are a must-have for those looking for hot booties! A stretchy pleather fabrication makes up these bottoms, featuring a high waistline, wide waistbands, and mid-thigh length.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 80% Polyester, 10% PU, 10% Spandex
- Care: Hand Wash Cold
- Measurement: 19" Waist to Hem

An excellent basic all-around. This material is much softer than yoga shorts (like butter). Dress up or down these shorts with a sleek top or cropped graphic tee! Stunning booty is a guarantee!
