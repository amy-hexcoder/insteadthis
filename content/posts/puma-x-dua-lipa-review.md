## PUMA X DUA LIPA T7 JACKET

You're about to leave your mark in the [PUMA X DUA Lipa T7 Jacket](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/puma-x-dua-lipa-t7-jacket). An ultra-soft stretch knit top features a mock neckline, a butterfly zipper pull, long sleeves, and stripe detailing on the sides. This cropped jacket has ribbed knit hems and an embroidered PUMA logo on the chest.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 69% polyester, 24% viscose, and 7% elastane
- Care: Machine Wash Cold
- Shoulder-to-hem length: 15"
- Sleeves: 24"
- Size: Small
