## AZALEA WANG STEPPING OUT QUILTED PEPLUM VEST

Its puffer base will make it easy to snuggle up in the AZALEA WANG [Stepping Out Quilted Peplum Vest](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/stepping-out-quilted-peplum-vest). Featuring a sleeveless silhouette, patchwork detailing, a fold-over collar, peplum detailing, and a duster length with front snap buttons, this coat is the ultimate in cozy comfort.

**Features:**

- Material: 100% polyester
- Care: Dry clean only
- Hem to shoulder: 37"
- (approximately measured from size small)
- Size: Small
