QUESTION

Hi Amanda,

As soon as the rainy season arrives, my pet cheesecake gets sick. Do you have any tips for keeping my puppy healthy and happy so that he doesn't get sick this time?

Love,

Shanaya!

ANSWER

Hi Shanaya,

Although the rainy season brings some respite from the heat, furry friends still suffer. During this time, it is crucial to care for our pets well. I'd like to share some tips on how to keep your pet healthy and happy during the rainy season.

**1. You must keep them dry:**

Keep your pet dry during the rainy season, and provide a warm, dry place for them to rest, preferably indoors. Whenever your pet is wet, make sure they are dried thoroughly with a towel to prevent them from catching a cold or developing skin problems.

**2. Provide proper shelter:**

Make sure your pet has a suitable shelter if they spend time outside. Keeping them in a sturdy doghouse or a covered area will keep them safe from the elements. Avoid water pooling inside the shelter by elevating it.

**3. Stay away from muddy areas:**

Try to avoid muddy areas during walks or playtime with your pet. If your pet does get muddy, rinse them with lukewarm water and dry them thoroughly.

**4. Ensure cleanliness:**

Your pet needs regular grooming to keep its coat healthy and free of infections. Make sure your pet's fur is brushed regularly to remove dirt, debris, and tangles, and check your pet's paws after walks for twigs, sticks, or mud. Trim their nails to prevent slipping on wet surfaces.

![](https://www.cessnalifeline.com/media/magefan_blog/Dogs_during_rainy.jpg)

**5. Exercise inside:**

Rain can disrupt your pet's regular exercise routine. Engage them in indoor activities such as playing with interactive toys or teaching them new tricks to keep them active and entertained. It will keep them mentally and physically healthy and prevent boredom.

**6. Keep pests at bay:**

You should ensure your pet is up-to-date with flea and tick prevention treatments during the rainy season. Check their fur for signs of infestation, such as excessive scratching or redness. Consult your veterinarian if you notice anything.

**7. Eat a healthy diet:**

If the rainy season affects your pet's appetite, be sure they are receiving a well-balanced diet and fresh, clean water at all times. Consult your veterinarian right away if your pet's appetite changes or if they show signs of illness.

So, I hope you like these tips to make sure your furry friend stays dry, clean, and protected from the challenges that rain brings. Don't forget to give your pets some extra attention during the rainy season to keep them healthy and happy.

Love,

Amanda!
