Falling in love with someone is one of the most miraculous feelings that you can experience in your life. It is a beautiful feeling, and that is the only reason why many songs, poems, and couplets try to capture the emotion very accurately. But the reality is again different, and it is not easy as it appears in the movies, even when we have [online dating applications](https://www.bustle.com/articles/186465-11-benefits-of-using-dating-apps-vs-dating-sites-because-efficiency-is-key). And even after trying all these dating applications, you wonder what am I still single? Why can't I find love? So, the first thing in changing any dating pattern is getting into the root of it and acknowledging what wrong you are doing. So, here is a slew of reasons why you might be single:

**1.)   You are selecting people who are not available:**

So, if you look back at your dating history, how would you characterize the people you have been with? So, if you are looking for a long-lasting relationship; but keep getting a different result every time, you may be drawn to unavailable people. In simple words, the reason you have not found love is that you choose to be with people who cannot commit.

If finding love for you is a priority, then just make a list of [red flags](https://www.gottman.com/blog/red-flag-green-flag-what-to-look-for-when-youre-dating/) that hit you hard. Review the list, date someone new, and just keep an eye out for the signs. You can also choose to be with someone who wants the same things you love doing. It will help in increasing the chances of falling in love.

**2.)   Your insecurities are making you clingy:**

Are you someone who keeps constantly looking for signs that are wrong in every dating scenario? Do you feel like you simply need constant reassurance from your love? Do you constantly keep worrying when someone does not text you back within a certain amount of time? So, if dating tends to bring up this kind of stress, then you might have an anxious attachment style. It is not your fault. It just stems way back to the unpredictability of your people's love and affection from a tender age.

**3.)   You do not allow yourself to experience the real intimacy:**

If you run when someone is getting close to you; then you are avoidant. You think you want a relationship, but when someone expects a certain level of intimacy, you need quality time or feel suffocated and make an exit. But, hey, relationships are only worthwhile if they force you out of your comfort zone. So, just allow yourself to experience the real intimacy in your relationship.

**4.)   You are afraid of getting hurt:**

We know that breakups are devastating, and if you have been hurt in the past, it becomes very challenging to start something new. Also, you constantly want a commitment, but deep down you fear getting hurt again. But to find love, you need to be vulnerable. Because this is how relationships work and personal [connections](https://blogs.webmd.com/relationships/20191224/why-making-real-connections-is-so-important) take place. Also, when you do not want to let go of your past, you find it difficult to move on. But, it is difficult to find love again because you need to trust someone from your heart again. Always move ahead instead of thinking about your memories. It will help you find the love of your love.

**5.)   Your confidence is low:**

Confidence and [self-esteem](https://calgarycounselling.com/blog/self-esteem-tips) play a crucial role when you want to find the love of your life. However, many people cannot find love because they do not think they are worth it. It happens mostly because of the childhood days that impact lives. So, if you are someone who struggles with low self-esteem, then it is time to recognize that this lack of confidence will affect you. But, hey, you are worth it. It is crucial to explore these feelings with the help of a counselor.

**6.)   Settling down:**

So, when it comes to finding love, it is crucial to set high standards for yourself. Rather than being with someone just for the materialistic things, be with someone because they love you. To find love- it is important to find someone with similar values and life goals.

You know, finding love takes real work. So, always remind yourself that the more people you meet, the more you push yourself out of your comfort zones. And this will also help you find the love of your life. So, by spicing up your routine and putting yourself in the dating world, find someone with whom you truly connect. It would help you find love, real love!
