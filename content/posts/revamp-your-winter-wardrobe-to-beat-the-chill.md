Fashion isn't always easy in extreme weather, specifically when the temperatures are below average. Many people seem to be experiencing a slump as winter approaches.

That's pretty normal at this point. So, how can we combat it? [Brighten](/stories/how-to-pull-together-a-go-to-outfit-when-you-have-nothing-to-wear) up your wardrobe with some fun clothing and accessories.

If you're used to subzero temperatures or cannot deal with anything below zero, now's the time to start planning your [winter wardrobe](/stories/stunning-winter-coats-that-will-elevate-your-style-a-worthwhile-investment). We've gathered some outfits to help you prepare.

We'll take a look at these outfits immediately, but first some tips to stay warm on chilly days:

#### Winter Survival Tips:

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/09/pexels-photo-3584505-683x1024.jpeg)
  *Credits@Pexels*

- For staying warm, layering is essential. Layer a camisole or tank top with a tee, a long sleeve shirt, or a sweater, and finish with a coat. Add a pair of warm tights or leggings (you can even double them!) underneath your pants or jeans.

- Make sure your hands and feet are covered all the time. It is safer to wear insulated boots or shoes or wear thick socks (wool usually works best). Be sure not to forget those cozy gloves and mittens!

- You can accessorize with many wintery accessories. Embroidered scarves, hats, knitted headbands, and earmuffs - let your imagination run wild.

- When choosing winter clothing, choose fabrics with a high thermal value, such as wool, fleece, cashmere, and flannel.

- Lastly, remember that safety will always take precedence over fashion. You can still be chic and warm, but frigid temperatures can be deadly, so it would be better to sacrifice your stylish attire than suffer the consequences.

Wear a multipurpose, [waterproof vest](https://www.decathlon.in/warm-and-waterproof-jackets-20233?id=20233&type=c) over a cozy gray hoodie in this look rather than a typical raincoat. You can keep out the cold with a pair of fun and comfy wool socks and sleek snow boots.
