## FAN BEHAVIOR CUTOUT LONG SLEEVE CORSET BLOUSE

From AKIRA Label, this [Corset Blouse with Cutouts](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/fan-behavior-cutout-long-sleeve-corset-blouse) is made from structured blazer fabric. A corset-style bodice and padded shoulders adorn the dress. Long sleeves with cuffed wrists, a sexy cutout at the chest, and a [princess seam](/stories/lace-love-is-real-review) give it an elegant look. In the back, there are also cutouts and lace-up enclosures.

**Features:**

- Material: 95% Polyester, 5% Spandex
- Care: Hand wash only
- Dimensions: 19.5" shoulder to hem and 23.5" sleeves
- Size: Small

*Credits@Akira*

![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/f/a/fan-behavior-cutout-long-sleeve-corset-blouse_black_5_5.jpg)
*Credits@Akira*

**A few thoughts:**

Oh my gosh, that blouse is so hot. I love it. I can't get enough of the back cutouts and lace-up enclosures. You can wear this in either an elegant or casual setting when you want to cover up while still looking chic.

## HEIDE BACK CUTOUT SKINNY JEANS

AKIRA Label's [Heide Back Cut Out Skinny Jeans](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/heide-back-cut-out-skinny-jeans) are made of black denim and feature a back cut out under the waistband. A button-up and zipper closure, five pockets, and a high-rise waistline complete these jeans.

**Features:**

- Material: 98% Cotton, 2% Spandex
- Stretch: Minimal
- Care: Hand wash only
- Measures: 42" waist to hem
- Fit: Size 15

**A few thoughts:**

Look no further for your bottom wear collection! Jazz up your wardrobe with these cutout jeans. Due to the cotton fabric, you'll stay comfortable throughout the day. Add a floral shirt, mules, and a tote to elevate your look.
