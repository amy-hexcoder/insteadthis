## AZALEA WANG FUNKY BEATS CHUNKY SANDAL IN PURPLE MULTI

Take your groove to the next level with the [AZALEA WANG Funky Beats Chunky Sandal in Purple Multi](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/azalea-wang-funky-beats-chunky-sandal-in-purple-multi). A holographic platform sole and a chunky heel complement the strappy faux leather upper on this glossy shoe. The shoe fastens at the ankle with an ankle buckle.

**Features:**

- Toe: Open
- Heel: Chunky
- Sole: Platform
- Platform Height: 2.25"
- Heel Height: 6"

**Features:**

Material: 100% Polyester
Care: Hand wash cold
Dimensions: 17" shoulder to hem
Sleeves: 24”
Wearing size S
