## GOLDEN BABY MAXI SATIN DRESS WITH GOLD CHAIN TRIM

Life is a celebration, so make the most of it. Get your accolades in this [Golden Baby Maxi](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/golden-baby-maxi-satin-dress-with-gold-chain-trim) Satin Dress with Gold Chain Trim by AKIRA Label. A cowl neckpiece with a satin fabrication, flowing maxi length, lined interior, and a back slit accent makes a stylish statement. A faux gold chain-link strap completes the look. Lobster claw clasps secure the straps.

**Features:**

- Material: 95% polyester, 5% spandex
- Care: Hand wash only
- Length: approx. 73" shoulder to hem
- Size: Small
