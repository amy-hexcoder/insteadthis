## BETTER THAN EXTENSIONS RHINESTONE DRIP HEADBAND

There's nothing more romantic than rhinestones. AKIRA Label's [Menage A Trois Strand Headband](https://www.shopakira.com/accessories/hair-accessories/better-than-extensions-rhinestone-drip-headband) is a stylish headpiece with a sparkling metallic silver base, a triple band design, and a rhinestone gemstone embellishment.

**Features:**
Material: Man-made
Size: One

**Reactions:**
I'm in love with this headband. Rhinestones dripping down make it a sexy accessory for any occasion. If you pair a satin midi skirt with a long-sleeved top with this headband, it's a perfect combination.
