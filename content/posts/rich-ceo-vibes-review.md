## A RICH BITCH LOOK RHINESTONE NECKLACE

A rich and chic chain link necklace featuring pave encrusted rhinestones along the front and a thin chain at the back with a lobster clasp, the AKIRA Label [A Rich Bitch Look Rhinestone Necklace](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/a-rich-bitch-look-rhinestone-necklace-yn6736gd) looks stunning on any woman.
