**VALENTINA TAFFETA RUFFLE SLEEVE ROMPER**

This shimmering, textured [Valentina Taffeta Ruffle Sleeve Romper](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/valentina-taffeta-ruffle-sleeve-romper) features a mock neckline, cap sleeves, and ruffle trim detailing on the bodice and back. A concealed back zipper closure, an adjustable waist belt, and attached mini shorts complete this ensemble.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 45% Nylon, 45% Polyester, 5% Spandex
- Care: Hand Wash Cold
- Shoulder to hem: 30"
- Inseam: 2.5"
- Sleeve: 6"
- (approx. measured from size small)
- Size: Small
