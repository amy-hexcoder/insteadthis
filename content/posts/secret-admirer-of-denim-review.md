## SECRET ADMIRER DENIM MAXI SKIRT

The [AKIRA Label Secret Admirer Denim Maxi Skirt](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/secret-admirer-denim-maxi-skirt) is made from a structured denim material and features a wide elastic waistband that will ensure you stay comfortable and stylish. Designed with two front pockets, contrast stitching along the seams, and practical pop-button closures on the front and sides, this jacket has a lot to offer.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 98% Cotton 2% Spandex
- Stretch-free
- Care: Hand wash cold
- Waist to hem: 37"
- Measure from small (approximately)
- Size: Small

[AKIRA Label Secret Admirer Denim Crop Top](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/secret-admirer-denim-crop-top) features a structured denim fabrication that makes it versatile and easy to wear. **Features:**
