## GIGI TEDDY SHACKET WITH RHINESTONES

The [AKIRA Label Gigi Teddy Shacket With Rhinestones](https://www.shopakira.com/clothing/gigi-teddy-shacket-with-rhinestones) is the perfect way to stay warm and bougie! With its glam rhinestone front design, long sleeves, and snap-button placket, this jacket is a fuzzy cropped jacket. With a collared neckline and a snap-button front chest pocket with fringed rhinestone details, this jacket has everything you need to look great.

**Features:**

- Material: 100% polyester
- Care: Hand Wash Cold or Dry Clean
- Shoulder to hem: 22.5"
- Sleeves: 22"
- Based on size small (approximately)
- Model Size: Small

## RHODE MIX MEDIA DENIM SHACKET

In this [AKIRA Label Rhode Mix Media Denim Shacket](https://www.shopakira.com/clothing/rhode-mix-media-denim-shacket), a denim vest top layer has a collared neckline. A distressed hem, mock snap buttons on the chest pockets, belt loops with an adjustable denim belt, and satiny lining. The jacket features vegan leather shoulder epaulets, long sleeves with belted cuffs, an extended hem, and snap button closures on the front.

**Features:**

- Shell 1: 100% cotton
- Shell 2: 50% polyurethane, 50% polyester
- Lining: 100% polyester
- Wipe with a damp cloth
- Shoulder to hem: 27.25"
- Sleeves: 18.75"
- (approximately measured from a size small)
- Model Size: Small
