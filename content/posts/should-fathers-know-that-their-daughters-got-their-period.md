Hi Louise!

Thank you so much for asking this question. I thought about it a couple of times, and I think it depends on one's relationship with their father. If this is something you feel comfortable telling him or not telling him, that is entirely up to your choice. In a healthy relationship, yes one should know and can casually tell their dad by behavior and bodily changes, but unfortunately, not all [relationships](/stories/is-there-any-impact-of-zodiac-signs-in-a-relationship) are healthy. And if you are not close to your dad like me, you can share it with your Mother or talk to your friend about it. I hope you comply with this.
