Hi Julie!

Thank you for making me your home, you beautiful! I get overwhelmed with all the love and support you guys shower upon me. I totally understand your concern lady-but hey the world is changing, and so is our perspective. Ok, wait, before answering your question, I will narrate an incident to you.

I had a few male friends in my life who loved and who hated wearing earrings. It was not an issue for me. They are their ears, and they can do anything with it. So, ok. Anyways, when I met my husband, and he didn't wear earrings, I still fell in love with him, and we started our relationship...

After some years, I was [missing](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/blogs/life-n-style/why-do-we-miss-people-or-a-place/) something in him, something that made him look handsome. So, upon thinking a bit, I realized that my man would look attractive with diamond studs. So, when I asked him if he would like to pierce his ears, he instantly agreed. He told me that he liked wearing studs and wanted to wear them for a very long time. He couldn't do it because he thought it would look stupid on him and thought maybe I would not like it. So, he did not even ask me. After we cleared this up, he pierced his ears. He wore two tiny diamond studs that made him look even more attractive. I loved it so much so that even I decided to sport them. So, my point here is Petrica, that earrings are not only a feminine thing. They can be sported by men too. It looks good on everyone. So, even you can sport them to enhance your beauty and carry them with grace. Yes, your [mother](https://amotherfarfromhome.com/the-love-of-a-mother-what-does-it-do/) is right, you should wear them frequently, you will steal the show.

Love,

Petrica!
