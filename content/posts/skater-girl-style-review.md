## COSMIC SKATES SWIRL PRINT ROLLER SKATER

This funky retro roller skate from [Cosmic Skates](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/cosmic-skates-swirl-print-roller-skates) comes in a swirl print. Faux-leather uppers feature lace-up closures, a block heel, flat metal plates, and high-quality PU wheels that absorb shock, provide traction, and are comfortable to wear.

**Features:**

- Fabric: Synthetic upper, heel, and sole
- Fastening: Metal eyelets
- Plate: Metal
- Shaft: 5.5"
- 2.25" 82A durometer urethane wheels
- With PU brake stoppers and ABEC-7 bearings

**A few thoughts:**
Almost everyone is crazy about swirl-print skates. And plain solid-colored skates don't cut it. This skate fits comfortably, and you won't get sore from it.

## PUT IN WORK STRAPPY CARGO PANT

The AKIRA Label [Put In Work Strappy Cargo Pant](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/put-in-work-strappy-cargo-pant#customer-reviews) will add some feminine edge to your workwear collection. These wide-leg pants feature all-over strappy details, zippered ankle slits, a cargo front pocket, and functional back pockets. The zippered front double buttons complete the look.

**Features:**

- Fabric: 100% cotton
- Stretch: No
- Care: Hand wash only
- Measurement: 12" rise, 40" waist to hem, 8" ankle slit approx.
- Size: Small
