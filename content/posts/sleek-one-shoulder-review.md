**WILD CHILD METALLIC BODYSUIT IN PINK**

Our hearts are racing with the AKIRA Label [Wild Child Metallic Bodysuit in Pink](https://www.shopakira.com/tops/wild-child-metallic-bodysuit-334899)! With a stretchy, metallic base fabrication, an asymmetrical one-shoulder silhouette, a sleeveless design, and a bodycon fit, this number is sleek and sexy. There is a cheeky bottom and a snap closure at the gusset.

**Features:**

- Material: 95% Polyester 5% Spandex
- All measurements are approximate (size small)
- Shoulder to hip: 21"

![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/w/i/wild-child-metallic-bodysuit_pink_13_13.jpg)
![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/w/i/wild-child-metallic-bodysuit_pink_6_6_c1.jpg)

**Thoughts:**

This bodysuit oozes sophistication and elegance with its bold pink fabric and metallic detailing. With its lightweight and stretchy material, the bodysuit is both comfortable and flattering, and its cut is cute as well.

Unique in aesthetics, it is sure to make a statement. Make your cyber-chic look complete with a mini skirt and knee-high boots, or relaxed cargo pants and chunky heels.

**SLEEK VINYL ONE-SHOULDER TOP IN BEIGE**

AKIRA Label's [Sleek Vinyl One Shoulder Top in Beige](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/sleek-vinyl-one-shoulder-top) will give you that sleek, glossy look you've been looking for! With a sleek, stretchy vinyl base, this one-shoulder piece has a wrapped bodycon design, a cropped length, mesh inner lining, and an enticing wet look.

**Features:**

- Main Fabric: 50% PU 40% Polyester 10% Spandex
- Lining: 95% Chinlon 5% Spandex
- Size small (all measurements approximate)
- Shoulder to hem: 17.5"

![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/s/l/sleek-vinyl-one-shoulder-top-in-beige_beige_8_8.jpg)
![](https://www.shopakira.com/media/catalog/product/cache/2fe4d03ca6777fca30f422575217cd53/s/l/sleek-vinyl-one-shoulder-top-in-beige_beige_1_1.jpg)

**Thoughts:**

Its beige color complements other colors and patterns nicely, and the top is well-made and looks great even after a lot of use. The top is both stylish and comfortable, and will quickly become a wardrobe staple. A sexy, night-out look can be achieved with low-rise bottoms and pumps.
