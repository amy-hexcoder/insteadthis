Hi Rosy!

How are you doing babe? Well, I can understand what you are going through.

Well, here is what I feel you should do:

1. Ask your stranger if she did the elaborate nail art herself if she is a hot woman wearing designer clothing, hair dyed every color that exists, multiple layers of makeup, and glitzy nail art. She will describe all the stages, safety measures, and other aspects in great detail. You can see a little bit of Leonardo in her as she gives birth!

2. Ask your stranger about his training regimen or preferred [protein shake](https://www.health.harvard.edu/blog/the-scoop-on-protein-powder-2020030918986) if he's a bearded, big, strong guy whose biceps are always shattering your self-image. His eyes will glitter as he talks to you about his 100 years of intense training!

3. Ask the really cool geek sitting next to you in a crowded bus who is watching "Game of Thrones" who their favorite character is, and if it coincides with yours, you'll become lifelong friends. But don't be afraid to chuck that stranger off of the moving bus if they provide any spoilers!

4. Appreciate his will and will for life if your stranger is an elderly guy sitting in the sweltering sun while singing songs of life. He will tell you every detail of his ups and downs and how he rewrote his days to match his upbringing.

5. Ask your [stranger](https://blogs.sussex.ac.uk/psychology/2023/06/13/nervous-about-talking-to-strangers-its-not-as-hard-as-you-think-and-youre-probably-already-better-at-it-than-you-know/) about his outlook on life and his views on the government if he happens to be a taxi driver who struggles through traffic, deals with unappreciative customers, and gets trashed by owners every evening, and you'll discover a brand-new angle on the same difficult and time-tested issues that are frequently discussed inside seminar halls.

6. Last but not least, if the person seated next to you is quiet and reserved and is browsing through their Quora feed and enjoying some wonderfully prepared answers, let them know that you are also a Quoran and you will have a lot to speak about then!

7. Stay trying new things, stay grinning, and keep changing up your look. Either you'll make an awful experience or a friend. For the long haul we refer to it as "life," I suppose both are essential.

Thank you!

Kiara
