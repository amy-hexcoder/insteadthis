The time of year when layering is all the rage is my jam. A stylish winter coat is a wise investment both financially and stylistically. If you [choose wisely](https://www.bustle.com/articles/46401-how-to-choose-your-perfect-winter-coat-7-essential-tips-for-making-this-important-choice), you will remain stylish and cozy for many years to come. But, if you make a wrong purchase, the likelihoods are that it will end up in the charity pile before the next freeze hits. It is, however, quite monotonous and tiresome to wear the same old coats all the time.

It is important to change up the topmost layer of your outfit to keep it trendy and stylish. We are seeing a wide range of styles in coats and jackets right now.

Below are some standout coats that make you look spectacular and completely turn heads.

## LEATHER TRENCH OVERCOATS

The versatile [trench coat](https://www.whowhatwear.com/leather-trench-coats) is a must-have in every capsule wardrobe. It's the classic wardrobe staple nearly everyone owns: a timeless, iconic piece that instantly makes you look more edgy and sophisticated. However, trench coats have made a massive style shift over the decades from solely the military's practical necessity to a sartorial essential on the runways.

Trench coats are not likely to slow down anytime soon; they are here to stay. It is a steal, so it's a wise decision to invest in this. In this way, you can effortlessly lift your look to the next level with a touch of incognito.

## CLASSIC CAMEL

A camel coat is the quintessential wardrobe staple. Also, they are perennial winter favorites. Usually, they are knee-length, woolen, a bit more or less buttoned, and have a collar. Apart from the standard [camel coat](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/uk/fashion/what-to-wear/news/g37285/best-camel-coats/), there are various variations, such as tie waists, puff-sleeved coats, faux fur collars, and shawl collars. Its camel-colored fabric and streamlined cut make it universally flattering since it goes well with any color combination.

Surely you will love this option if you are searching for something vintage and chic! Moreover, it won't break your budget.

## FASHIONABLE FAUX-FUR COATS

The longline faux-fur jacket will make you look glitzier than anything else.

Nothing speaks fashion better than a faux-fur coat, which keeps you as warm as a puffer but doesn't make you feel overstuffed. The worst feeling is dressing to the hilts on a date night or formal event, then covering it up with a black coat, which ruins the entire look. Consider a faux-fur jacket instead, since it'll look great with cocktail dresses.

During the process of making fur coats, animals are injured and mistreated. As a result, [faux fur](https://nymag.com/strategist/article/best-faux-fur-coats.html) became my favorite. It looks adorable, and there is no animal cruelty involved in its production. With the perfect faux fur coat, you can add a pop of color to your winter wardrobe without emptying your wallet.

## NEUTRAL-COLORED PLAID COAT

There is no denying that plaids are back and are a stylish way to upgrade your printed coat collection. If you stick with neutral colors, you will have greater chances to mix and match them with a broad range of looks. Moreover, it is also ideal for people with more minimalistic tastes.

Even if you do not typically wear the pattern, a plaid coat is something you absolutely cannot resist. These can be worn both for special occasions and as everyday wear. It'll give you a more edgy look, plus it'll make you feel like [Meghan Markle](https://www.meghansfashion.com/burberry-double-breasted-tartan-coat.html).

## INFLATABLE PUFFER

If I had to choose a favorite fashion trend, there's no denying that puffers are above the rest because of their versatility. Puffers can be worn year-round as a layering piece but are particularly helpful during the winter months when snow is forecast. Typically, [puffer jackets](https://www.zara.com/in/en/woman-outerwear-padded-l1195.html) pair well with more contemporary, streetwear-inspired ensembles. You can even find them in funnel neck puffers and vegan leather mini puffers, and they are not too expensive either.

A chic puffer is essential to stay warm on those days when you don't need to shell out. Their best feature is that they go with anything, whether at work or on errands. Put a pom hat on top of it for extra winter cuteness.

## BOLD COLOURED OUTWEAR

You can completely change the look of your outfit with the right hue. Experiencing new tones can be a little intimidating, so it's normal to feel uneasy at first. However, color-coordinated looks will energize your wardrobe and enhance your fashion sense. I've also been smitten with [bolder outerwear](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/209910032618361293/) for a more sophisticated look when the jacket is the focal point of the ensemble. A bold color, such as red, bold green, and yellow to chic pastels, will ensure you stand out from the crowd. A printed scarf can draw attention if a look has too much of a single color. It's affordable too.
