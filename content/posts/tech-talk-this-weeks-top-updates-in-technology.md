1. **OpenAI's Voice Engine: Let's Talk Cloning**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ifupO7FdE88

You've always wanted to sound like Morgan Freeman or Elon Musk, haven't you? Well, OpenAI just announced something that might make your dream come true. In their latest release, they have introduced a tool called [‘Voice Engine’,](https://openai.com/blog/navigating-the-challenges-and-opportunities-of-synthetic-voices) which allows users to clone voices with astounding accuracy. In short, this is not your everyday voice changer; we're dealing with some highly advanced technology. Whatever your desire may be, Voice Engine will make it possible for you to create personalized voice assistants or prank your friends with celebrity impersonations. But use the Voice Engine feature responsibly. Misusing technology can have serious consequences, such as stealing identities and making fake stuff. The use of such powerful tools requires ethical practices and respect for privacy.

2. **Google's Circle to Search: Find What You Need, When You Need It**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdbeqSQjZI8&t=5s

It seems like [Google](/stories/chatgpt-vs-google-search-which-one-should-you-pick-and-for-what) always innovates, and this time they're making searching on Android even more intuitive. Say you're talking about the best sushi spots around, and instead of switching apps to Google, you just draw a circle around the text and a list of relevant results pops up in Google Search. Just like magic, but with Google's ever-improving algorithms. With Circle to Search, we'll be able to find info faster and easier on our Android devices.

3. **Apple WWDC 2024: Brace Yourself for Some Major Reveals**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XMD-zlTNowk

Ah, WWDC - the annual event that unveils Apple's latest and greatest products. It has been confirmed that WWDC will take place in June this year, so tech enthusiasts everywhere are buzzing with excitement. We're rumored to be getting new features in iOS 18 that will change the way we interact with our iPhones and iPads. Then there's visionOS 2 - Apple's attempt to break into the field of augmented reality. In keeping with Apple's tradition of innovation, WWDC promises to be nothing short of spectacular.
