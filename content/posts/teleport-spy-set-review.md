## TELEPORT FOIL EFFECT DENIM BUTTON DOWN

The [AKIRA Label Teleport Foil Effect Denim Button Down](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/golden-hour-foil-effect-denim-buttondown) is made from a washed denim fabrication base. It comes with metallic foil details, a pointed collar, and long sleeves with buttoned cuffs. The longline style has a patch pocket on the chest, a button-down closure in front, and an oversized fit.
