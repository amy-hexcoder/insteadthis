Dear Petrica,

As this year draws to a close, I find myself reflecting on the countless moments that have made it truly special. Among the myriad of experiences, there's one person who stands out – you. I am writing to express my deepest gratitude for the immense joy, warmth, and significance you brought into my life over the past year.

Your thoughtfulness and generosity have turned ordinary days into extraordinary ones. From the small, thoughtful gestures to the grand celebrations, each moment shared with you has been a source of happiness and inspiration. Your ability to make every occasion memorable is a rare gift, and I feel incredibly fortunate to have you in my life.

Whether it was the surprise birthday celebration, the [encouraging words](https://jamesriver.church/blog/why-encouragement-counts) during challenging times, or the simple acts of kindness that brightened my days, your presence has been a constant source of positivity. You have an extraordinary talent for making people feel seen, appreciated, and loved, and I am profoundly grateful for the impact you've had on my life this year.

Your friendship has been a beacon of support, and your enthusiasm for life has been infectious. As I look back on the past twelve months, I can't help but smile at the memories we've created together. Your role in making this year special is beyond measure, and I wanted to take a moment to express my sincere appreciation.

Thank you for being the extraordinary person you are and for making my year truly unforgettable. I am excited to see what the future holds and to continue cherishing the moments we share.

Wishing you a joyous holiday season and looking forward to creating more beautiful memories in the coming year.

With heartfelt gratitude,

Rose
