We can all agree that our [furry friends](/stories/dog-behavior-myths-that-need-debunking) are more than just pets. They're like children or babies to us. As a result, we ensure they have access to funky accessories, fashionable clothes, and yummy food.

Bringing home a new dog accessory brings up a multitude of emotions for you both.

While you're exceedingly excited for your adorable pet to have a [savvy new item](https://www.countryliving.com/shopping/gifts/g1478/gifts-for-dog-lovers/?slide=1), yet at the same time, you're concerned that it won't just be cute but also practical and functional.

Instead of stumbling upon pet accessories online, we have compiled our favorite picks that you and your furry friend will love.

Dogs aren't very good at swimming, so if your pet hangs out by the pool a lot, it's best to wear a life jacket and be worry-free. Besides, aren't these life jackets super cute? You'll find playful, ironic designs like mermaids, sharks, and fish and a variety of colors.

## Water Bottle Sipper

In case you're an [avid dog walker](/stories/paw-parents-travel-checklist-while-road-tripping-with-their-fur-babies) or you regularly walk your pet everywhere you go, be sure you have a water bottle on hand as they stay hydrated during long walks and hot days. The only downside to carrying a plain old water bottle is that it won't keep liquids cold at all.

A water bottle sipper is one of the best dog inventions ever. Not only does this bottle have a convenient pull-top spout, which allows your dog to drink directly from it, but it also has a misting feature to keep your dog cool. This water bottle has an easy carry loop built-in, so carrying that cumbersome bottle is a thing of the past.

## Dog Brush

Many pet parents swear by [dog brushes](https://prf.hn/l/7ggGznQ), as they save them a great deal of time vacuuming up pet hair from their carpets and furniture. Both long-haired and short-haired dogs can use the brush since it removes loose hair from the undercoat without causing damage to the topcoat. The de-shedding brush is pricier than other brushes, but you'll swear it's worth every penny if your dog sheds excessively.

## Cooling Bandanas

It isn't the first time you've seen cute bandanas on dogs, but this one is unique: it's a [cooling bandana](https://www.pinkpaws.co.in/product/trixie-cooling-bandana/). With the advent of new technology, pet brands have created adorable and practical bandanas that will keep your dog feeling comfortable and fashionable.

If you're going for a day at the beach or a hike through the woods, merely tie the bandana around your dog's neck once you've wetted it in the water, frozen it, and then thawed it. A cooling bandana is guaranteed to keep your pet cool for at least two hours.

Do your furry friend a favor and get one of these as soon as possible, regardless of the season.
