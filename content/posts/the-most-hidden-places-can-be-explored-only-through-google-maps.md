This place is situated around 83 miles north of Las Vegas in Nevada. This area is classified as a US military installation. Also, the US government never accepts keeping one undisclosed conspiracy theory that no one knows about it or has access to it.

This place is built for sci-fi circumstances. This vault currently contains thousands of seeds of every single type of plant all over the world. Also, if ever a catastrophe arises, these seeds can be then used to regrow the plants and provide the human population with food.

**Mezhgorye is a place on this planet:**

It is one of the closed towns, situated in the Republic of Bashkortostan, Russia. People living in this area are on a mission and work on highly classified secret programs around the mountains of [Mount Yamantaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Yamantau). Also, no one is allowed here.

**The North Sentinel Island in Andaman:**

North Sentinel is one of the most isolated places on this earth. It has houses of the most dangerous tribe in the world which is known as the Sentinelese tribe. This tribe does not believe in much human interaction and has rejected people from the outside world. In 2006, this tribe even killed a fisherman who illegally wanted to enter this region. The people belonging to this region are very hostile and due to this, they are considered one of the most dangerous people in the world.

**We are sure, you have never heard about this Snake Island in São Paulo:**

This place is popularly known as snake island. Scientists have estimated that more than 4000 snakes live here. This also means that you can witness one snake for every six square yards. Also, this is the home of the golden lancehead, which is one of the most venomous vipers in the world.

**Let's talk about Room 39, North Korea:**

It is one of the most difficult places to get in. It is a mysterious North Korean government facility that is a center for many illegal operations. Also, many believe that it is the major reason for Kim Jong's continued power.

**Last but not the least, the Church of Our Lady Mary of Zion:**

This place is situated in Ethiopia. It has a structure that contains some of the most important biblical objects such as the original Ark of the Covenant. Due to this, no one can visit this place.
