**Do 11:11 wishes come true?**

Many people consider this, if they glance at the clock and see 11:11, then their wishes come true. There are different theories surrounding this divinity and the factor of luck associated with it.

**Why is 11:11 so special?**

New age philosophers and numerologists say that the events linked to the time 11:11 are by coincidence. While, some believe that it is an auspicious sign, others think that there is a presence of spirit during this time. Also, babies born at this time get special attention from the media.

**The meaning of 11:11:**

It is considered that wishes made at 11:11 get manifested easily. The number associated with it is linked to revelation and intuition. Also, this number carries many psychic vibrations, thus giving people psychic awareness.

**Is the number 11 lucky?**

Number 11 is associated with angels. It is considered that when angles send messages containing the master number 11, they are sending encouragement for you. This number is associated with principles of spiritual enlightenment.

People also say that when they see the time 11:11, they should stop working and realize the significance of the moment. People can recognize this moment in their own way. It needs not to be just the digital clock, but there can be a number plate, street number, or any such thing that shows the significance of this combination.

**11:11 is the manifestation time:**

The number 11 is evident with [manifestation](https://malpaper.com/blogs/news/the-science-behind-manifestation-why-journaling-for-manifestation-works) powers. So, be sure to pay attention to your own thoughts. People heavily emphasize thinking positively, especially during 11:11 and on Nov 11. It is because it is quite possible that you just have to go through the power of this timing and make certain things happen. Also, be sure to focus on the things you'd like to make happen and not what you are afraid of happening. Just think positive during this time and seize opportunities if you do.

1111 is a spiritual number. **What do you see at 11:11?**

1.) Be mindful of your thoughts: This is a prime manifestation time. So, never dwell on what could go wrong. Instead, look forward to the possibilities you are excited about and do so with non-attachment to the outcome.

2.) Be open and receptive: Life is full of opportunities. So, through all your hardships and celebrations, be open to the opportunities destiny might have for you.

3.) Asking for guidance: If you are stuck, ask for help. There is shame in doing that. Listen to your intuition, it has so much to say.
