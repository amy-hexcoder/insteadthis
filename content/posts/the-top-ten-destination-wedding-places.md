So, you have made it past the awkward [date](/stories/basic-first-date-etiquette-rules-everyone-should-follow), cracked the meeting with parents, and dealt with the nerve-wracking proposal. Your partner has said 'yes' and all that is left to organize is the wedding. You always wanted your wedding on the swaying palms on a secluded beach, in the allure of a stress-free day courtesy of wedding planners and the scenic backdrop of mountains. That is pure magic! Destination weddings spread every bit of magic. Guests get a fabulous vacation, and you take back home a pool of wonderful memories. So, to ensure that your D-Day aligns with the fairytale future in front of you, here are the top wedding destinations to plan your special day.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/11-4.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**1.)   SANTORINI: Destination that spells magic!**

For a destination that matches seamlessly with any bride's beautiful white dress, the Greek island of [Santorini](https://santorinisecrets.com/trip-to-santorini/#:~:text=If%20you%20live%20anywhere%20outside,ferry%20from%20Athens%20to%20Santorini).) speaks in volume. At this location, the whitewashed houses cling to the breathtaking cliffs. Also, the famous cobalt blue roofs amalgamate with the glittering ocean. For something unique, head to this mesmerizing location and let your destination wedding do all the talking.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/SANTO-1024x640.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**2.)   Tuscany: Experience heaven on earth!**

At the heart of Italy lies the most beautiful region of Tuscany. It is one of the dream destinations on the wishlist of every couple who is planning for their destination wedding. Tuscany's capital Florence is home to the world's most famous artists. This place is known for its romantic and serene atmosphere. With endless fields of stunning vineyards, this picture-perfect destination transports your guests to a world of romance and tranquillity.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/TUS-1024x681.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**3.)   Tenerife, Spain: As beautiful as its name!**

The serene deep colors of the sea surrounding Tenerife are enchanting. With year-round mild temperatures, this is one of the largest paradises on the mesmerizing European land. It has stunning landscapes, seaside luxury hotels, and Michelin-starred cuisines. The island is accessible and offers a beautiful backdrop for your [pre-wedding photoshoots](https://www.canvera.com/blog/pre-wedding-shoot-important/). The resorts here offer a range of wedding packages to add glitters to your D-Day.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/SPA.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**4.)   Paris: Love in the air of Paris!**

Romance always lingers in the air in France's capital city, Paris. It has always been synonymous with love, making it a dream destination for fiancés seeking to create an unforgettable wedding. With stunning artistic and architectural wonders, lush gardens, the Eiffel Tower, this city is a perfect place to host your dream wedding. Whether you opt for a daylight ceremony or a nighttime event, this place is a timeless classic for your wedding nuptials.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/PARI.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**5.)   Lucerne, Switzerland: Celebrating the beauty of love!**

Celebrate your love in one of the most scenic locations in [Switzerland](https://traveltriangle.com/blog/switzerland/). Chapel Bridge is one of the oldest wooden bridges in Europe, where pre-wedding celebrations take place. You can then exchange your vows at the Chapel of Castle Meggenhorn, a picture-perfect destination located above 6000 feet above sea level.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/SWISS.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**6.)   Lake Placid, New York: Adventures speak in volume!**

This alpine village is loaded with loads of activities and attractions and snow-capped settings. It gets backed with mountain vistas and beautiful hills. So, just leave the worries at home and choose a venue from the stunning resorts and grand lakeside lodges. You can later incorporate a gondola ride for your dream wedding. You can also opt for the hot-air balloon for your "Yes, I do!" moments. The region gets dotted with 3000 lakes and mountains with waterfalls that come to life in the spring, providing you with a picture-perfect background for your wedding nuptials.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/ny.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**7.)   US: Kauai, Hawaii: Weather beautified!**

Hawaii is known as the "Garden Isle" due to its incredible landscape and beautiful looking secluded spots. It remains the least developed land, and life here moves at a slower pace. This land is ideal for your destination wedding due to its amazing weather. Your wedding shenanigans can surf, hike or take a helicopter tour. So, when you are thinking of a budget wedding, make sure that you avoid winter and early spring, when the tourist season is in full swing.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/us.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**8.)   Sri Lanka: The beauty that never leaves you disappointed!**

For a wedding surrounded by a rainbow of vibrant colors, pack your luggage to the beautiful island of Sri Lanka. It is located in the Indian Ocean, the diverse landscapes with stunning beauty. From rainforests where elephants roam freely to timeless Buddhist ruins, a wedding in [Sri Lanka](https://traveltriangle.com/blog/most-beautiful-places-in-sri-lanka/) is always memorable. Apart from this, the wildlife, sparkling oceans, and ancient temples add to the beauty of this land. So, if you are looking for a quiet and unique wedding destination, this is a standout option on my list.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/sri-1024x640.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**9.)   Banff, Canada: Marry in royalty!**

The Banff National Park is like a quintessential fairy-tale destination with chateau-style hotels and alpine scenery. It was built in Scottish baronial style in 1988. This place offers a wide variety of options for your summer mountain wedding and a selection of lounges, ballrooms, and dining rooms. A surrounding parkland is a good place for wildlife viewing and boutique shops with fine dining.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/banff.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

**10.) French Riviera, Wrapped in royalty and beauty!**

One of the most popular resort destinations, the French Riviera was once a favorite vacation spot in the 19th century. Today, this giant resort is home to celebrities like Brigitte Bardot and Elton John. So, if you envision lush forests, sparkling clear blue water, breathtaking cliffs, then this is your ideal destination to marry. If you are planning your dream wedding on the French Riviera, the Rothschild Villa is your dream venue to take your wedding nuptials.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/french.jpg)
  ***Credits@GoogleImages***

If you are looking for a wedding on a beach or a chapel, here is the list of options. Find the best wedding destination and take home a bunch of happy memories with you.
