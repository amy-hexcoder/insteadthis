QUESTION

Hi Petrica!

What's up with you? How are you preparing for Valentine's Day? My boyfriend and I are going on a romantic getaway for two days. As I have already learned a lot about [manicures](/stories/how-to-give-yourself-a-mani-like-a-pro-at-home) and [pedicures](/stories/get-happy-and-healthy-feet-with-a-pedicure-for-dry-cracked-feet) from your posts, I need your help finding that gorgeous nail polish. I'm sure you have some stunning Valentine's Day nail polish ideas, so let me know what you have.

Love,

Josie!

ANSWER

Hi Josie!

You're a sweetheart to me. With Valentine's Day looming closer, many women plan their dream day around this time of year. Getting my nails done for this holiday is one of my favorite ways to be all out without looking cheesy. I took a look at the Pinterest archives and gathered the hottest Valentine's Day nail designs for you to try out! You'll find pink nails to hearts with a variety of colors. I'm excited to try out some [Valentine's Day nails](https://www.elle.com/uk/beauty/nails/articles/g31372/valentines-day-nail-art-ideas-inspiration/) as the holiday draws closer! So, let's jump right in.

## Heart-Shaped Cut-Outs

You don't have to choose between French tips or festive hearts. Let your heart be the perfect one. Make the heart's top by connecting two dots. Fill in the V below the dots with a thin detail brush. I think this could work as an icon for two people in love. So sweet.

![](/images/uploads/2022/01/bycheznails20valentines20day20nails.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## Let's Get Grooved

Retro vibes are evident here, highlighted by the use of cupid's favorite colors. Create the swirl using a detailer brush and red polish. Over it, trace pink, coral, and purple shades keeping the same width. These nails are just gorgeous!

![](/images/uploads/2022/01/dissey20valentines20nails.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## The Rainbow Heart Tips

No one says you have to paint your nails red and pink just because it's Valentine's Day. This whimsical design is easily re-created at home using colors you already own. Apply a nude or ivory nail polish to your nails, and then use a stencil to create heart tips. To prevent chips and add shine, apply a glossy topcoat.

![](/images/uploads/2022/01/valentines20day20nail20art20ideas20rainbow20heart20tips-1024x1024.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## A Mix of Shiny and Matte

Valentine's Day nails may be red or pink, but some unexpected combinations create super-cute nails. As for the nails with the pale, matte pink color and those with the glossy red color, use the shade Fire red gel polish. A sleek and ultra-minimal version of the classic love heart design.

![](/images/uploads/2022/01/valentines20day20nails20Nikoleta20Szalak-1024x1024.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## Pink and White Lips

I love how this flirty manicure looks by painting the nails long, squared off, and adding cute little lips to four of them. It's high romance. Pair these super feminine nails with your lips.

![](/images/uploads/2022/01/valentines20day20nails20claw20mafia-1024x1024.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## Valentine's Candy

Valentine's Day can't get any sweeter than giving or receiving candies! It's easy to create this look! To make it, draw the lines with a toothpick on a white base! Although this is a pretty, pastel interpretation of classic messages, there are no limitations.

![](/images/uploads/2022/01/e75e62bf0da013553e8db1679f894237.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## Pair and Mix

Can't decide which classic symbol to use for Valentine's Day? A red heart and a black "XO" sit on a white base, along with two creamy, pale pink nails.

![](/images/uploads/2022/01/valentines20day20nails20ashley20brimer-1.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## Popping Hearts

There's no better way to spend Valentine's Day than with this festive option. On the pinkies, apply pink and white gel polishes loosely. Mix the colors gently with a dotting tool to create the marble effect. Using a liner brush and the same dotting tool, create a red border around the pink nails and the little hearts. Adding a pop of color to your Valentine's Day design is sure to catch everyone's eye.

![](/images/uploads/2022/01/valentines20day20nail20art20ideas20jelly20hearts-1024x1024.webp)
*Credits@Google*

## Metallic Love

Being a minimalist and passionate about neutrals, I always find it striking when nails are white and gold. The trend of pastel pinks and metallics is just as charming. You can easily recreate this look at home using a nude base, white nail polish, and gold chrome pigment.

![](/images/uploads/2022/01/valentines20day20nail20art20ideas20metallic20hearts-1024x1024.webp)
*Credits@Google*

Some of these nail art ideas are easy enough to do yourself, making them even more exciting. I'd love to hear your favorites. I hope you have a wonderful Valentine's Day!
