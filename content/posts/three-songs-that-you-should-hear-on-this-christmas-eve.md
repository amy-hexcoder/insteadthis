1. **"All I Want for Christmas Is You" by Mariah Carey:**

Mariah Carey's iconic Christmas anthem is a must-listen during the holiday season. Its catchy melody and festive lyrics make it a timeless classic that never fails to bring a joyful and festive atmosphere.

2. **"Last Christmas" by Wham!:**

This '80s hit by Wham! has become a holiday staple. The upbeat rhythm and heartfelt lyrics about love and heartbreak during the Christmas season make it a perfect addition to any festive playlist.

3. **"Jingle Bell Rock" by Bobby Helms:**

"Jingle Bell Rock" is a lively and upbeat Christmas song that adds a rock and roll twist to the holiday spirit. Its cheerful tune and energetic vibe make it an excellent choice for spreading Christmas cheer.

These songs capture the essence of Christmas with their festive tunes and lyrics, making them essential additions to your holiday playlist.
