- **"[The Silent Patient" by Alex Michaelides](https://www.amazon.in/Silent-Patient-Alex-Michaelides/dp/1409181634):** This psychological thriller follows the story of Alicia Berenson, a famous painter who suddenly stops speaking after she is accused of murdering her husband. Theo Faber, a psychotherapist, becomes obsessed with uncovering the truth behind Alicia's silence and delves deep into her mysterious past, leading to shocking revelations.
- **"The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo" by Stieg Larsson:** This gripping novel introduces readers to the enigmatic Lisbeth Salander, a brilliant hacker with a troubled past, and Mikael Blomkvist, an investigative journalist. When the two join forces to solve a decades-old disappearance, they uncover a complex web of corruption, conspiracy, and violence that puts their lives at risk.
- "**Gone Girl" by Gillian Flynn:** In this chilling psychological thriller, Nick Dunne's wife, Amy, goes missing on their fifth wedding anniversary, and all signs point to foul play. As the media frenzy escalates and suspicions mount, Nick finds himself at the center of a twisted game of deception and manipulation. Told from alternating perspectives, "Gone Girl" keeps readers guessing until the shocking conclusion.

These books are sure to keep you on the edge of your seat with their suspenseful plots, complex characters, and unexpected twists and turns. Happy reading!
