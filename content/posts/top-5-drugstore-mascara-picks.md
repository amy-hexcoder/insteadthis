While most women started their love affair with [mascaras](https://www.byrdie.com/best-mascaras-4159378) in their early teens, makeup today knows no gender. [Makeup](https://www.glamour.com/gallery/beauty-awards-makeup-winners) is a powerful tool that allows self-expression. But with changing times we are seeing, and we hope it continues, more and more men are exploring their relationship with makeup. Many K-pop stars, including Taeyong, talk about how makeup instantly changes their mood and boosts their confidence on screen.

There is a lot to explore in makeup, from shimmery eyeshadows to the perfectly-pointed winged eyeliner. But one thing that most people begin with is mascara. It makes sense because it is the most user-friendly product out there. Unlike foundation or eyeshadow that requires some amount of perfection with blending, mascaras are easy to use. You do not need to be an expert or professional to use this product. So, whether you are going for a no-makeup makeup look or full glam makeup, mascara is your go-to product.

Even if you are not makeup-obsessed, you cannot miss out on wearing mascara. People have different wants and needs when it comes to mascaras. Some look for volume and thickness, while some others want length and curl for their lashes.

But who does not want a mascara that gives luscious, thick, long, and [voluminous lashes](https://www.cosmopolitan.com/style-beauty/beauty/g15773998/best-drugstore-makeup-brands-products/)? Right?

However, finding out the perfect mascara can get overwhelming with the multiple options available in the market. Also, mascaras have the shortest shelf life of all the beauty products, making them quite heavy on your pocket. But you need not worry because drugstore mascaras with the best formulas are here to the rescue. So, we have come up with a list of the top 5 [drugstore mascaras](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com/beauty/makeup/tips/g2329/best-drugstore-cosmetics/) that can make your lash dreams come true.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/kaboompics_The-woman-is-applying-mascara-on-her-eyelashes-1-683x1024.jpg)

*Credits @kaboompics*

1. **Milani Highly Rated - 10-in-1 Volume Mascara.**

This mascara tops our list for its affordable price range, as well as the best formula that lifts your lashes and gives you dramatic sky-high lashes. The brush of this mascara holds the perfect amount of product that gives you perfectly elongated and intense lashes with a single coat. However, for a more dramatic look, you can go on for a second coat without the fear of clumping your lashes. The tapered end of the wand gives you a sleek application, separating every individual lash.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/1-4.jpg)

*Credits @Google Images*

It has a long-lasting formula that will make you look good throughout the day without flaking your lashes. But you can take it off easily with a makeup remover.

It has a rich, creamy formula that glides smoothly from the roots to the tips of your lashes. It is a plant-based product which makes it cruelty-free. It is also paraben-free, alcohol-free, and has no added perfume.

So if you are looking for a mascara that gives you intensely defined, darker lashes, you can go for this mascara.

**2. L'Oreal Paris Voluminous Lash Paradise Mascara**

The second product on our list is another affordable and popular mascara. We are talking about the 'L'Oreal Paris Voluminous Lash Paradise Mascara. It comes in gorgeous rose-gold packaging with a thick brush that defines every individual lash. It gives you sleek lines and smooth application. The hourglass shape of this brush delivers the right curl, volume, and length to your lashes.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/3-4.jpg)

*Credits @Google Images*

It has a volumizing and lengthening formula that provides thick and soft fringes without any clumping of the lashes. This mascara gives you full coverage that lasts all day long. So if you are looking for a good mascara that gives you full, long lashes, this is your pick.

Experts suggest combining this product with the Voluminous Lash Paradise primer. They are said to make the ultimate combo together. It gives you the appearance of wearing falsies without getting into the mess of glue.

So if you want those super soft, feathery lashes, make sure to add this one to your kit!

**3.  Covergirl Lash Blast Active Mascara**

The next mascara on the list is the most reliable and affordable among all other mascaras. A single stroke of this is enough to lengthen and separate your lashes.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/4-2-1024x1024.jpg)

*Credits @Google Images*

It comes in colorful packaging with an easy-going wand. It has sturdy plastic bristles, which are shorter than the nylon ones of a classic mascara. It requires you to be more patient and precise with its application. But the wand is designed to provide easy application. Also, a less amount of product is sufficient for smooth strokes. It gives you an instant blast of volume and length.

It has a long-lasting formula to give you beautiful lashes without flaking or clumping.

This mascara comes in four different variants - Black, Very Black, Extreme black, and Black-Brown. You can pick your shade according to your preference.

**4.  Revlon Volumazing Mascara**

The next one is Revlon Volumazing Mascara. It has four color variants - Black (902), Blackened Brown (903), Blackest Black (901), and Blackest Black Waterproof (951).

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/7-1-1024x576.jpg)

*Credits @Goggle Images*

It comes in easy-to-use packaging that fits just right in your hands.

The wand of this mascara is slightly wavy in shape. It allows you to smoothly glide up from the bottom to the tips of your lashes. The bristles are designed in a way to give you a fanned-out look within seconds.

The rich, creamy formula ensures to stay on your lashes throughout the day without flaking or smudging.

Make sure to take a smaller amount of product while applying. It has more of a wetter consistency than other mascaras. So it requires you to be slow and precise with the application to avoid the product on the skin. If you want an extra blast, take a clear and run through your lashes. It gives a more curled and lifted effect to your lashes.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/6-3-1024x1024.jpg)

*Credits @Google Images*

**5.Rimmel Lash Accelerator Mascara**

Our final pick is the Rimmel Lash Accelerator Mascara. With an affordable price and good quality, this product allows smooth application with a single coat. You can surely go for a second coat as it does not clump or tangle your lashes. Instead, it gives up long, beautiful, and luscious lashes. In comparison to other mascaras, this one takes a little longer to dry. It requires you to be patient between layers of coats.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/08/Rimmel-Lash-Accelerator-Endless-Mascara-1024x682.jpg)

*Credits @Google Images*

The outer packaging is kept simple with a gold cap and black body. It has a sleek body that gives you a good hold for clean and fuzz-free application.

Enriched with the benefits of Procapil, this mascara claims to enhance the length of your lashes within 30 days. If you are looking for a good mascara that treats your lashes, this has to be your pick.

**So there you go!**

**Now you have a list of the five best drugstore mascaras to ditch those high-end ones. Save all that money and go ahead with your favorite one as per your preference. Now you can let your eyes do all the talking!**
