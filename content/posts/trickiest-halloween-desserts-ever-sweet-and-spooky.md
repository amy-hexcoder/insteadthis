Want to host a kickass party? You've got to have these [spooky treats](https://www.delish.com/holiday-recipes/halloween/g314/halloween-party-treats/) on the menu! Make the witchiest night of the year even better with these treats! Here are some creepy yet sweet ideas. The scarily cute party favors are perfect for Halloween parties with witches and [monsters](https://mozartcultures.com/en/japanese-ghosts-night-parade-101/) [dressed](/stories/most-bizarre-outfits-you-shouldnt-miss-this-halloween) up! You can't help but marvel at the look and taste of these Halloween desserts.
Ready to celebrate Halloween in a fun and bizarre way? Here is how!

## Cookie-crawling Spider

I love how easy it is to make these spider cookies. A show stopper at a Halloween party! These spider cookies won't scare even the most arachnophobic's.
As an added delight, sugar cookies were topped with chocolate dough and decorated with chocolate spider legs. Those creepy big-eyed crawlers are adorable! Only these spiders I would eat gladly.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/Halloween-Peanut-Spider-Cookies_EXPS_FT20_257512_F_0811_1.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

## Baked Eyeballs

If you want to impress your guests but lack creativity or baking skills. This recipe is perfect for you. I'll tell you why.
First and foremost, they're adorable! Everyone would love to own them.
Second, these bite-sized cakes are incredibly easy to make! Dunk these cake balls in melted colored chocolate and decorate them.
It's fun to dress them up! Let your imagination run wild when choosing the colors for the chocolate and sprinkles.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/Cake-Eyeballs_EXPS_TOHON19_161701_E06_11_6b-1.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

## Jack-o'-Lantern Cake

Do you want to add a touch of fall to your Halloween treats? This gap-toothed grinning monster made from two fluted tube pancakes is pieced together and topped with an ice cream cone. This centerpiece will be the hit of your Halloween party. The Jack-o'-lantern-shaped cake looks both adorable and spooky. A must-have this Halloween!

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/exps192501_UH153291D05_13_4b-2.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

## Pumpkin Coffin Cake

This spooky cake tastes delicious because of the maple flavoring in the frosting. It looks like a pine box with the gingersnap crumbs. Perfectly appropriate for the season.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/Coffin-Pumpkin-Cake_EXPS_PCBZ18_40288_B04_27_3b-2.jpg)

## A Witch's Fingers

These cookies are sometimes also referred to as severed finger cookies or ladies' finger cookies. I haven't seen anything more gruesome than this version! Cookies made from green sugar cookie dough are molded into fingers with wrinkly knuckles. This spooky treat is easy to make.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/58100a0d67c18b3fa45a41a5b640daa6.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

## Bones of the Meringue

People will be amazed at how easy it is! You'll most likely receive requests for the recipe! Egg whites, lemon juice, and granulated sugar are the only three ingredients needed for these simple bone-shaped cookies. These simple meringue bones make an adorable party decoration as well as a tasty treat all in one. The perfect treat!

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/e5bb274c4d28d7bb1fc6122ca438acf3.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

## Cookies for Death Day

You can make these cookies for any special occasion. For spring, cut them into flower shapes and insert a lollipop stick into each to make bouquets. Try your hand at making this Day of the Dead version using candy, food coloring, and your imagination this Halloween.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/Day-of-the-Dead-Cookies_exps158027_UH2464847C03_20_5b_RMS.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

## Watching you!

Keep an eye out! The crazy cupcake is watching you closely. However, don't worry, the cupcake is delicious! A combination of vanilla and chocolate cupcakes is the ultimate treat. M&M Mini goodness is oozing from these soft, chewy, marshmallowy treats.

## Bites the Boo

I love these little boo bites!
They are shortbread cookies garnished with colorful sugar pearls. They're so simple yet so delicious. The festive nonpareils and crumbly shortbread give these cookies a Halloween vibe.

## Sugar Cookie Swirls

Candy corn-flavored cookies - how gorgeous! Instagram-worthy! Colorful swirls aren't the only thing that makes these cookies irresistible. They also have mouthwatering flavors and textures. You'll love how soft and buttery they are and almost melt in your mouth when you bite into them.

## House of Horrors

This spooky showstopper is quite beautiful, and the color scheme and decoration are superb. It is decorated with sprinkles and candy, making it a unique treat. You can make this silly haunted house with fudgy brownies. Halloween trick-or-treaters beware!

## Cake with Brains

Science museums will be delighted to have this exhibit. Think how surprised your guests will be when they bite into these white (or gray-colored) cakes and find bright red, cherry-scented (faux) blood dripping from them. Geez! What a creepy idea!

## Yummy Marshmallows

The leftovers can serve as favors wrapped in cellophane. Pour melted candy over marshmallows and dredge in colored sugar. The colors make these perfect for Halloween. So cute!

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/72be61c957e0462437d4ca59bf9fa21d.jpg)

## Dessert in a Cemetery

You'll find a delicious chocolate cake prepared from scratch in a few simple steps underneath the tombstones, ghouls, pumpkins, bugs, and other details that make this dessert stand out. On gravestones, you can either write "RIP" or come up with a creative name. This Halloween dessert is like no other!

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/10/exps9298_UH153291C04_28_2b-1.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

From cookies to cakes to cupcakes, Halloween treats are the ideal match for your haunted [costumes](/stories/most-bizarre-outfits-you-shouldnt-miss-this-halloween) and [pranks](/stories/youre-sure-to-go-bonkers-with-these-maddening-pranks). Treat your guests to these festive treats at your big party or pass them out to trick-or-treaters. These Halloween-ey desserts are perfect for getting in the Halloween spirit!
