## WIDE LEG RESORT EASY PLAY JUMPSUIT

This AKIRA Label [Resort Easy Play Wide Leg Jumpsuit](https://www.shopakira.com/whats-new/easy-play-wide-leg-jumpsuit) features an exquisite chiffon fabrication. It has an open back, a button-down top neckline, and an elasticized backband. These wide-leg pants also have a tie waist detail, adding a touch of elegance to the ensemble.

![](/images/uploads/2022/03/resort-easy-play-wide-leg-jumpsuit_green_4_4-678x1024.jpg)
*Credits@Akira*

![](/images/uploads/2022/03/resort-easy-play-wide-leg-jumpsuit_green_10_10-678x1024.jpg)
*Credits@Akira*

**Features:**

- Fabric: 100% Polyester, 100% Rayon
- Care: Hand wash only
- Dimensions: 60" neck to hem (approximately)
- Size: Small

**I thought:**
A relaxed fit and trans-seasonal vibe make this wide-leg jumpsuit perfect for any occasion. Combine this piece with strappy sandals and a sun hat for a colorful and chic vacation look.
