## TYPES OF SLING BAGS

### THE PRINT POWER

##### Printed sling bags are a great way of making your dull outfit come to life. It helps you make a strong styling statement effortlessly. These sling bags can be paired with a pair of denim and a graphic t-shirt or a crop top. To complete this look, pair it with a pair of printed sneakers for your college appearance. The printed sling bag carries your daily essentials and ups your fashion game too!

### THE TASSEL TRENDS

![](/images/uploads/2021/09/112-1024x1024.jpg)

##### Tasseled sling bags are always trendy. They help to bring out your inner [bohemian](https://www.thepearlsource.com/blog/bohemian-style-ultimate-guide/) spirit. A tasseled sling bag with a floral printed flowy maxi dress will draw a stylish picture for your summer wears. You can wear bangles and a statement neckpiece to add to the boho vibes. Lastly, finish off the look with a pair of slider sandals that makes you look mesmerizing.

### THE PASTEL STATEMENT

##### Pastel-hued sling bags are must-haves in your spring wardrobe. You can pair a [pastel](https://www.shutterstock.com/blog/use-pastel-colors-designs) sling bag with a dress in a sorbet shade. This is going to make you rock the pastel trend like a pro. You can also carry a powder blue sling bag with a baby pink dress for your lunch date. To complete your look, sport a pair of peep-toes and a layered neckpiece and be ready to woo your date with your stylish look.

## REASONS TO LOVE SLING BAGS

#### THEY ARE HANDS-FREE

In today's evolving times, women are multi-taskers. In that case, your handbags should not be a part of your juggling act, they should be hands-free, allowing you to keep all your essentials.

#### THEY ARE COMPACT

We need a handbag that helps in carrying all our essentials. They should also help you survive an apocalypse. The problem is when we have space in a handbag, we use it and have to bear the consequences of a lob-sided posture. So, in that case, use a sling bag that is compact and restricts you to carry only absolute necessities.

#### THEY ARE VERSATILE

Sling bags are not only quirky or casual but are also designed for every occasion. Your sling bags are good for the office and it doubles your night-life companion.

#### THEY ARE EASY TO CARRY

Sling bags are best. They can be used as a quintessential accessory to any outfit. So, if you are not the one who loves wearing much jewelry or sparkle, then you can pair your outfit with a glittery sling bag or a sling bag. Always choose an eye-catching sling bag. Animal print, bright patterns, and oversized bows look eye-catching.

#### THEY ARE EFFORTLESSLY STYLISH

Sling bags are fashionable and comfortable. They provide us with a well-rounded trendsetting look. They are effortlessly stylish and comfortable. You can pair take your sling bag for a shopping date or a picnic, it always looks great.

There are sling bags that are perfect for every occasion and having these five sling bags in your wardrobe is going to solve your problems.
