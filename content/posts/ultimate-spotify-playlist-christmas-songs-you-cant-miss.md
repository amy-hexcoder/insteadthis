The holiday season isn't complete without a favorite Christmas hymn, but boy, there are lots of them! Whether you enjoy classical music or modern renditions, Spotify has it all. My favorite song ever is "All I Want for Christmas Is You" by Mariah Carey, hands down. It is culturally indelible!

Let's not wait any longer! Make your personalized holiday playlist with these popular and well-known tunes, and [keep the party](/stories/december-festive-activities-holiday-glee-in-full-swing) [going](/stories/december-festive-activities-holiday-glee-in-full-swing) all season.

## White Christmas

Originally from the movie [White Christmas](https://www.google.com/search?q=bing+crosby+white+christmas&stick=H4sIAAAAAAAAAONgFuLRT9c3LDTNMjcqSs9W4gXxDNOMzLIMzU3StLSyk630c0uLM5P1i1KT84tSMvPS45NzSotLUous8ksyUosUylKLijPz84oXsUonAWUVkovyi5MqFcozMktSFZIzijKLS3ITiwF9ueOBaQAAAA&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwimmdWW1tb0AhWx8XMBHQ2MD98Qri56BAgREAo), this is a classic Christmas song. We all dream of white Christmases, don't we? It is an original Bing Crosby song from that legendary movie, and I like that version better than any other. I hope your holidays be cheery and festive!

## Santa Claus is Coming To Town

Especially around Christmas time, this [song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HSmsq2iq4bQ) reminds me that I am a child at heart and should treat others with kindness. People who have just lost a loved one or don't have any family nearby may find it difficult during this time. Be sure to reach out to those who seem lonely.

## Have Yourself A Merry Little Christmas

I love [Ingrid's rendition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I6BI0dGPFWI) of this classic Christmas song. My heart melts whenever I hear it because Michaelson's voice is so delicate and fits well with the acoustic music. Additionally, the holidays remind us to put troubles aside and savor the company of our loved ones.

## Whatever Happened To Christmas?

[Frank Sinatra's](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HO6TAu-6S-4) Christmas song is one of my favorites. It evokes all the positive attributes of Christmas: the magic, the giving spirit, and the pleasant sights and sounds. The song's lyrics express that it's a heartfelt plea to enjoy and cherish the Christmas holidays and keep them close to your heart as you embark on the new year.

## Do You Hear What I Hear?

The chorus of this holiday song is ideal sung by a choir. All in all, the [song's sound](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t84OSRahBRo) is quite magical, with so many voices!

## I’ll Be Home For Christmas

For college students living far from their families over the holidays, this is a perfect song to [play](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tItGjgFZpjk). Christmas finds you here where the light of love gleans.

## Christmas Memories

I'm sure you'll understand why I love this [song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGsZnr1vH5I) once you hear it. It releases a ton of nostalgia in me to play in the snow, sing carols, and string popcorn. These things brighten my mood every year!

## Silent Night

[Silent Night](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nEH7_2c644Q) is a classic, isn't it? Especially when sung by the choir at church, this song is one of my favorites for the holidays. I love this song because it reminds me of the magic behind Christmas every year.

## Frosty The Snowman

It's like my childhood is surfacing again! The tune always reminds me of a cute cartoon. Be sure to put it on your Christmas [playlist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k6zW225k_O0).

## All I Want For Christmas Is You

Here's a [heartfelt song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aAkMkVFwAoo) for all the lonely people out there who want to spend the holiday season with someone. Whatever stage you're at in your relationship or how long you've been apart, we can all relate! After all, who does not want a loving partner at Christmas?

## Angels We Have Heard On High

A must-have for your Spotify Christmas music playlist is [Andrea Bocelli](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B5iaV989_5M). Known for his work with other artists worldwide, he has performed Angels We Have Heard On High like no other artist. At the highest volume, of course, I enjoy singing this at midnight mass on Christmas Eve with my siblings.

## Santa Tell Me

I am in awe of [Ariana Grande](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nlR0MkrRklg). Her song is so catchy you'll be humming it for hours. It's the perfect addition to your holiday playlist. Absolutely nothing can match it.

## Mistletoe

Oh, how sweet. Bieber Baby! [Justin Bieber](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUjn3RpkcKY) released a holiday album in 2011, which is pretty typical for a teen pop idol. Mistletoe made all the girls swoon, including me. I feel young and fresh in the corner of my eyes while listening to it.

## Holidays (feat. Earth, Wind & Fire)

It is a track by Meghan Trainor, a hymn to heavenly [music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2nzAxt27aDI). I am deeply smitten by her look and by the video's aesthetics. My gosh, how I wish I could be an ornament now!

## Christmas Without You

It is her songs that make me slavish to [Ava Max](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L1KEJzfsQEY). Every time one of her songs plays, I simply can't help but dance. She's bringing Christmas pop back to life, and I'm eternally grateful. The tune is upbeat, bouncy, and retro-influenced.

Is there a Christmas song you like that I missed? With these top Spotify tracks, you will be able to curate your [dreamy playlist](/stories/sad-songs-that-heal-your-broken-soul) in no time. Get a glass of eggnog and enjoy the holiday fête while you listen to your favorite holiday tunes. Don't forget to share this playlist with your BFFs!
