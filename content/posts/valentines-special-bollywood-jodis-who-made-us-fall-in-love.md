February, a month of love; a month for lovers, and a month to make the craziest thing happen because **#LOVE IS IN THE AIR…**

![](/images/uploads/2022/02/mo.webp)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**“Kuch kuch hota hai Anjali… tum nahi samjogi,   Tum oxygen aur main double hydrogen ... humari chemistry ekdum pani ki tarah hai”…** I love how these Bollywood dialogues have become a part of our lives. Not only these dialogues, but even our Bollywood jodis made us fall in love with their exotic onscreen chemistry. So, on this Valentine’s month, let’s talk about our Bollywood couples who made us believe in love!

**1.) [Romantic Raj Kapoor and noor-e-Nargis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oXLzfldeDcM&ab_channel=Shemaroo) under one umbrella-**

*( Pyaar hua ikrar hua hai, pyaar se fir kyu darta hai dil….)*

*( Pyaar hua ikrar hua hai, pyaar se fir kyu darta hai dil….)*

This famous Jodi made us believe in magical love. Every time it rains, a Romeo in every heart wants to take his Juliet under the same umbrella and romance like Raj Kapoor and Nargis. That's exactly why the old-school romance will never go outdated.

![](/images/uploads/2022/02/1M.jpg)
***Credits@Sa re ga ma Carvaan***

**2.) Radiant Rekha and angry young man Amitabh in snowy Shimla-**

*( [Dekha ek khwab toh yeh silsilee hue, door tak nigaahon mein hain gul khile huye](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dO_MS9tZ5E&ab_channel=YRF)…)*

Indeed one of my mother’s favorite couples from yesteryears. This couple dates back to you in the era where love was so divine and so sparkling. The intensity of their chemistry spilled the beans of love in every young heart. Their ‘reel and real love’ was the showbiz for their fans. Rekha’s dark lipstick shades and Amitabh’s simple gestures make us go “AWW” even today…

![](/images/uploads/2022/02/2M.jpg)
***Credits@Sa re ga ma carvaan***

**3.) Haseena Hema Malini and Dashing Dharmendra’s romantic journey on a carriage-**

*([Koi haseena jab rooth jaati hai toh, aur bhi hasin ho jaati hai…](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Wf398_7shk&ab_channel=ShemarooFilmiGaane))*

This couple’s love story is nothing less than a sizzling story that ruled many hearts. Actor Dharmendra and actress Hema Malini fell in love on the sets of 'Tum Haseen Main Jawan'. Their reel to real journey was and is still liked by everyone. This couple was so much in love with each other that Hema turned down many marriage proposals from many superstars. Hema’s winged eyeliner and Dharmendra’s shararti mizaaz make us go “AWW” even today…

![](/images/uploads/2022/02/3M.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**4.) Mesmerising Madhuri Dixit and Shy Salman’s love quoting “Kyu…hum aapke hai kaun?”-**

*([Pehla pehla pyaar hai, pehli pehli baar hai](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gIODEFkvjTA&ab_channel=Rajshri)…)*

Aww, Madhuri Dixit’s smile creates Tsunamis in many innocent hearts even today. After the magical impact of this movie, I always wished that this ‘reel’ COUPLE of Bollywood could be a real-life couple too. But, anyways their charm won many hearts, and I am sure not only I, many more like me also wished the same. Salman as Prem in this movie was a heartthrob for many girls. Madhuri’s Pink tunic Saturn dress and Salman’s shy smile make us go “AWW” even today…

![](/images/uploads/2022/02/4m.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**5.) Killer Kajol and Spunky Shahrukh Khan are always ‘great’ out of those good couples-**

***([Tuzhe dekha toh yeh jana sanam, pyaar hota hai dewana sanam](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cNV5hLSa9H8&ab_channel=YRF)…)***

Hands down to the most lovable couple of the Bollywood! This magical duo made people believe that they can fall in love on a train, in a mustard field, or in Switzerland. This couple made their fans fall in love with the narration that “A girl and guy can be best friends”, ESPECIALLY IF THEY ARE BASKETBALL LOVERS. Not only in the 90s but even today, they have set high standards for the other Bollywood celebrities. Kajol’s simple plain sarees and Shahrukh’s plain transparent shirts with classic backgrounds make us go “AWW” even today…

![](/images/uploads/2022/02/5M.webp)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**6.)Ravishing Raani Mukherjee and handsome hunk Abhishek ko “Kabhi alveda na kehna”-**

*([Kabhi Neem Neem Kabhi Shahad Shahad, Kabhi Naram Naram Kabhi Sakht](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Dh-X9oCHIY&ab_channel=IshtarMusic)…)*

Together they are ‘BUNTY AUR BABLI’ who stole hearts. The kind of superhit chemistry this couple has made me believe that love has no boundaries. Post this movie they, were a part of many super hit movies. Raani’s gracious presence and Abhishek’s beard look make us go “AWW” even today…

![](/images/uploads/2022/02/bb-1024x683.webp)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**7.) Passionate Parineeti and Shehjada Siddharth Malhotra ki patang kya solid uddi na?**

*([Zehnaseeb, Zehanaseeb, Tujhe chaahun betahasha zehnaseeb](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WnU0lH6C0EA&ab_channel=SonyMusicIndiaVEVO))*

The movie's perfect storyline ruled many hearts. Parineeti's hilarious personality and Siddharth's lovey-dovey attitude made people fall in love with them. The romantic song 'Zehnaseeb' is Valentine's day special wishlist. Even today, their fans still want them to reunite and spread their love in the air. Parineeti’s red hoodie and Siddharth’s chocolate boy looks from the movie make us go” AWW” even today.

![](/images/uploads/2022/02/7M.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**8.) Dazzling Deepika and Rockstar Ranveer ka zikra hoga ibadad ki tarah-**

*([Mera Naam Ishq, Tera Naam Ishq…Mera Naam Ishq, Tera Naam Ishq](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iHy4prQuckA&ab_channel=ShittyThoughts)…)*

This movie 'BAJIRAO MASTANI' saw the sizzling chemistry between these two superstars. This superhit couple came together for many super hit movies like 'Padmavat, Ramleela' etc. Fans always wanted them to get married in real life, and finally, it happened when these two got hitched in 2019.

![](/images/uploads/2022/02/8m.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

Valentine’s day is always incomplete without these romantic hits. So, if you are planning something different to do on this Valentine’s day, then you can surely plan to watch these romantic movies and spend your quality time together.
