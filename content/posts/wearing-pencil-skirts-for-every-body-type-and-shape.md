Hi Kiara!

I am doing great. I hope, you are fine? Well, when it comes to Pencil skirts, I believe that anybody type can easily pull off the Pencil skirts. Even I have given it a miss many times in the past. The reason was that I was too conscious of the way it looked especially, around the torso area. But I have a few pointers that have worked for me and will work for you too.

1.) So, if you have an hourglass figure,  you can choose a well-fitted skirt that follows your body's contours.

2.) When we talk about tapered skirts, they look best on women with fuller hips and thighs. You can complete the look with a [tucked-in top](https://www.missmalini.com/2020/07/29/9-easy-hacks-to-tuck-in-your-clothes-like-a-fashion-pro/) to make it look good for you.

3.) You can also add a tapered skirt if you have a straight figure. Flares and pleats look flattering. It makes you look fuller too.

4.) A broad tummy can be rectified with a skirt that has a waist belt. So, do not worry about it. It will look good on you. I hope you have got a clear answer.
