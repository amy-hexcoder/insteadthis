Is your wedding around the corner? Are you looking for the top wedding destinations here in USA and Canada? Then we are here to get you the best wedding destinations for your wedding.

The benefit of staying here in the USA is that you get so many wedding destinations that will make you go ‘Awww’. The wide variety of spectacular destinations from tropical beaches to mountains is an add-on here. Plus, there are no passports required. So, check this list out:

**1. Hawaii**

This is a lush tropical paradise that has got its personality and flavor. The main islands to visit here would be Oahu, Kauai, Maui, Molokai, and Hawaii. Oahu is one of the most populated islands and has got a relaxing beach. Kauai is good for some great outdoor adventure. So, no matter what island you choose, there is something on every island that will make you go crazy.

**2. Florida:**

This is one of the great wedding destinations because of several possibilities. Isn't it fun to get married in crystal blue waters as your stunning backdrop? There is also Disney World where you can have a wedding fit for your girl.

![](/images/uploads/2022/10/h1-1024x683.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**3. Colorado:**

If you are looking for a wedding destination with rustic mountains and a snowy backdrop, then Colorado is your destination to be. If you get married in the winter, then many ski resorts will host your event while allowing your people to have a fun-filled ski or snowboarding weekend. Also, if you get married in the spring or summer, then you will have beautiful flowering fields as your backdrop for your destination wedding.

**4. Sedona:**

With stunning orange and red rocks as your backdrop, this is one of the most beautiful wedding destinations in the world. [Sedona Arizona](https://www.sedonadestinationweddings.com/planning/weddings-in-sedona/) is a vibrant city and is known for its art. Also, it is a great location for the free spirit bride and groom who love art. With a ton to do from rock climbing classes to take a jeep tour, this exotic location has so many things to offer.

![](/images/uploads/2022/10/h2-1024x683.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**5. Las Vegas:**

Vegas is not a destination for an impromptu wedding. It is a destination for a great wedding scene. So, if you are looking to get married on the rooftop or sky-rise balcony, then this is the perfect destination for you. Also, think about doing a destination wedding in Vegas where you will be the happiest.

**6. New York:**

Are you a modern groom, who loves getting married in the land of skyscrapers? Then, this is your place to be. There are so many locations to get married in New York that choosing one will be a bit difficult. Getting married in the spring for the flowers in Central Park is an amazing idea. Also, if you are thinking about a white wedding here, then there are so many exotic locations that will make you go crazy.

![](/images/uploads/2022/10/h3.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**7. California:**

Not many people have heard about this beautiful destination. It is a destination with beautiful rocky coastlines. So, you can get married up and down the California Coast. San Diego is a popular destination due to the number of activities and laid-back surfer vibe in the amazing downtown area. Santa Barbara is a beautiful destination that is about 2 hours north of LA with many exotic resorts and an artsy feel. Also, San Francisco has rolling hills and an amazing city vibe. So, no matter where you get married in California, stunning views and happiness is assured.

**8. Vancouver:**

It is a very cool city. It has many beautiful rocky beaches that butt up to the lush forest with the city lights as the main backdrop. [Vancouver](https://traveltriangle.com/blog/wedding-venues-in-vancouver/#:~:text=With%20the%20waterfront%20views%2C%20mountain,is%20just%20seen%20in%20movies.) is a great mix of natural beauty and stunning views. Like Alaska, there are many amazing opportunities to take your guests on a whale-watching adventure.

![](/images/uploads/2022/10/h5-1024x683.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**9. Cruise:**

Cruising is one of the great ways to see a lot of areas at once. If you do a roundtrip to a US city, you will find the best wedding destinations to say your 'Yes I do!'. Also, there are many based cruises where you have an unforgettable wedding experience. You can do a California Coast cruise and cherish the best wedding experiences.

We hope, this blog will help you to select the best wedding destinations.
