Hi Candice!

Yes, I had a good January. And glad to know that your January was joyous too. Well, whenever I achieve something for which I feel delighted, but not even that much to throw a grand party, I make sure that go for a bike ride. Yeah, I love riding bikes.

So, I mostly go for rural areas because those places are more natural and there is less probability to meet people who know you. So, I would like to convey that do what makes you happy and excited, simultaneously keeping you [motivated](https://positivepsychology.com/benefits-motivation/#:~:text=Finding%20ways%20to%20increase%20motivation,develop%20talents%2C%20and%20boost%20engagement.). And try to celebrate the small wins in your life.

This hobby gives me peace and happiness and that is the only reason, I love doing this.
