Hi Kiara!

This is Stellar. And I have been continuously reading your blogs on relationships. But this time, I have a different question for you. I hope you help me with the best possible solutions for it.

So, Kiara, tell me, what are some good reasons parents should not take their [child's phones](https://www.mcafee.com/blogs/family-safety/my-child-wants-a-phone-should-i-say-yes/) as punishment?

Thank you!

Stellar

![](/images/uploads/2023/04/c1-1024x640.jpg)

Hi Stellar!

I can so relate to this question. Being a parent, I have so many reasons to give you. To list a few of them:

1. What if there is an emergency? School shootings are becoming common these days. My child's school was once a nice place, but after a few threats and real lockdowns, I was afraid to not take his phone away from him. Security-related issues, I hope you know that.

2. Do you want your kid to be social with friends, or lonely? Do not be a parent that says, "My kids do not need a friend they have me". You are old enough and understand much of the younger generation.

3. You can also use it to keep a check on your kids. Do not call them every hour when they are having fun with their friends.

I hope this answers your questions. Thank you for understanding.

Regards,

Kiara
