Hi Reena!

Babe, I am so happy that you are having a gala time in India. I am doing perfectly fine and enjoying this season to the fullest. Babe, haircare is one of the most important things to take care of during monsoon.

Frizz is the result of excessive humidity in the air. [Dry hair](https://www.webmd.com/beauty/dry-hair-causes) is another reason for the frizz. But adhering to some of the following simple tips will make you prevent dullness and fizziness.

So, choose a shampoo with a high concentration of glycerine which helps in fighting the frizz by penetrating the hair and hydrating your hair inside-out.

Glycerin also helps in absorbing the extra moisture in the air and it helps in forming a protective layer over the outside of the hair by trapping the moisture. So, ensure that you use a sulfate-free shampoo when you are washing your hair. Sulfate deprives the hair of natural oil and leaves them dry and frizzy.

On the other hand, sulfate-free shampoos maintain the natural oil levels of the hair. They allow them to have more moisture. You also need not shampoo your hair several times a week. You can skip [shampooing](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india/how-to-pick-the-right-shampoo/story-CQb9U5wZ3lvqVvGwBOt52M.html) your hair on alternate days and apply conditioner, instead. The conditioner contains some amount of surfactant that cleanses the hair and contains a small amount of oil that cleanses it away.

I hope this remedy will help you.

Thank you!
