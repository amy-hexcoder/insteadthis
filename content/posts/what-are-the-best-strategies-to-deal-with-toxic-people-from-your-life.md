Hi Nelson!

I am doing good Nelson. I hope you had an amazing weekend. What are your plans for Valentine's Day? Well, coming back to your question, here are some strategies that I do and they are very effective:

1. I try to ignore them as much as possible.

2. Converse with them only when it is necessary. I do not greet them.

3. When they talk to you, just answer in one word.

4. While communicating with them, do not look at them and make no eye contact. Instead, focus your attention somewhere else. This makes you look disinterested and they will understand that you do not like them. It will also irritate them and they will start distancing themselves.

These are from my personal experiences and are not at all expert opinions. I hope, you get this!
