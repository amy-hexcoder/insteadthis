Hi Kathrina!

I am doing perfectly fine babe. Well, I do not know about others, but for me and my fiancé, we are strong and happy at the moment. Yes, our future is still not set, but we want to make our relationship long-lasting and happy. Hence, we do the following things to keep our relationship fresh:

**1.) We surprise each other:**

We try new things together. We experiment with new recipes together and visit new places. These things make our bond stronger and make us happy too.

**2.) We never bottle things up:**

If something is bothering us, we make sure to [communicate](https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/health/healthyliving/relationships-and-communication). It is the only way to keep your relationship healthy. If you are being honest about what you feel; your relationship will be steady and happy.

**3.) We give each other space and freedom:**

We have our separate activities and shared activities. We allow each other to spend time on the things we enjoy separately. This makes us satisfied and fulfilled as individuals. Independence and space enable us to contribute to a happier relationship.

I hope this answer is helpful for you!

Thank you!

Kiara
