Hi Steward!

Thank you so much for reading my blogs. I am grateful that you do like them.

To answer you, yes absolutely!

What matters most in life is balance. A healthy balance between work and personal life. others who claim that a relationship is the most essential thing are incorrect, as are others who claim that only a career, job, or education are vital!

Both a career and a love relationship are important, and life would be incomplete without either. I know my response will elicit comments claiming that a relationship or [marriage](https://blog.devoteematch.com/2019/07/21/secret-of-happy-married-life/) isn't necessary for a good life... etc.., but the point is - it DOES make you happy! Even scientists have demonstrated that those who are pleased in their relationships are happier/satisfied/successful at work. It has a beneficial impact on your health, therefore it is something to think about.

If you call yourself successful and have failed as a boyfriend/husband, you are mistaken. You may be a good CEO, but when it comes to relationships, you set a poor example. Steve Jobs was an exceptional entrepreneur, an intelligent personality who built Apple, and an innovator who altered the world, but as a person, he couldn't accept his daughter Lisa and only acknowledged her when he was near death. If you listen to his talks, you will notice that he has highlighted the importance of balance in life.

Work to live, not live to work...

Thank you!

Kiara!
