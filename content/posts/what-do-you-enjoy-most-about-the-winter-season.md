I am Alisha, and I live in India. I have heard a lot about you and probably wanted to write you. I hope, you are doing great. So, winter is approaching here in India. I wanted to know what you enjoy the most about the winter season. Can you let me know about this?

Love,

Alisha!

Hi Alisha!

I am so glad that my readers are writing to me from India as well. I am doing well Alisha, and I hope, you are doing the same. Well, I love winters. The colder, the windier the better. I like spending winter in a warm or moderate place. And there are so many other reasons to love winters too.

1. I love wearing an oversized men's coat in winter.

2. I love the way the cold air bites our skin.

3. I love how the night air seems to sparkle around the people you love and trust as they laugh telling their favorite stories for the zillionth time.

4. But most importantly, I love the way people seem to glow as one year ends and another approach.

The [winter season](https://www.insider.com/why-winter-is-best-season-2017-12#:~:text=From%20skiing%20and%20snowboarding%20in,to%20do%20in%20the%20wintertime.) brings the hope and promise of a new year. People are happy as they take time off from work just to be with their friends and family. I love being around the bonfire and drinking hot chocolate and observing my people having great talks about their future.

To me, memories are important. So, I love winters and making new memories with my family and friends. I hope, you have a happy winter!

Bear hugs,

Petrica!
