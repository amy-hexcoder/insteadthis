Dear Shelly,

Thank you so much for writing back again. It makes me feel so special when you guys come to me and ask me for my suggestions or solutions. I get overwhelmed with your responses. Anyways, I am sure a majority of them do not know what exactly a six-second kiss is. So, let me start by asking you how long you share a kiss with your love? I know you have never timed it before. So, try setting your phone's stopwatch to six seconds. Does it feel longer than you thought?

Well, kissing is a good exercise in mindfulness. It stops the busyness in your brain and puts your focus on your soulmate at that moment. Also, lengthening kisses to six seconds is way too healthy for a relationship to sustain. Hence, a six-second kiss is the easiest thing. It's all about the connection which you feel with your partner. It brings you physically close to them. So, what does a six-second kiss does:

1.) It builds appreciation between you and your [partner](https://bestself.co/blogs/the-bestself-hub/10-ways-to-appreciate-your-partner).

2.) It boosts admiration.

3.) It adds to your emotional quotient.

4.) It helps in building a strong [connection](https://www.goodtherapy.org/blog/emotional-connection-what-couples-who-stay-together-do-every-day-0425185).

So, the more you practice an elongated kiss, the more you feel a connection with your partner. So, keep kissing! It will only lead to a healthy relationship.

Love,

Kiara
