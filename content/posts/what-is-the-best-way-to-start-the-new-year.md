Hi Genna!

Thank you so much, babe! Wishing you an exciting and thrilling new year. I initially start with the simple attitude that no matter what, I will prioritize myself over everything else.

[Prioritizing myself](https://www.tonyrobbins.com/mind-meaning/how-to-prioritize-yourself/) means I will sharpen my health, improve my creativity, and will develop my skills.

I will focus more on my consistent progress rather than taking a big leap.

I make a sheet and keep a track of all my activities. I also promise to be highly productive and stand a much better chance of nailing my goals.

This is my way of starting a new year on a fresh note. I hope, you have an amazing year filled with love, warmth, and affection!

Petrica!
