Hi Shelly!

Yes, I am doing great. Well, appearance nowadays is very essential. Wearing fresh clothes, maintaining a clean body, and [flossing teeth](https://www.healthline.com/health/benefits-of-flossing#:~:text=Flossing%20helps%20remove%20bacteria%2C%20plaque,at%20least%20twice%20a%20year.) is the need of the hour.

So, if you respect yourself enough to learn the best ways to groom and look presentable, then you have no other option but to maintain yourself.

Grooming also shows professionalism in the workplace and shows that you need to be taken seriously.

Looking neat and beautiful is always acceptable in any place or occasion, whereas a shabby appearance is not welcomed in most places.

Not grooming yourself can cause a lot of health problems and skin damage too. So, it is always essential to take self-care and there is nothing wrong with pampering and grooming yourself.
