Hi Fresca!

I appreciate your warm sentiments very much. Well, I would say that move slowly. Go extremely slowly.

You thought they were attractive. He or she is also drawn to you, so you decide to start dating. However, after a few days, weeks, or months, you find the [relationship](https://headspace.org.au/explore-topics/for-young-people/why-relationships-are-important/) is not working, so you decide to end it. The time following a breakup is all about cursing love and posting depressing quotations as a status pursuing one another. It's just wasting time.

So go slowly. Attend dates. We frequently cross paths. Learn about one another. I'm not referring to going to the movies or having dinner dates. Visit locations where you two can converse. Talk only. It doesn't need to be elaborate. Sometimes all you need is two cups of coffee and a couch. Discuss each other with each other. Examine your feelings for the other person. Is it genuine love or merely a fleeting fad?

Enjoy the trip. There is no question that the final destination will be gorgeous. But first, savor the journey.

Thank you!

Kiara
