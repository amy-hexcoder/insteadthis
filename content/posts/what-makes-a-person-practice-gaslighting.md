Hi Sheen!

That's a perfectly fine babe! You know, when people talk to me about relationships, I tend to think about it through so many aspects. Well, when I look at my situation with my ex, my ex would come home. He would always fight. This started with little things like taking out the trash, finding a reason to get angry, and exploding on me. So, the real reason people gaslight is because if you are fighting with your spouse, it gives you either a reason to go out, or be online possibly talking to another man/woman. This is because they get the space they want to do so, or it is because they are being chased by you, and rejecting you it makes them feel wanted and in a way have the power to control. So, all you can do is secretly move out of a relationship that makes you feel depressed and let down. I hope you got the answer.
