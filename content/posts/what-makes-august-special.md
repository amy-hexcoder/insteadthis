Welcome, August!

So, it's 1st August today and before summer comes to an end let me squeeze you in for some more fun activities and celebrations. From National Root beer float day to National Relaxation Day, there are many reasons to celebrate August.

![](/images/uploads/2022/08/Aug1-1024x1024.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**August 1: National Girlfriends Day**

Some women get utterly lost without their girlfriends. So, today is the day to tell your girlfriends how much they mean to you.

**August 2: National Ice Cream Sandwich Day**

On this day, make your ice cream even better. Smash it between two soft cookies, and get indulged in this amazing cool treat.

**August 3: National Watermelon Day**

Watermelon is everyone's favorite. So, in honor of Watermelon Day, grab a bite of this tempting pink fruit. And look for some recipes which you can use to make your summers refreshing.

**August 4: National Chocolate Chip Cookie Day**

Chewy, crispy, or straight from the oven, this chocolate chip cookie will make your day. So, pour yourself a cold glass of milk and enjoy.

**August 5: Work Like a Dog Day**

Ok. On this day, say thank you to your co-workers for being reliable, consistent, and hardworking. And try offering some affection too.

**August 6: National Root Beer Float Day**

So, take a trip back to your childhood days, and sip on a beer topped with vanilla ice cream.

**August 7: Friendship's Day**

So, cherish this day by meeting your friends and making your day worth memorable. Say thank you to them because they have been your great support.

![](/images/uploads/2022/08/August-2-1024x569.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**August 8: International Cat Day**

If you are a cat owner, the joy of cat ownership already makes your day. Give your kitty a little attention today. Spoil her with a new toy or some tasty treats.

**August 9: [National Rice Pudding Day](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=unR-MpB1l2k&ab_channel=FoodWishes)**

Rice pudding is rice mixed with milk or water and flavored with cinnamon, raisins, and sugar.

**August 10: National S’mores Day**

This is perfectly delicious in its original form with milk, chocolate, graham crackers, and marshmallows.

**August 11: Raksha Bandhan**

We know that many people are looking forward to this Day. So, celebrate the bond of love between brothers and sisters by doing something special on this day.

![](/images/uploads/2022/08/August3.webp)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**August 12: Baseball Fans Day**

If you like baseball, then celebrate this day by visiting your baseball park. If not then, you can enjoy hot dogs, popcorn, and ice-cold beer while you watch a game on TV.

**August 13: National Filet Mignon Day**

Have you tried filet mignon before? Well, if not then treat yourself to this delicious cuisine on this day.

**August 14: National Creamsicle Day**

Dairy and citrus are not a common pair. But when it comes to creamsicles, it works. So, cool yourself off with one for National Creamsicle Day.

**August 15: Independence Day (India)**

If you are someone who loves watching the Indian Parade on television then you are a die-hard Indian. Indians celebrate this day by hoisting the 'Tiranga'.

![](/images/uploads/2022/08/August4-1024x640.jpg)
***Credits@webneel.com***

**August 16: National Bratwurst Day**

When we talk about Bratwurst, they are German sausages made of pork, veal, or beef. Try cooking a classic dish from this.

**August 17:National Black Cat Appreciation Day**

Usually, black cats are considered inauspicious. But it is good when they have a day dedicated to their appreciation.

**August 18: [Janmasthami](https://www.bbc.co.uk/teach/janmashtami-why-do-some-hindus-go-without-sleep-for-2-days/zmmwgwx#:~:text=The%20Krishna%20Janmashtami%20festival%20marks,bond%20between%20brothers%20and%20sisters.)**

Indians celebrate this day to mark the birth of Lord Krishna. They visit temples, offer prayers, and make desserts for Lord Krishna.

![](/images/uploads/2022/08/August7-1-1024x576.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**August 19: National Potato Day**

If you are a Potato lover like me, then enjoy your favorite spud preparations for National Potato Day.

**August 20: National Bacon Lover’s Day**

If you are a Bacon lover, then celebrate this day by making some special Bacon recipes.

**August 21: National Spumoni Day**

Spumoni is a traditional dessert having gelato, whipped cream, nuts, and candied fruits. Order one Spumoni and cherish this day.

**August 22: National Bao Day**

Bao is a met or veggie-filled steamed momo. You can find them at your local Chinese restaurant.

**August 23: National Sponge Cake Day**

If you have never tried your hand son making sponge cake, then today is the day to change that. It is an easy recipe that you can jazz up with icing, fruits, and more.

**August 24: National Waffle Day**

Waffles are versatile. So, there is no better time than to try some inventive waffle concoctions than today.

**August 25:National Whiskey Sour Day**

Celebrate the royalty of this day by sipping on a whiskey sour. Try making a classic cocktail containing fresh lemon, simple syrup, bourbon, and yet more.

**August 26:National Dog Day**

Ok. So, if you are someone who loves your four-legged friend, then celebrate this day by giving your day a special treat. They deserve it!

![](/images/uploads/2022/08/August6.webp)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**August 27: National Banana Lovers Day**

It is a  day for all health enthusiasts. Enjoy a banana recipe to celebrate this amazing day.

**August 28: National Bow Tie Day**

This day is a perfect excuse to rock a fun accessory and show off your unique style.

**August 29: Lemon Juice Day**

It adds brightness to any dish. So, on this day find a new way to use lemon juice in your recipe.

**August 30: National Toasted Marshmallow Day**

If you want to indulge in toasted marshmallows on National S’mores Day, then here is your chance to catch up.

**August 31: [Ganesh Chaturthi](https://blog.thomascook.in/when-wisdom-was-born-ganesh-chaturthi/)**

People who love bringing Lord Ganesha to their homes, eagerly wait for this day. It is a day to celebrate Lord Ganesha's arrival at our homes.

***Credits@GoogleImages***

So, we hope you have a super happy August. Even we are looking forward to bringing some special blogs in this month.
