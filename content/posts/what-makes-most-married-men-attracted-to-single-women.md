## QUESTION

Hi Kiara,

Among the married men I know, there is one who leans towards me while I am equally attracted to him. Out of curiosity, I was wondering, what makes most married men so attracted to women who aren't married?

Love,

Nancy!

## ANSWER

Hi Nancy,

A married man may be attracted to a single woman for a variety of reasons. Attractions can be complex and multifaceted, depending on psychological and societal factors. It is impossible to find a one-size-fits-all answer to why single women are alluring, but there are a few common reasons.

![](https://liveboldandbloom.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Add_a_little_bit_of_body_text_10-1-768x538.png)

**1. Intrigue and Excitement:** Married men may be attracted to single women because they're exciting and mysterious. New relationships and adventures can spark feelings of attraction and desire.

**2. Reinforcement of Masculinity:** Some married men may perceive their masculinity as waning due to their marriage. By [rekindling their attraction](/stories/how-to-find-love-through-spiritual-methods) and validation of their masculinity, single women can boost their self-esteem.

**3. Renewed Sense of Youthful Energy:** Single women can remind married men of their youthful energy and vitality. Engaging in activities or experiencing things that they missed while married can create a sense of rejuvenation and attraction for them.

**4. Fantasy and Escape:** Some married men fantasize about having an affair with a single woman, as a means of escaping their daily routine and marital problems.

**5. Challenging Stereotypes:** Single women may attract married men because their expectations and stereotypes are challenged. Secret affairs and illicit romance can make them feel rebellious and empowered.

Though these reasons might make [married men](https://www.google.com/url?sa=i&url=https%3A%2F%2Fliveboldandbloom.com%2F11%2Frelationships%2Fmarried-men-attracted&psig=AOvVaw2NSMmJuvcNVuYEeAz7T2h9&ust=1700737159542000&source=images&cd=vfe&opi=89978449&ved=0CBQQjhxqFwoTCIjH5OW514IDFQAAAAAdAAAAABAX) want to be with single women, it's important to realize that their actions can have a serious impact. If you have an affair, you can end up with broken marriages, emotional pain, and heartbreak for everyone involved. Open and honest communication within a marriage helps fix problems.

Love,

Kiara!
