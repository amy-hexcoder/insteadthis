Imagine you are at home, scrolling through Instagram posts, especially on the weekend. I am sure you will find many delicious pictures of people enjoying their Sunday [brunch](https://metro.co.uk/2015/04/04/11-reasons-we-love-brunch-5128502/).
The following weekend you are out to catch up with your friends or taking your family out to a cozy restaurant, and the aroma of a mouth-watering buffet lingers around you. Does it not sound like a perfect weekend to you?

A playful blend of two words, breakfast and lunch, Brunch is undoubtedly the most-loved meal of today’s food culture. Though at times, it might sound like a new concept to the dining world, it has been there for decades now. The idea of Brunch came back in the 1890s when Guy Beringer talked about late Sunday breakfast in his article titled *Brunch: A Plea*. However, with changing times, brunch scenes have changed from hash browns to hashtags. But the only constant thing about such scenes is enjoying the meal together.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/09/pexels-cottonbro-4877863-1-683x1024.jpg)
  *Credits@Pexels*

- **What is the tradition behind Brunch scenes?**

Many believe that Brunch originated in European households as a traditional Sunday family meal. People gathered with families and enjoyed a wholesome, comforting meal in each other's company. Eventually, this tradition spread across the world, including the United States. Initially, it became a part of clubs, hotels, and households. But with time, many restaurants started offering fantastic spreads in the late morning hours on weekends.

In the world that we live in, people are all the more connected through social media. But somewhere, it lacks the deep social bonding among people. People need more and more face-to-face conversations to connect. So, meeting your friends or spending time with your family over a meal is the best way to reconnect. Brunch, at such times, is more than just a meal! It is a way of creating a sense of belongingness among people.

- **What are some of the exciting brunch ideas?**

Brunch is not only about steamy-hot waffles or sumptuous eggs and toast! There is a truck-load variety to choose your [brunch meal](https://magicpin.in/blog/10-minute-brunch-recipes/). It includes regular meals served with champagne or cocktails. People across the world enjoy different meals.

**A perfect English brunch**

It would include eggs (fried, scrambled, or poached), baked beans, sausages, bacon, and a thick slice of toast (layered with a generous amount of butter). Baked potatoes with a heavy topping of cheese or tuna is another go-to English meal. It would also include some classic desserts like some freshly baked [muffins](/stories/five-classic-desserts-from-across-the-world) with butter and marmalade.

**Brunch in the U.S.**

In the United States, the influence of its diverse culture is visible in a brunch menu. It features the absolute favorite, classic whole wheat pancakes topped with whipped cream and berries. You have the option of trying unique dishes at an American restaurant like some perfectly cooked eggs Benedict or Burgers with peanut-chipotle barbecue sauce. You could also go experimental and try out the giant biscuit burger. It is a biscuit sandwich topped with eggs, lots of cheese, and ham.

**What makes Sunday brunch an obsession?**

- **Recharges you for the week ahead**

After the burn-out of the entire week, a Sunday brunch is an ideal getaway. A well-planned Sunday can save you a lot of time for yourself while still giving you that desired [escape](https://www.usatoday.com/story/college/2014/08/13/oh-we-brunch-whats-with-this-brunch-obsession-anyway/37395067/)! Get enough sleep, do all the household chores, and head out to meet your friends. It saves your time and money. Doing this will sort out the day with the only wholesome and formal meal of the day. The relaxing ambiance, good music, and good food will set the right mood for your coming week.

- **It is all about GOOD Food**

Almost everyone, including the pickiest eaters, will have a variety of delicious food on the table. Most restaurants bring out their special menu for brunch. These places run out of seats on a weekend brunch. They spread out a wholesome buffet, including omelets, fresh fruits, pancakes, waffles, and the list goes on and on. So, no more confusion about what to eat on the weekend. Head out with friends or family and grab your plate from your favorite restaurant.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/09/pexels-cottonbro-4877842-1-683x1024.jpg)

- **It is the easiest way to get along with friends.**

You have a long overdue meetup with friends, but the tight schedule throughout the week hardly allows you to catch up with your friends. Planning a weekend brunch is a perfect idea in such cases. You get along with your friends, share the happenings of the week, and that sums up an ideal weekend. You could add more activities to this plan, such as a movie after brunch or a short walk down the streets. Consider including this plan in your lifestyle, and you will see the changes in your relationships for sure.

- **Have a brunch party at home**

Yes! Brunch is a perfect way to have your friends over in your house. You could set up an outdoor brunch by the [poolside](https://www.dellaresorts.com/blog/enjoy-lavish-sunday-brunch-pool/ "https://www.dellaresorts.com/blog/enjoy-lavish-sunday-brunch-pool/") or in your backyard. You could spread out a buffet from your kitchen. It creates a chilled-out vibe and saves you from carrying the dishes in and out of the kitchen. Then you can go ahead planning the menu for the day. What's better than a comforting house party with your favorites?
