QUESTION

Hi Kiara,

When a relationship doesn't feel right, is it okay to end it? The way my life is right now, everything is going wrong for me and I am not sure what I should do. Please help.

Thanks,

Catherine!

ANSWER

Hi Catherine,

Definitely, it's okay to end a relationship if it doesn't feel right. [Relationships](/stories/what-does-a-successful-relationship-look-like) don't always work out, but your happiness and well-being are important. Here's why it's okay:

**Your Happiness Matters:** It's important to be in a relationship that makes you happy, fulfilled and loved. An unhappy or stressed state is a sign that something isn't right.

**Trust Your Gut:** Don't ignore that nagging feeling if it's been bothering you for some time. Most of the time, your instincts are right.

**Not All Relationships Are Meant to Last:** In some cases, a relationship that was great once may no longer be a good fit in the future. It's okay for relationships to run their course as people change and circumstances change.

![](https://imgix.bustle.com/rehost/2016/9/13/389f0b77-0dec-4f5d-b490-1e1a576d1062.jpg?w=1200&h=630&fit=crop&crop=faces&fm=jpg)

**Communication Doesn't Always Fix Everything:** In relationships, communication is crucial, but sometimes things may not improve despite having talked it out. It's okay to walk away if it's not working.

**Your Well-Being:** Ending a relationship that feels wrong can have a positive impact on your mental and emotional well-being.

**Life Is Too Short:** It's not worth it to stay in a relationship that feels like a constant struggle. Ending it can open up new opportunities for better and more compatible relationships in the future.

There's no doubt that [ending a relationship](https://www.google.com/url?sa=i&url=https%3A%2F%2Ftinybuddha.com%2Fblog%2F3-ways-know-relationship-isnt-right%2F&psig=AOvVaw0mDfmV87aM2xLU5K45VJxv&ust=1698761144014000&source=images&cd=vfe&opi=89978449&ved=0CBQQjhxqFwoTCJij6cb4nYIDFQAAAAAdAAAAABAD) is never easy, and it's okay to seek support from friends, family, or a therapist. It's an important decision that should be made with care, but don't be afraid to prioritize your happiness before anything else.

Love,

Kiara!
