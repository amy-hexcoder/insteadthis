Holidays herald a joyous time of year filled with [festivities](/stories/december-festive-activities-holiday-glee-in-full-swing), along with the stress of having to choose the right outfits for each occasion. Often, we overdress for events, layering sweaters, skirts, and scarves all at once. We got caught up in the question, "How fancy do I need to get?", "Should I go modern or vintage?", "Are boots better than pumps?".
I know it's tricky to pinpoint which variables to look at…but fear not; I'm here to make dressing up less stressful. Fortunately, it's pretty simple. I'll show you how.

## 1. Glitzy Christmas

[Christmas](/stories/solo-christmas-celebrations-how-to-have-a-magical-time), the holiday we all love. You'll be surprised at the sheer number of choices you have for a Christmas dinner, as it's the perfect time to enhance color and beauty. The best option would be to wear something that can be worn indoors or outdoors, with a bit of elegance.
Nothing makes you feel better than dressing up for a [Christmas event](/stories/netflixs-christmas-shows-and-movies-to-cheer-you-up). The weather may be cold, but Christmas [parties](/stories/ultimate-spotify-playlist-christmas-songs-you-cant-miss) are often indoor affairs, which means there will likely be a fireplace. Women should dress in something stylish and patterned, such as matching plaid sets with heels and accessories that reflect the decor. Accessorize with gold jewelry or a fashionable hat. Coordinated sets are in fashion at Christmastime. Thus, when it comes to men, they could wear something similar or even identical, with a touch of masculinity. If you are looking for a traditional pattern for your outfit, a red plaid might be the best choice. As with women, men can also wear fur coats and parkas. You can find plenty of warm clothing for sale during the holiday season.

## 2. Cheerful New Year

All over the world, people wait with great anticipation for the New Year. People often celebrate this holiday with fireworks, parades, and reflections on the past year, which varies by culture. New Year's celebrations can be fun since there are so many things to do. People enjoy going to parties and celebrating the countdown to the New Year because it marks the end of the previous year. The fireworks display occurs at a family or club gathering.
It is the time when you can show off your [flashiest](https://www.seventeen.com/fashion/trends/g34932759/fashion-trends-2021/) fashion side. Everywhere you go, you can expect to find a lot of activity, lots of people, and a lot of noise. An asymmetrical sequin dress paired with a tiny bag and high heels will never go out of style. [Men](/stories/ditch-the-razor-and-grin-the-november) shouldn't be afraid to be edgy. It is the perfect time to wear a sheer shirt and leather pants with boots. Let your outfit take you through the night as you let loose.

## 3. Delightful Easter

Easter is a Christian holiday celebrating Jesus Christ's resurrection. Egg hunts and dinners often highlight the holiday. Because the weather is so pleasant for Easter, the options for outfits are countless. If you wear something that reflects Easter's fun and tranquility, it's sure to draw compliments.
The earth is at its most beautiful when the skies are clear while the sun is shining. So is your outfit. For women, it is [best to wear](https://www.outfittrends.com/outfits-to-wear-this-easter/) a sundress, slippers, a big hat, and sunglasses in the sun to prevent their makeup from melting. Shirts with a button-down can be worn with shorts and regular sneakers by men. It's okay to wear casual clothing to Easter events since they are primarily about kids' egg hunts.

## 4. Blessed Thanksgiving

Thanksgiving began as a time to be thankful for the blessings of the harvest. Thanksgiving is now more of a time to express gratitude to our friends and family than it was a few decades ago. It can be tricky to dress up for a Thanksgiving dinner depending on your religion. During this holiday, all family members usually wear similar clothing, such as an outfit similar to a nun's.
You should choose something casual to wear to a Thanksgiving event because you will be moving around a lot and conversing with others. Therefore, you should wear something that allows you to sit relaxed since you will probably spend much of the meal sitting down.
During the holidays, women should wear something fall-like. The perfect outfit for women would be skinny jeans with long brown boots, a matching long sleeve shirt, and a stylish hat to complete the look with some extra accessories. For men, loose or fitted clothing like jeans, flannel shirts, boots, or loafers would be [suitable](/stories/dear-men-skincare-doesnt-make-you-more-feminine). It shouldn't be anything casual that you wear sloppily to the mall, but rather something people will notice.
