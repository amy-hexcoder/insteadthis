"Fast fashion is not free. Someone somewhere is paying" - Lucy Siegle.

The process of switching from Fast to Slow fashion brings a sense of recognition that there are people behind the clothes we choose to wear! And the first step you can take towards having a more conscious style is by asking WHY because buying anything.

Living a conscious lifestyle requires you to surround yourself with inspiration. And after knowing what is Fast Fashion and what you could do to not support it (check out my previous blog), you might have decided to take the road of conscious styling! So where to find such inspiration to keep yourself on track while still having the best of everything? The best option here could be to read blogs of such people who have the same thoughts on fashion as you. And trust me, there are many! Following such blogs will help you get a hold of some good sustainable living tips, ethical styling tips, sustainable fashion brands, and a lot more!

So, sit back and relax while you read through this blog and discover some good sustainable fashion blogs and influencers who are sure to leave you feeling inspired to start your journey!

## EcoCult:

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/222_1f142452-757a-4554-a17a-815cd5184a19_1024x1024-1.jpg)
  *Credits@GoogleImage*

So, here we have a blog that transformed from a woman's passion project into an international information hub! Alden Wicker started EcoCult way back in 2013 because she realized what fashion does to the planet and its people. Among other things about fashion, she felt there were no reliable, honest, and science-based views about how one could reduce their wardrobe's impact on the planet. And that is where EcoCult comes in - it is a thoughtful, science-based take on the international fashion industry. They believe in discovering the truth in the fast fashion industry and sharing it with the readers to help them become better consumers!

## Eco Warrior Princess:

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/What-Do-You-Think-of-Our-New-Eco-Warrior-Princess-Vlog-Series-1-1024x576.png)
  *Credits@GoogleImage*

One of the big sustainable fashion blogs of 2020 was Eco-Warriors by Jennifer Nini. This farm owner and environmentalist started the blog way back in 2010 with a vision to redefine the meaning of living of sustainable lifestyle. Moreover, Eco Warrior Princess is like an eco-friendly media brand, where they keep launching exciting webinars and conferences! Do check out their articles covering Fashion, Wellness, Beauty, and more.

## Sustainably Chic:

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/Sustainably_chic_1200x630.png)
  *Credits@GoogleImage*

Here is another one-stop destination for all those who love fashion and at the same time understand that it needs to exist responsibly. Sustainably Chic allows fashion to exist responsibly so we (the consumers) can enjoy the art and love behind everything we wear! Natalie, a resident of Florida, started this blog in the summer of 2014, where she shares her thoughts about sustainable fashion. Here, she promotes some good brands from across the world, doing a great deal for sustainable fashion. However, she has gradually expanded the space by bringing voices of serval other contributors to share their experiences and add more to the whole. In fact, you could too join their space by writing to them. If you are interested, head to their website and check for the details.

## Aditi Mayer @aditimayer:

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/Aditi-Mayer-768x1024.jpg)
  *Credits@GoogleImage*

Instagram is another way to look for inspiration from some of the best ethical fashion influencers who beautifully curate their pages to inspire people to live a conscious life.

One such influencer is this Los Angeles-based girl, Aditi Mayer. The purpose behind her efforts is to create looks that bring together style and sustainable fashion. Her process of creating such amazing content includes visual storytelling that combines nature with her amazing pictures!

## Sustainable Jungle:

- ![](/images/uploads/2022/01/download-1024x545.jpg)
  *Credits@GoogleImage*

At Sustainable Jungle, the creators believe in bringing a positive change by encouraging people to find value in nature, to live more sustainably. As people, we hold the power to bring the changes we want. And therefore, the creators have decided to come up with the best of organizations, brands, and change-makers and share them with the world to inspire them to live an eco-friendly lifestyle. They cover anything and everything about zero waste - from zero waste mascara to sunscreens, they come up with the best recommendations. Please check out their website, covering all things sustainable, from travel and lifestyle to beauty and fashion!

So, with these blogs and influencers, you know where to look for inspiration, and I'm sure you will never run out of inspiration again. I hope these blogs fulfill the purpose of inspiring you to follow minimal and sustainable fashion from here on. Also, please let me know in the comments below which ones are your favorites and why?
