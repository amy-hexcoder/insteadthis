When you feel confident and comfortable enough to take a stand for yourself and put your ideas and words forward, you excel in impregnable ways.

To feel comfortable in your outfit is as important as being presentable. Because if you dress up every day for your office just for being office-ready, you’ll get bored of your work as well as of yourself. It is essential to keep your work interesting to maintain the efficiency of your work.

**Get the office ready with a twist.**

It is easy to be well dressed in a fascinating way. You have to find the correct attire and the perfect pair of work heels. If you wish to make a fashion statement at your workplace, here are a few tips for you.

Your work footwear needs to be comfortable and suitable for work wear, especially when you decide to wear heels to your workplace. If you wish to get over your basic formal outfits and wish for a change, then be the change, pair the most [comfortable](https://www.oprahdaily.com/style/g26240376/comfortable-heels-for-work/?slide=10 "https://www.oprahdaily.com/style/g26240376/comfortable-heels-for-work/?slide=10") work [heels](/stories/high-heels-obsession-and-how-to-find-your-perfect-pair "/stories/high-heels-obsession-and-how-to-find-your-perfect-pair") with stylish outfits.

**Most suitable Heels for Work and [Stylish](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/uk/fashion/what-to-wear/a14587839/how-to-be-more-stylish/ "https://www.harpersbazaar.com/uk/fashion/what-to-wear/a14587839/how-to-be-more-stylish/") work outfits**

- **Wedge with Pencil Skirt:**

Wedges provide height and also the support to distribute the weight of your body, which makes them comforting. As well as wedges look stylish enough to be paired with any outfit, but they go well with a formal pencil skirt. A plain pencil skirt with a pastel-colored shirt or aesthetic print pencil skirt with a solid-colored shirt can steal the thunder with class and comfort.

![](/images/uploads/2021/09/heels1-2-1024x681.jpg)

**Credits@Pexels**

- Platform Heels with Statement Pants, Tank, and Blazer:

Platform heels can be so handy they make walking in heels easier with a sassy touch. Pairing them with statement pants as office wear comes with risks, as carrying statement pants is not easy, especially with a tank top. You can prefer to choose less complicated prints to make it look easy going. Pairing the entire outfit with a blazer can be handy and stylish.

- **Cone Heels and Jumpsuit:**

Cone heels are the classics. They can go with literally anything and everything, but pairing them with a jumpsuit will be a style statement in itself. Don’t try to keep it simple: pair floral print cone heels with soothing off-white baggy or oversized jumpsuit and [oxidized](https://jewellerista.com/oxidised-jewellery/ "https://jewellerista.com/oxidised-jewellery/") jewelry.

![](/images/uploads/2021/09/heels6-1024x621.jpg)

- **Ankle Bootie and Trench Coat:**

If you wish for a badass bossy look, go with ankle [booties](/stories/getting-winter-ready-with-your-stylish-boots "/stories/getting-winter-ready-with-your-stylish-boots") and a trench coat. Use dark vintage colors and whites to make your outfit look more exclusive. Pair them with a tote or a hobo [bag](https://www.trunkclub.com/womens-style/types-of-purses "https://www.trunkclub.com/womens-style/types-of-purses") and hoop earrings.

- **Block Heels and Midi Dress:**

Midi dresses can work on different occasions in different ways but, pairing block heels with a midi dress as work wear can be a task. Have you ever given a thought to wearing a bright color to your work? Try pairing a bright geo print midi dress and moody block heels with a basket bag. You’ll be stunned by the final look.
