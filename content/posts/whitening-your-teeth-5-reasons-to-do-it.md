Teeth color is one of the most [common problems](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/ss/slideshow-tooth-problems) with smiles. Sadly, many things can stain or discolor our pearly whites, leaving them looking stained and unattractive. Fortunately, a solution exists. A cosmetic dentistry treatment such as professional teeth whitening provides people with a painless, affordable, and highly effective way to brighten their smiles in no time.

The following are five good reasons why you should get your teeth whitened ASAP:

## Removes Dental Stains

Tooth whitening is the most effective method of [removing teeth stains](https://www.colgate.com/en-in/oral-health/teeth-whitening/teeth-stain-removal-types). Stains on teeth often result from eating and drinking certain foods and beverages, including smoking. Eventually, you will see spots on your teeth from dietary deposits.
Even after brushing and flossing daily or using mouth rinses, these stains are difficult to remove. Dental whitening can quickly eliminate these stains. And these stains will never haunt you for the rest of your life.

- ![](/images/uploads/2021/09/teeth-whitening-1024x642.jpg)
  *Credits@Google*

## Painless Procedure

Dental whitening does not cause any [pain](https://www.westenddental.com/blog/reducing-teeth-sensitivity-after-whitening/). During this procedure, your teeth are scrubbed thoroughly before the application process. After that, the teeth are soaked in a whitening agent for a specific amount of time. Once the treatment is over, your mouth is sanitized and, you are good to go.

## Affordable

The most affordable and cheapest cosmetic dental treatment is tooth whitening. Its [affordability](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/beauty/health/g32176566/best-teeth-whitening-kits/) is one of the main reasons for its popularity. Depending on the type and extent of the treatment, the cost of professional teeth whitening may vary.

## Quick and Easy Process

The [treatment](https://hancockvillagedental.com/tooth-whitening-how-long-until-i-see-results/) typically lasts 30-60 minutes. With in-office whitening, the whole process gets completed in one visit. You can start enjoying your white smile right away. Treatment does not interfere with your busy schedule. Alternately, some dentists offer at-home teeth whitening products.

## Maintain Good Oral Hygiene

The consensus is that whitening your teeth will lead to [better oral hygiene](https://www.dentalhealth.org/blog/facts-tips-tooth-whitening) if you invest in it. By taking proper care of your teeth, you are more likely to maintain a beautiful smile.
The more time spent brushing and flossing your teeth, the better your oral health will be. Remember to continue to get routine dental examinations after the whitening treatment.

# Teeth whitening's life-changing effect:

## Reduce Aging Signs

Your teeth will look dull and unhealthy as you consume coffee, tea, red wine, and other beverages over time.
However, you can get rid of the yellow tint by whitening your teeth. The whiter and brighter your smile is, the younger you will look and perhaps even more attractive.

> What is the best way to preserve tooth enamel?
> The stigma of stained teeth tends to make people brush harder. There is a misconception that rubbing hard enough will remove the stain. In contrast, brushing too hard can result in gum recession, enamel erosion, and tooth decay. It is best to be gentle while brushing and [maintain](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/features/tooth-enamel-damage) the enamel.
