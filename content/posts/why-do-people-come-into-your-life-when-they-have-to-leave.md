It's been a long haven't written you. The last few days have been heartbreaking for me. I had someone in my life who meant the world to me.  He showed me an altogether different world. But one day, he decided to leave and choose someone else over me. I was shattered, heartbroken, and aloof. I had so many negative thoughts in mind. But still, I decided to come out of that particular situation and move on. But Kiara, why do people come into your life when they have to leave one day?  Is it too much to ask for?

Regards,

Shelly!

Hi Shelly,

I hope you gather the strength and courage to deal with all this. Shelly, I can understand your situation because somewhere down the line, even I was going through the same. There was a time in my life when I had asked the same question God n number of times. It was when I lost my first [love](/stories/how-does-an-emotionally-damaged-man-show-his-love-for-a-woman) because of our compatibility issues. I had never planned to fall in love with him, but it just happened naturally. I was shy but somehow, I gelled with him instantly maybe because he was just like me. He became my best friend, my mentor, and my guide. But after a year, we parted ways. I cried asking the question but received no answer. After this incident, I fell in love with another man who is now my husband. When I fell for my husband, he never let me break up though he knew I was not sure. It was 5 years ago, and now we have a family. I never knew that my initial heartbreak would lead me to meet my soulmate.

So, whoever is wondering this question, just have a little patience and faith because everything will fall into place. God always has better plans for us than we think. So, Shelly, just stay strong and be kind. I wish you find the love of your life way too soon.

Hugs,

Kiara
