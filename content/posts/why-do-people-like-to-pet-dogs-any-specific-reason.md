Hi Liana!

I am doing fantastic. Yes, the birthday party was indeed good. We ate, laughed, and cherished our good times. And coming back to your question, I do not know about others, but for me, my pet calms me. It makes me feel happier and good in general. I pet my dog every day, and he loves it. And when I see someone walking their pet, I almost always ask if I can pet their [dog](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K1DsMwjVXN4&ab_channel=GingerCat). The dog also gets excited to see me, and I love how excited they get. You can feel love. And honestly, I still prefer hugging a dog to a human.

I hope you got my emotions.
