Hi Joseph!

Even I love eating ice cream. Ice cream releases happy chemicals. They are called [dopamines](https://psychcentral.com/blog/dopamine-fasting-probably-doesnt-work-try-this-instead). And eating ice cream is one of the ways to deal with stress and depression. In fact, it is an easy way of reducing the pain that we experience.

I used to do that when I was 18 years old, and when I would have a tough day or be unhappy with my life. So, it is just like eating your feelings. In simple words, it is a maladaptive coping mechanism because, after having it, you feel better.

I read about it in an online journal today, hence sharing this with you.
