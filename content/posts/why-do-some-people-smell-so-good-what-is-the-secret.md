Hi Lilly!

Oooo I am in love with this question. I am a mentor so, I need to smell good. Also, I work closely with people daily. I love walking past people knowing that I smell good. It's very much a confidence booster for me. From my personal experience, a lot of people who have some to hug me have always said that I [smell good](https://www.degruyter.com/document/doi/10.1515/sem-2015-0093/html).

I guess this is something I put the effort into because my mum always said that, ***"A man will always remember how you smelled on every date"...***

**3.) To avoid bad breath, I brush my teeth twice a day and after a meal. Also, I always chew a piece of gum or a piece of mint.**

**4.) Deodorant is love! (Every morning after a shower and after you sweat, this acts as your savior)**

This is a must! Also, remember to stay healthy as your body's natural chemistry produces smells that make you smell nice. Remember, to not overdo it with the perfume as sometimes it can overwhelm the person you are with! So, here were my tips, hope this helps you too!
