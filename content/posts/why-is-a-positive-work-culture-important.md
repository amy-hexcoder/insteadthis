Hi Rochel!

Yes, I am doing fabulous. Well, thank you so much for extracting this topic. Well, there are many reasons why a positive [corporate culture](https://www.alfraconsulting.eu/benefits-of-organizational-culture/#:~:text=A%20strong%20and%20healthy%20organizational%20culture%20can%20provide%20many%20benefits,employee%20engagement%20and%20retention%20rates%2C) plays an important role. It is critical today because it can help to attract the right employees in an organization.

In a competitive job market, employees have a lot of choices and they tend to be selective about where they would like to work. An organization with a negative or toxic culture can quickly lose top talent.  Also, a positive work culture helps in the creation of a more positive work environment. Employees who are always appreciated and valued are more likely to be productive as well as happy. It leads to improve employee morale and can yield better outcomes. I hope, you got your answer.
