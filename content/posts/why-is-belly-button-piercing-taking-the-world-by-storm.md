**BELLY PIERCING: A bold fashion statement!**

It is a piece of jewelry, typically inserted- through the skin, just directly above the belly button. This piercing procedure is done with the help of tools. In this process, the belly area is cleaned first. If your belly area has got some hair, the piercer shaves it off with the help of a razor.

He then makes use of his piercing tools and pierces on the correct spot. A hollow [needle](https://www.almostfamouspiercing.com/blog/piercing-guns-vs-needles-better/) is pushed through the hole, followed by jewelry. It is very normal if you bleed or get some redness immediately after the belly piercing. It will subside quickly.

**PAIN AND HEALING TIME REQUIRED:**

When it comes to belly piercing, they pinch a little but don't hurt too much. Because the skin on your stomach is fleshy, you can probably expect it to be less painful than cartilage piercing, which is fine! A naval piercing takes around six months to a year to fully heal depending, on how you care for it and your day-to-day lifestyle. Regardless of this, it is always important to check with your piercer when you believe it's healed.

**AFTERCARE:**

Following the correct aftercare procedure during the healing process is extremely important. The first thing to take care of is washing your hands before touching your piercing to ensure that you avoid passing any [bacteria](https://blogs.scientificamerican.com/lab-rat/how-bacteria-sneak-into-your-blood-through-your-mouth/). Then clean your piercing twice a day, either with a sterile saline solution or gentle soap and water. Regardless of what you use, be sure to dry that area with a proper towel after, as a typical hand towel harbors bacteria. Post this, rinse your area with warm water. Do not pick at the area as it can cause bleeding or irritation. It is also important to avoid soaking your belly button piercing in anything but a sterile saline solution.

**HOW TO CHANGE OUT A BELLY BUTTON PIERCING?**

You can effectively change out a navel piercing yourself, but just make sure that your piercing gets fully healed. As mentioned earlier, it takes around 6-8 months of the typical healing period, so make sure that you wait for a full year-long before switching it out.

**JEWELRY USED FOR BELLY BUTTON PIERCING:**

When it comes to navel piercing, curved barbell jewelry is used to give you a sharper look. It slightly curves outward to pop against your belly button and fits better with the natural shape of your body. It has a bead on each end, with one of them being adjustable/ removable if you want.

**JEWELRY MATERIAL USED FOR NAVEL PIERCING:**

- **IMPLANT-GRADE STAINLESS STEEL:** This is one of the safest types of metal used for navel piercing because of its low irritant or allergic sensitizing potential.
- **TITANIUM:** If you want to avoid the negative effects of surgical steel, switch to medium-grade titanium. It is similar to surgical steel, a bit lighter, and without a nickel in it.
- **GOLD:** If you look to keep that shine on, then go for gold piercing.
