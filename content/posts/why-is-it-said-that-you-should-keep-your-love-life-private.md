Jessy

Hi Jessy!

Yes, I am doing good beautiful. How about you? Well, people keep their salaries private.

They keep their investments private. They keep their secrets private. They keep their sex life private. They keep their love life private.

So, can you find the similarity? Yes! All the things we are obsessed with and the things we want are only ours, we keep them private. The fewer people you share this with the less drama you deal with.

What would you earn by making it public? Enviousness or [jealousy](https://www.verywellmind.com/overcome-jealousy-in-your-marriage-2303979#:~:text=Eventually%2C%20jealousy%20can%20lead%20to,also%20result%20in%20physical%20symptoms.)? And Jessy, this statement does not mean you should keep your love life private. It just means that until you or your partner are married, there is no point in sharing your story with the outside world.

Some vultures cannot see you happy. So, just think about it!

Have a great day!

Love,

Kiara!
