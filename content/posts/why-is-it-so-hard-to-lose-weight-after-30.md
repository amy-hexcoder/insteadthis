Hi Misha!

Thank you for much for this huge compliment. Well, losing weight in your 30s can be challenging because:

1. Metabolism slowing degrades: As we age, our metabolism keeps on slowing down naturally. This makes it harder to lose weight and burn calories.

2. There are hormonal changes: Hormonal changes in women, can make weight loss slightly difficult in women.

3. Less muscle mass can lower [metabolism](https://www.healthifyme.com/blog/how-to-increase-metabolism/): As we age, we naturally lose muscle mass. This lowers our metabolism and still gain weight.

4. Poor quality of sleep: Poor sleep quality is common in your 30s and it leads to hormonal imbalances which makes losing your weight slightly difficult.

5. Stress: This is common after 30s and can lead to weight gain.

6. No physical activity: Being physically less active, can lead to weight gain.

Yes, these are big challenges. But, it is still possible to lose weight in your 30s. So, maintain a healthy lifestyle and engage in regular physical activity, manage stress, and stay consistent.
