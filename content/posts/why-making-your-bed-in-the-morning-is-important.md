QUESTION

Hi,

Is there a reason why it is important to make your bed every morning?

Love,

Sia!

ANSWER

Hi Sia,

There is nothing more important than making your bed for the day in the morning. This simple act can have a significant impact on your daily life. Even though it may seem mundane, making your bed can influence your mood for the rest of the day. Making your bed in the morning has a few benefits:

1. Productivity: Getting your bed made can take you a long way to start your day positively. It establishes a sense of order and discipline, setting the stage for a productive day. It creates momentum that can carry over to other tasks throughout the day when you accomplish this task at the beginning of your day.

2. Mental Clarity: A clean and organized space can improve your mental health. By making your bed, you create a clean, inviting environment that can help you relax and reduce stress. Making your bed neatly at the end of the day can also help you relax and feel calm.

3. Habit Formation: A simple habit such as making your bed can lead to a variety of healthy habits. You cultivate a mindset of order and cleanliness by starting your day with this small act of discipline. You can apply this to other aspects of your life, such as keeping your living space clean, staying organized, and being punctual.

![](https://d12mivgeuoigbq.cloudfront.net/magento-media/blog/2018/June2018_GenericMattressB.jpg?w=700)

4. Sense of Accomplishment: Even making your bed can make you feel accomplished and proud. A little bit of self-care, pride, and responsibility goes a long way toward creating a pleasant living environment. It can boost your self-esteem and motivate you to tackle other tasks throughout the day.

5. Improved Sleep: Making your bed in the morning can actually help you sleep better at night. It's easier to sleep when your sheets are straightened and your pillows are fluffed. Relaxing in a well-made bed can help you unwind and prepare for a restful night's sleep.

I hope you now understand why making your bed has a profound impact on your overall health. Make your bed every morning and benefit from the benefits it brings.

Love,

Amanda!
