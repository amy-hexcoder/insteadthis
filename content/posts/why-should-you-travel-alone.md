Hi John!

Yes, I am doing great. What about you John? Surely, let's meet soon. Well, now that you have mentioned traveling, so here are the reasons why you should travel alone:

1. The sheer freedom to explore more: Travelling alone can be an incredibly liberating experience. So, you have the experience of doing what you want to do.

2. Planning your perfect trip: Half the fun of [traveling](https://heritagehotelsofeurope.com/why-travel-on-your-own-benefits-of-travelling-solo/) is in the planning process. There is no compromise, no tensions, and no worries.

3. Making new friends is easy: When traveling, you are much more approachable. You will also be inclined to have more conversations with people you meet along the way.

4. You learn a new language: To make the most of your trip away, learning a different language can help you get to know the places you are visiting. Traveling alone means you interact with the local people and pick up some language skills.

5. Travelling alone increases your self-confidence:  Travelling alone increases your self-confidence and it helps you become more confident, assertive, and decisive.

I know, it can be somewhat daunting how to start planning a solo trip. But trust me, when you travel alone you discover a lot about yourself. You can simply understand yourself much better and lead a much-happening life.

I hope, this helps.
