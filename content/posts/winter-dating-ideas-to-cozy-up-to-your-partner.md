Winter is coming, and we cannot wait for this magical season to arrive. There is so much love to share in this season. Yes, the days might be shorter, and the weather can be unforgiving, but we are ready to believe that it is the most amazing time of the year. It is because we are in the mood to snuggle up to our better half.

So, if you are thinking to go on a non-traditional date this cold season, then read this guide. It may be tempting to avoid the hassle of leaving your home, but it is worth it. Or else just stay at your home and keep cuddling, sounds romantic, right? So, check out these amazing romantic winter ideas for extra chill and fun.

![](/images/uploads/2022/10/w6-1024x683.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**1. Making a snowman together:**

It is just like building a sandcastle. We know that making a snowman, might sound a bit juvenile, but is a romantic activity. Doing something together and admiring something is fun.

**2. Going on a hiking date:**

Even if you do not like going outdoors, going on a winter hike with your pater could change your mind. After all, an amazing view with the person in love is always memorable, isn't it? You can also plan for a picnic.

**3. Sipping hot chocolate:**

Whether you love going in the kitchen or not, this is an amazing dating idea. You can make hot chocolate from the scratch and cherish having [good conversations](https://www.relationshipsnsw.org.au/how-to-have-better-conversations-with-your-partner/#:~:text=Good%20conversations%20are%20critical%20to,a%20skill%20and%20takes%20practice.) with your partner on a cold day.

**4. Going ice skating:**

Ice skating is indeed like bowling. So, you can bond over this fun activity and help each other from falling. Heading to a frozen lake will make you have a blast together.

![](/images/uploads/2022/10/w1-1024x1024.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**5. Heading to a theatre:**

Sometimes we need a reason to get dressed up to break that monotonous winter dressing. So, if you are not in the good mood to go out for a dinner, just make sure that you head out to a theatre and enjoy the cultural extravaganza.

**6. Watching a movie together:**

Yes, we know that dinner and a movie may not be an original idea, but there is always a reason why it makes the romantic dating idea. Also, winters happen to be awards season, so you can choose to indulge in a cerebral drama. If the weather is not inspiring you to go outside, watch a movie at home and cuddle on the couch in the soft glow of the television screen.

**7. Indoor skydiving is awesome:**

Fear of height should not stop you and your better half from trying this sport. So, if you have always wanted to try this activity, but jumping out of a plane sucks, then float in the sky with the help of a stimulator. It is an awesome activity you can do together.

**8. Playing board games:**

Snow days are a great excuse to stay in your pajamas and play board games. Try chess because this is an excellent option that is designed for you two. It lets you think competitively.

![](/images/uploads/2022/10/w5-1024x683.jpg)
***Credits@GoogleImages***

**9. A picnic date is amazing:**

Yes, we know that a traditional picnic is enjoyed outside, but winter is not the season to eat sandwiches and fruit on the front lawn. Though hanging in the living room lacks the invigorating sunshine, that is ok. At least you get a chance to spend some quality together with your love. So, spread out on a throw basket and nosh all your favorite items. Also, do not forget to pack your favorite bottle of wine of Champagne.

**10. Doing yoga:**

Nothing gets your blood flowing as yoga does. So, if anyone of you knows asanas, then try interlinking couple poses to know your partner's body.

**11. Visiting the holiday lights:**

Every town has a street that is decked out in holiday lights. So, why not take a scenic drive with your soulmate and admire all the hard work people do?

**12. Enjoying a home spa:**

There is no need to head out and have a tranquil spa experience. You do not need to be fancy. So, just invest in some [aromatherapy](https://revolutionforlove.com/aromatherapy-essential-oil-love-sex-intimacy-romance-couples-intimate-relationship/) and trade massages with your partner for a particularly romantic evening.

**13. Cooking breakfast together:**

Breakfast is an important morning meal. So, why not make it twice? Whether you like sweet or savory foods, try making a delicious meal together after the sun goes down. It is a perfect way to spend some time together.
