Cozy vibes, Christmas parties, celebrations, fun on the slopes, festivals and cheer in the air: Winter has so much to offer. It is the only season that offers many highlights alongside chilly vibes in everyday life. For hair, however, the cold is tough. So, it is important that your hair survives the constant back and forth between cold and hot air. Hence, we are here to reveal how to take care of your hair during winter and keep it shiny.

**Tip 1: Avoid frequent washing of your dry hair**

To avoid drier hair in winter, avoid frequent washing.

Natural oils provide a protective layer between your hair and [scalp](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/scalp-buildup). Bridge the gap between washes and looking greasy. Also, dry shampoo delivers instant volume. Also, it is mandatory not to leave your home with wet hair in winter.

**Tip 2: Blow dry your hair gently in winter**

Using a hair dryer in winter? Not at all a great idea. The heat from your dryer is bad for your hair. So, apply a heat protection product and blow dry your hair in a low heat setting.

**Tip 3: Wearing a heat**

We always love to go out on winter weekends. But exposure to rain, snow, and cold gives real stress to your hair. Hence, you should always wear a hat to minimize exposure to the cold air. Also, there are so many stylish options to protect your hair. So, go for it!

**Tip 4: Moisturizing your dry hair daily**

Cold weather and dry central heating do not impact your skin, but it affects your hair. So, always use a moisture-restoring shampoo and a hair conditioner during winter. You would not be able to completely avoid winter dryness, but moisturizing your hair with a mask helps in protecting your hair for a long. This should be included in your daily hair-care routine.

**Tip 5: Treating your dry hair with hair oil in winters**

Hair oil is always a blessing for stressed winter hair. It helps dry hair to regenerate and gives your hair shine and freshness. [Oil](https://www.healthline.com/health/hair-oiling-benefits-oil-and-how-to) can also be applied to your wet hair after you rinse out your shampoo or after blow drying. The ends of your hair will benefit from this.

**Tip 6: Banishing flyaway hair on cold nights**

Hair can sometimes get a static change from sweaters, hats, and scarves. So, you can counteract this effect by using some heat protection spray while styling, or moisturizing. Also, to keep your hair smooth, it is mandatory to use anti-frizz shampoos.

**Frequently asked questions (FAQs):**

**1. Why does your hair needs special attention in winter?**

Winter is the harsh season on your hair. So, the cold temperature and lack of moisture in the air cause your hair to become dry and dull. Also, your hair finds it difficult to retain moisture and it becomes brittle and prone to damage. The woolen clothes you wear also rub against the hair and lead to static friction. So, be informed about what extra care you have to give your hair by following a strict hair care regime.

**2. What are the problems your hair is likely to face in winter?**

**Dry scalp:** The cold winter temperatures and air cause your scalp to become dry and itchy. It leads to the formation of small white flakes. This scalp can cause split ends and dry hair. Due to this, your hair growth becomes minimum and is prone to breakage.

Since the scalp is under-nourished, so you need to concentrate on providing it will all the healthy nutrients. So, use a good conditioner, shampoo, and oil to keep your hair healthy. Infusing your hair oil with the essential oils of lavender, rosemary and thyme will not only invigorate your scalp but will make you relax. So, be sure to message your hair with this oil for at least 10 minutes as this also helps in stimulating blood circulation.

**3. Why does your hair results in less hair growth?**

During winter, you should always reduce your hair washes. But if you sleep with dirt in your hair, it can result in clogging your pores. These clogged pores again lead to many hair problems and reduced growth.

So, do not use too much shampoo as it might result in drying out your hair even more. Massage your hair gently with shampoo, and never scratch your hair with your nails.

Winters are the best season to go out and shine. But, simultaneously you should also take care of your hair and probably find the best alternative to make your hair healthy.
