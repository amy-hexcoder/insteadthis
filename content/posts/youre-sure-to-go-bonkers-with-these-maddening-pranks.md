There's no denying that Halloween is downright scary, but it can also be quite humorous! While you're putting together your [Halloween costumes](/stories/most-bizarre-outfits-you-shouldnt-miss-this-halloween), plan some pranks that will tickle your funny bone.

I'm a nervous wreck. Even though I am easily scared, I enjoy pranks, especially horror-themed Halloween ones.
While the Coronavirus outbreak has thrown a wrench in our holiday plans, we can still pull off a trick. Of course, "playing a prank" does not mean telling someone they are pregnant or getting divorced and then saying, "Damn, you got me now!".

'Real' [pranks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a-8WCFtPhBU) should scare people for a little while before they can enjoy them. So, be careful when planning your Halloween pranks this year. Don't forget to be funny as well as scary!

You're sure to hear lots of screaming and jumping when you pull out these Halloween pranks, and luckily, most of them aren't that complicated. Get spooked!

## Writing on Mirror

On a fogged-up mirror, haven't we all drawn a smiley face? It'll take a bit of trickery to scare them on Halloween. Make an incriminating message on the bathroom mirror with a bit of soap before your significant other, roommate, or sibling pops in. Make your message less readable by retracing your words with a piece of tissue. It will become visible as it becomes fogged up. Get ready to hear the panicked yelp.

## Fake Handshake

To play this prank, you can either DIY the [fake hand](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=fake+hand&rh=n%3A165793011%2Cn%3A166034011) or buy one. Make sure your sleeves completely cover the open part of the made-up hand. That is the key to the prank. To score extra punk points, tell your prankee to grab some milk from the fridge. They'll get severe chills when they do. The whole thing will be a bit creepy for a minute or so.

## Box of Snake - A Prank Gift

OMGosh! It's a snake inside. Run! Hurry!
It is a great way to prank someone because everyone is afraid of snakes unless they have had a pet snake, which is very unlikely. For the plan to work, tie the fake snake around the box's lid. It will fool them into thinking the snake is real. Make sure it's glued or taped with good quality; otherwise, the snake's weight might tear it off. Otherwise, it won't look realistic enough to pull off the prank.

Another DIY! In the night, tell your friend about the strange thing you saw. After seeing it, they might not want to return for a while, at least not at night. It would look fantastic, too!

## Let the Bed Bugs Bite

After a night of Halloween celebrations, the only wish is to jump into bed and warm up. Just imagine how terrible it would be if your loved ones pulled back the covers, only to find a swarm of bugs in residence. Bugs are fake, of course, but people will still freak out.

## Apple Anyone?

Rather than getting a mouthful of delicious Halloween candy, unwitting partygoers will get a bite of onion-flavored yuck. When you make the onions, make sure you include apple slices too to avoid swaying allergy sufferers. Do not punch them in the mouth; otherwise, it would be excruciating.

## Glitches in Computers

It's no secret that millennials are scared of faulty electronics, so why not scare your party-goers even more with a fake computer malfunction? How about some weird matrix effects or a "Blue Screen Of Death"? Guests can be immediately thrown into a tailspin, believing that their electronics have failed. Once you've found the perfect glitch to send, just sit back and watch the chaos unfold.

Imagine how your roommates would react if there were blood in the shower instead of water. They will go numb.
To pull off this prank, you will need [Kool-Aid](https://www.instructables.com/Kool-aid-shower-prank/). Also, watch your roommate's hilarious reactions when they come out of the bathroom screaming.

## Toilet Pants Prank

How about pranking your coworkers? This prank is sure to frustrate your friends more than anything. When they discovered one of their colleagues had been using the office restroom for hours, they would feel tormented.
It's a simple prank where you have to place a pair of pants or jeans on the toilet seat. As you sit on the toilet seat, adjust it so it appears that real legs are on the floor.
